# Bears / Ayula — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Ayula, Queen Among Bears | Colors: Green (Mono-G)

## Strategy Overview
Mono-green Bear tribal deck built around Ayula's triggered ability. Every Bear ETB gives a choice: +2 counters on a Bear OR Ayula fights a non-Bear creature. The deck wants to deploy Ayula early, chain bear plays for counter accumulation, and use the fight mode as removal for key threats.

## Key Synergies
- **Ayula + any Bear**: Each Bear ETB is either +2 counters (growing threats) or a fight-based removal spell.
- **Kami of Whispered Hopes + Ayula counters**: Kami adds +1 to any counter placement (2 becomes 3). Also taps for mana equal to its power — grows into a massive mana dork if counters land on it.
- **Roaming Throne (Bear type) + Ayula**: Throne IS the chosen type, so it triggers Ayula on ETB AND doubles all future Ayula triggers. One bear = two triggers.
- **Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma**: 4/3 Bear that reduces power 4+ creature costs by {2} and gives trample + pump on attack. Enables Roaming Throne for {2}.
- **Rishkar's Expertise**: Draw cards equal to greatest power (Ayula with counters draws a lot) + free cast MV 5 or less.

## Key Cards
- **Ayula** (2/2, {1}{G}): Engine commander — every bear is either a pump or removal
- **Kami of Whispered Hopes** ({2}{G}): Counter amplifier + scaling mana dork
- **Roaming Throne** ({4}): Trigger doubler — transforms single bears into double triggers
- **Goreclaw** ({3}{G}): Cost reducer + attack pump/trample for power 4+
- **Dragon-Scarred Bear** ({2}{G}, 3/2): Bear with formidable regenerate
- **Werebear** ({1}{G}): Bear mana dork, threshold makes it 4/4
- **Rishkar's Expertise** ({4}{G}{G}): Card draw + free cast based on creature power

## Game History

### Game 38 (piloted by Claude): Bears/Ayula vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T13 via lethal combat damage (Cicada 22/22 trample + Construct 7/7, concession from 22 life).
- T4: Ayula deployed. T6: Werebear → Ayula trigger → 2 counters on Ayula (4/4). Attacked for 4 commander damage.
- T8: Kami + Balduvian Bears → 3 counters on Ayula (7/7, Kami amplified). Zuko's Exile removed Ayula at instant speed mid-combat. Lost all counters.
- T10: Goreclaw deployed. Attacked with Balduvian Bears — opponent flashed in Zhulodok via Cicada, pumping Cicada to 8/8. Bears died blocking.
- T11: Platinum Angel cascaded in with Ornithopter + Palladium Myr. Cicada 14/14 trample + Construct 7/7 dealt 21 damage (blocked Construct with Goreclaw, took 14).
- T12: Ayula recast + Roaming Throne (Bear type). Throne triggered Ayula twice → 3 counters on Ayula (5/5) + fight Platinum Angel (5/5 vs 4/4, Ayula survives). Platinum Angel destroyed.
- T13: Ulamog cast trigger destroyed Ayula again. Double cascade into Breaker of Armies + Expedition Map. Cicada 22/22 trample = lethal.
- **Rating:** 3 — Eldrazi engine was humming, Bears couldn't keep up once cascades started.
- **Key mistake:** Missed Roaming Throne's Ayula trigger initially (it IS a Bear). Would have missed the Platinum Angel kill without the user's prompt.
- **Key lesson:** Ayula's counter accumulation resets every time she's removed. Against decks with exile/destroy, she's fragile. Need to protect her or have redundant threats.
- **Key lesson:** Mono-G Bears has almost no interaction for artifacts/enchantments or the cascade engine. Must win before Zhulodok comes online.
- **Deck suggestion:** Needs protection spells (Heroic Intervention, Tyvar's Stand, Tamiyo's Safekeeping) to keep Ayula alive through removal. Also consider Kenrith's Transformation or Beast Within for problematic permanents like Platinum Angel.

### Game 49 (piloted by Claude): Bears/Ayula vs. Mill-Steal/Wrexial (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T24 via concession (Stormtide Leviathan lock + unblockable Wrexial + mill). Final life: Human 32, Claude 23.
- T7: Ayula deployed. T9: Stasis Field locked Ayula (0/2 defender, no abilities). Snakeskin Veil protection countered by Negate.
- T9: Beast Within freed Ayula. Ashcoat Bear trigger → +2 counters (4/4). Attacked for 4 CDMG.
- T11: Panharmonicon deployed. T13: Ayula recast attempt (tax +{2}) countered by Cancel. Professor of Zoomancy (Bear Druid) deployed instead with doubled ETB — 2 Pest tokens + 6 scries.
- T15: Ayula third cast attempt (tax +{4}) countered by Saw It Coming (foretold). Severe flooding — drew 6 lands in 8 draws.
- T19: Ayula fourth cast (tax +{6}) resolved but immediately chumped Wrexial to prevent GY steal trigger.
- T22: Stormtide Leviathan deployed. All lands became Islands → Wrexial permanently unblockable, bears can't attack. Magic Mirror at 2 knowledge counters drawing 2+/turn.
- T23: Narset's Reversal on own kicked Maddening Cacophony — milled 27 cards, kept Cacophony in hand. Boseiju and Chomping Changeling (only outs) both milled into GY.
- T24: Bloatfly Swarm (5/5 flying) + Wrexial + Stormtide = 18 unblockable. Concession.
- **Rating:** 2 — Hard-fought. Mill deck had no ramp until Thran Dynamo T14, forced to choose between developing and holding counters. Lock didn't come online until T22.
- **Key lesson:** Ayula is too fragile as the sole engine against counter-heavy decks. Four cast attempts, only resolved twice, and each removal resets all counters. Commander tax spiraled to {8}.
- **Key lesson:** Stormtide Leviathan is an unbeatable lock for mono-G bears — no flying, no islandwalk, no way to attack. Only answers are fight triggers (need 8+ power Ayula) or artifact/enchantment removal on Stormtide... but Stormtide is a creature. Bears has almost no creature removal besides Ayula fight.
- **Key lesson:** Don't attack Ayula into Wrexial (5/8). T13 attack lost Ayula + Ashcoat Bear for zero value. Should have held back.
- **Deck suggestion:** Needs more redundant threats that don't depend on Ayula. Surrak and Goreclaw, Ruxa provide alternate win conditions. Also consider Kenrith's Transformation to answer Stormtide/Wrexial (turns them into 3/3 Elk, no abilities).

### Game 53 (piloted by Claude): Bears/Ayula vs. Meteor Apes/Roxanne (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 via commander damage (21 from Ayula). Final life: Claude 7, Human 34. Dominance 2.
- Severe flood early (drew 6 forests in first 10 cards). Ozolith T2, Ayula T4.
- T7: Gaea's Gift on Ayula (3/3 permanently) to block Hajar — killed Hajar, saved 3 life, and critically made Ayula survive Meteorite 2-damage pings.
- T9: Roxanne landed. Meteorite pinged face (not Ayula — 3 toughness survived).
- T10: Rishkar's Expertise drew 2 off Ayula 3/3. Drew Titania's Command + Evolution Charm.
- T11: Kogla ETB fought and killed Ayula (7/6 vs 3/3). Counter moved to Ozolith.
- T12: Recast Ayula ({3}{G} tax). Ozolith returned the counter (3/3).
- T13: Opponent swung with Kogla + Roxanne + Kird Ape (13 damage, 20→9). Earth aftermath X=2 from GY — Evolution Charm gave Ayula flying to dodge it. Roxanne and Kogla each took 2.
- T14: Roaming Throne (Bear) → doubled Ayula triggers: +2 pump + fight Roxanne (5/5 kills 4/3). Werebear → two more triggers: +2, +2. Ayula to 9/9. Held back to block.
- T15: Roxanne recast, Meteorite killed Werebear. Kogla and Yidaro (7/7 trample haste) deployed. Ward {2} on Roaming Throne denied Kogla's attack trigger (opponent tapped out, couldn't pay). Blocked K&Y with Ayula (9/9), Kogla with Throne. K&Y died, Throne died, took 2 from Spirit Guide. Life: 7.

- **Key lesson:** Gaea's Gift's permanent +1/+1 counter is huge — surviving Meteorite pings (3 toughness > 2 damage) changed the entire game trajectory.
- **Key lesson:** Evolution Charm's flying mode dodges Earth aftermath ("each creature without flying"). Situational but game-saving.
- **Key lesson:** Ward on Roaming Throne was the game-deciding moment. Opponent couldn't pay {2} while tapped out, preserving a blocker that prevented lethal.
- **Key lesson:** When opponent's creatures are tapped from attacking, fight the UNTAPPED blockers to clear the way for a lethal swing. Don't waste fight triggers on tapped creatures that can't block.
- **Key lesson:** Grizzly Fate with threshold (4 bears) is a massive Ayula turn — 4 triggers = +8 counters or mix of pump/fight.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Mill-Steal/Wrexial:** Wrexial has islandwalk/swampwalk — irrelevant against mono-G unless Stormtide makes all lands Islands. THAT is the real threat. Stormtide + Wrexial = permanent unblockable lock + bears can't attack. Must destroy Stormtide before it sticks (Beast Within, fight trigger if Ayula is big enough). Mill fuels Wrexial's GY steal — Beast Within and Snakeskin Veil in GY get weaponized against you. Deploy Ayula early before counter mana is online. Sapphire Medallion makes all counters cheaper — destroy it if possible. Boseiju channels can't hit creatures (doesn't answer Stormtide). Chomping Changeling ETB only hits artifacts/enchantments. Need creature-based removal or fight triggers for the Leviathan.
- **vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang:** Ayula's fight trigger hard-counters Chatterfang (3 toughness dies to 4+ power Ayula). Keep stacking counters — each bear ETB is either more pump or creature removal. Changeling bears with utility ETBs (Chomping Changeling) are all-stars. Chatterfang's forestwalk is unblockable but only 3 damage per swing — race favors bears if Ayula gets 2+ counter triggers before Chatterfang attacks.
- **vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok:** Must pressure aggressively before T10. Once Zhulodok cascades, the board state snowballs beyond what bears can handle. Ayula's fight trigger can remove key pieces (killed Platinum Angel) but requires setup. Cicada flash + pump makes even the combat step dangerous. Removal for Cicada is high priority — it enables flash AND becomes the primary damage threat.
- **vs. Meteor Apes/Roxanne (1-0):** Roxanne's Meteorite pings (2 damage) threaten Ayula at base 2/2 — get a +1/+1 counter on Ayula ASAP (Gaea's Gift, first bear trigger) so she survives pings. Kogla ETB fight kills Ayula cleanly (7 vs 2-3) — have protection or accept the recast tax. Kogla's attack trigger destroys artifacts (Ozolith, Signet) — sequence Ozolith counter transfers before it dies. Earth aftermath ({X}{R}{R} from GY) hits all non-flyers — Evolution Charm's flying mode dodges it. Roxanne + Meteorites snowball mana fast (doubling). Kill Roxanne with fight triggers to shut down the engine. Ward on Roaming Throne matters — opponent often taps out for big plays and can't pay. When opponent's board is tapped from attacking, fight the untapped blockers, not the tapped threats.
