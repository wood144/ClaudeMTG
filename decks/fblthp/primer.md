# Fblthp, the Lost — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
Commander: Fblthp, the Lost | Colors: Blue (Mono-U)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
**Combo shell — only ~6 core cards:**
- **3 library removers:** Enter the Infinite, Leveler, Mirror of Fate
- **3 empty-library wins:** Thassa's Oracle, Laboratory Maniac, Jace Wielder of Mysteries
- **Backup win:** Triskaidekaphile (exactly 13 cards in hand)
- All three win conditions can be in hand simultaneously after Enter the Infinite. Answering one isn't enough.

**Mana engine:**
- Extraplanar Lens (exile Island) + Gauntlet of Power (blue) = each Island produces {U}{U}{U}. 4 Islands = 12 mana.
- **Proper Fblthp play** is to cast Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir or Tidal Barracuda BEFORE the combo — removes opponent's ability to interact at instant speed entirely.

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Need planeswalker removal** (Beast Within, Anguished Unmaking, Generous Gift) not just creature/enchantment removal. Path + Mortify can stop Oracle but not Jace.
- **Destroy mana doublers early.** Extraplanar Lens + Gauntlet of Power in mono-color = explosive mana. Need artifact removal (Hull Breach, Krosan Grip, Nature's Claim).
- **Hullbreaker Horror** is a root-cause threat — don't counter triggers, exile/destroy Horror itself.

## Game History

### Game 1 (opponent): Fblthp vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Claude)
**Result:** Fblthp wins via Thassa's Oracle + Enter the Infinite.
- Hullbreaker Horror resolved and could not be answered with partial responses (countering triggers).

### Game 6 (opponent): Fblthp vs. Food and Fellowship (Claude)
**Result:** Fblthp wins T11. Enter the Infinite → Jace +1 for the win.
- Oracle was stopped (Path on Fblthp + Mortify on Oracle), but Jace was the backup win with 12+ mana available.

## Matchup Notes
- This deck has been faced twice and won both times. Critical weakness: relies on resolving Enter the Infinite (12 mana sorcery). Counterspells or hand disruption before T9-10 are the best answers.
