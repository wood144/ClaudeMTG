# Fblthp, the Lost — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
Commander: Fblthp, the Lost | Colors: Blue (Mono-U)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
**Combo shell — only ~6 core cards:**
- **3 library removers:** Enter the Infinite, Leveler, Mirror of Fate
- **3 empty-library wins:** Thassa's Oracle, Laboratory Maniac, Jace Wielder of Mysteries
- **Backup win:** Triskaidekaphile (exactly 13 cards in hand)
- All three win conditions can be in hand simultaneously after Enter the Infinite. Answering one isn't enough.

**Mana engine:**
- Extraplanar Lens (exile Island) + Gauntlet of Power (blue) = each Island produces {U}{U}{U}. 4 Islands = 12 mana.
- **Proper Fblthp play** is to cast Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir or Tidal Barracuda BEFORE the combo — removes opponent's ability to interact at instant speed entirely.

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Need planeswalker removal** (Beast Within, Anguished Unmaking, Generous Gift) not just creature/enchantment removal. Path + Mortify can stop Oracle but not Jace.
- **Destroy mana doublers early.** Extraplanar Lens + Gauntlet of Power in mono-color = explosive mana. Need artifact removal (Hull Breach, Krosan Grip, Nature's Claim).
- **Hullbreaker Horror** is a root-cause threat — don't counter triggers, exile/destroy Horror itself.

## Game History

### Game 1 (opponent): Fblthp vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Claude)
**Result:** Fblthp wins via Thassa's Oracle + Enter the Infinite.
- Hullbreaker Horror resolved and could not be answered with partial responses (countering triggers).

### Game 6 (opponent): Fblthp vs. Food and Fellowship (Claude)
**Result:** Fblthp wins T11. Enter the Infinite → Jace +1 for the win.
- Oracle was stopped (Path on Fblthp + Mortify on Oracle), but Jace was the backup win with 12+ mana available.

### Game 23 (piloted): Fblthp (Claude) vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T23 via combat damage. Claude at 15 life, Human at 65.

**Key plays:**
- T1: Leyline of Anticipation free from opening hand — but hand had ZERO lands. Should have been an auto-mulligan.
- Didn't draw first Island until T5. Second Island T7. Third Island T9. Never recovered from mana deficit.
- T19: Finally cast Apprentice Wizard at 3 mana — immediately killed by Deathspore Thallid saccing a Saproling.
- T21: Bounced Mycoloth (14/14, 10 counters) with Unsummon, but opponent just replayed it immediately devouring 5 creatures.
- Ghave + Winding Constrictor + Zulaport Cutthroat drain engine ground Claude from 40 to 15 over the game.

**Key lessons:**
- **MULLIGAN ZERO-LAND HANDS.** No matter how exciting other cards look. A free Leyline means nothing without mana to cast spells.
- **NEVER discard combo pieces.** Enter the Infinite and Mirror of Fate were discarded because "I can't cast 12 mana." The entire deck is built to GET to 12 mana. Discarding the win condition because you're behind is throwing away the game plan. Discard interaction or redundant pieces, never combo enablers.
- **Use the decklist. Run odds.** Check what ramp/answers exist in the 90+ card library. Calculate draw probabilities to find lands or combo pieces. Plan toward the win condition, not just the current turn.
- **Aura Shards completely locks out artifacts/enchantments.** With Ghave making Saprolings at will, every creature ETB destroys something. Extraplanar Lens, Insight Engine — all unplayable.
- **Deathspore Thallid kills mana dorks and small creatures for free** by saccing Saprolings. Don't deploy vulnerable creatures without protection.
- **Winding Constrictor + Ghave sac loop = net +1 counter per cycle.** Each sac adds 2 counters (Constrictor), remove 1 to make replacement Saproling. Ghave grows while draining with Zulaport.
- **Multiple hidden information violations T1.** Named cards from hand, described holdup plans.
- **USE YOUR COMMANDER TO DIG.** Fblthp costs {1}{U} and draws a card on ETB. He's not the win condition — he's a cantrip on legs. When mana-screwed, cast Fblthp to draw into lands/ramp. He chump blocks, and if he dies he goes to command zone for recast. If targeted by a spell, shuffles into library. There is never a reason to sit on 2 open mana doing nothing when your commander is available.
- **Fblthp + any bounce spell = draw + shuffle.** Cast Fblthp (draw 1), then target him with any bounce effect (Unsummon, Snap, Unsubstantiate, etc.). Fblthp sees the targeting → shuffles into library, reshuffling the deck. One piece is always in the command zone, and the deck runs many bounce effects. Use this to break up known-bad top cards after Ponder/scry.

## Matchup Notes
- This deck has been faced twice as opponent (won both) and once as pilot (lost). Critical weakness: relies on resolving Enter the Infinite (12 mana sorcery). Counterspells or hand disruption before T9-10 are the best answers.
- **vs. Fun Guys/Ghave:** Aura Shards shuts down the artifact/enchantment mana engine entirely. Need to counter or bounce Aura Shards before deploying Extraplanar Lens/Gauntlet of Power. Bounce spells are temporary — Ghave decks have enough mana to replay threats immediately.
