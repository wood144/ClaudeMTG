# Dicebots / Mr. House — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Mr. House, President and CEO | Colors: Red/White/Black (Mardu)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Mr. House** creates Robot tokens and triggers pinger effects.
- **Agility Bobblehead + Saddle + Sword of Hours** is a must-answer package — unblockable + growing counters = fast clock.
- **Brazen Dwarf + Delina + Hedron Detonator** — pinger engine that deals damage on token creation and dice rolls.
- **Attrition** — sacrifice a creature to destroy target nonblack creature. Note: does NOT work on black creatures (relevant vs Ghave).

## How to Beat This Deck
- Board wipe (Damnation) before the pinger engine fires — once Brazen Dwarf + Hedron Detonator are online with Robot tokens, damage adds up fast.
- Answer Agility Bobblehead or Sword of Hours before counters accumulate.
- Attrition is strong but has the nonblack restriction — exploit this with black commanders/creatures.

## Game History

### Game 7 (opponent): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Claude)
**Result:** Claude (Ghave) wins T20 via infinite Zulaport Cutthroat drain.
- T10 Damnation cleared Mr. House + entire pinger package before it could fire.
- Attrition couldn't target Ghave (black). Opponent's removal plan fell apart.

### Game 13 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Dargons/Scion (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T15 via commander damage (21 exactly).
- Wyll killed T5 by Locthwain Scorn before dice engine started.
- Anim Pakal token engine was the best performer — generated 3 Gnomes and dealt consistent ground damage.
- Earth-Cult Elemental ETB rolled 19 → forced opponent to sac Scion of Draco. Great value.
- T13 Coiling Rebirth reanimated Ureni — Ureni attack trigger cheated in Rith.
- T15 lethal: Scion copied Earthquake Dragon (10/10 flying) for 10 CDMG (11→21).
- Mr. House never cast — game ended too fast.
- **Lesson:** Deck struggled without early dice engine. Need to protect Wyll or have backup dice rollers early. No flying blockers = Scion flies over freely.

### Game 41 (opponent): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Every Shrine Ever/Go-Shintai (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T7 (sides 13) via concession. Dominant snowball — Mr. House + Wyll + Ancient Copper Dragon.
- Sol Ring T1 into Arcane Signet = explosive start. Mr. House T3.
- Hull Breach removed Mr. House T4, but recast T5 at commander tax with ease.
- Wyll + Bobbleheads created a dice-rolling engine that scaled every trigger. Wyll grew to 8/8 by end.
- Ancient Copper Dragon nat 20 = 20 Treasures. Game-ending resource explosion.
- Inspiring Statuary + Treasure pile = cast anything. Earth-Cult Elemental forced a sac, Chaos Dragon added flying pressure.
- Agility Bobblehead making Wyll + Earth-Cult unblockable was the kill — 14 unavoidable damage vs 5 life.
- **Lesson:** Deck fires on all cylinders when Wyll survives. Ancient Copper Dragon is a must-answer threat — connecting once generates unrecoverable advantage.

### Game 59 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T9 (17 sides) via commander damage (Zinnia). Human life 20, Claude life 8. CDMG: 15 (lethal on final swing).
- Opening hand was 6 lands + Farideh's Fireball. Kept for guaranteed T3 Mr. House — correct but topdecked lands for 4 more turns.
- T6 threw away Ragavan by attacking into Zinnia (1/3 blocked and killed it). Lost a key Treasure generator for zero value.
- T10 Farideh's Fireball killed Zinnia + d20 triggered Mr. House for Robot + Treasure. Good turn.
- T13 Devilish Valet + Aether Channeler with offspring = 4 Alliance triggers, Valet hit 16/3 trample. 15-damage swing was the turning point.
- T16 The Deck of Many Things came online but too late — drew Strength Bobblehead (mana rock, not removal).
- **No flying defense.** Zinnia flew over every turn. Robots can't block flyers. Needed removal or a reach creature.
- **Lesson:** Don't attack valuable creatures (Ragavan) into obvious blocks. Hold them for repeated value. Against Zinnia, prioritize removing her or finding flyer blockers — ground Robots are irrelevant if she flies over for CDMG every turn.

### Game 64 (opponent): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T14 (14 sides) via lethal combat. Human life 39, Claude life -7. Dominance 3.
- Perfect curve: Ragavan T2 → Lightfoot Rogue T4 → Mr. House T6 → Chaos Dragon T8.
- Every Lightfoot attack and Chaos Dragon combat roll triggered Mr. House for Robots + Treasures. Exponential board growth.
- Revivify T9 rolled 17 to return Chaos Dragon after Spider Food removed it. Tempo blowout.
- Mr. House end-step activation (3d6: 1,4,5) added 2 more Robots. Board hit 6+ Robots by T14.
- Ygra T13 would have stabilized (all creatures become Food, ward = sac a creature to target) but came one turn too late. 20 power on board vs 10 life = lethal.
- **Lesson:** Deck fires when the dice engine assembles early. Lightfoot Rogue is a sleeper MVP — cheap, triggers Mr. House every attack, deathtouch makes blocking awkward.

## Matchup Notes
- Token/pinger strategy is vulnerable to well-timed board wipes. Don't wait for the engine to assemble — wipe proactively.
- **vs. Dargons/Scion:** No flying defense is a major problem. Scion copies 10/10 flyers for lethal commander damage. Need early pressure + removal for Scion before it snowballs. Reanimation (Coiling Rebirth, Virtue of Persistence) brings back dragons — exile-based removal preferred.
- **vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (1-0):** Token-for-token, Dicebots outpaces Squirrel Food. Mr. House generates Robots passively from every dice trigger while Chatterfang needs multiple pieces assembled. Ygra is the scary stabilizer — turns all Robots into Food and grows on trades. Kill Ygra on sight or win before T11. Revivify recovers key threats after removal. Deck has no board wipes to punish going wide.
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever/Go-Shintai:** Shrine deck is slow to set up. Aggro with unblockable Robots + Agility Bobblehead outraces the shrine engine. Ancient Copper Dragon + Wyll boosted rolls = Treasure avalanche. Only real out is Farewell — if shrine player sacs a land instead of a shrine to Earth-Cult Elemental, they don't have it.
- **vs. Zinnia Precon (0-1):** Zinnia flies over Robot army for consistent CDMG. Devilish Valet + offspring/token creatures = explosive Alliance doublings. Must kill Zinnia repeatedly or find flyer blockers. Jacked Rabbit + Valet is a kill combo (6 tokens entering = Valet at 64+ power trample). Prioritize removal for Zinnia and Devilish Valet. Deck has no natural flying defense.
