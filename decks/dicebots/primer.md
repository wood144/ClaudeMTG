# Dicebots / Mr. House — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
Commander: Mr. House, President and CEO | Colors: Red/White/Black (Mardu)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Mr. House** creates Robot tokens and triggers pinger effects.
- **Agility Bobblehead + Saddle + Sword of Hours** is a must-answer package — unblockable + growing counters = fast clock.
- **Brazen Dwarf + Delina + Hedron Detonator** — pinger engine that deals damage on token creation and dice rolls.
- **Attrition** — sacrifice a creature to destroy target nonblack creature. Note: does NOT work on black creatures (relevant vs Ghave).

## How to Beat This Deck
- Board wipe (Damnation) before the pinger engine fires — once Brazen Dwarf + Hedron Detonator are online with Robot tokens, damage adds up fast.
- Answer Agility Bobblehead or Sword of Hours before counters accumulate.
- Attrition is strong but has the nonblack restriction — exploit this with black commanders/creatures.

## Game History

### Game 7 (opponent): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Claude)
**Result:** Claude (Ghave) wins T20 via infinite Zulaport Cutthroat drain.
- T10 Damnation cleared Mr. House + entire pinger package before it could fire.
- Attrition couldn't target Ghave (black). Opponent's removal plan fell apart.

### Game 13 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Dargons/Scion (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T15 via commander damage (21 exactly).
- Wyll killed T5 by Locthwain Scorn before dice engine started.
- Anim Pakal token engine was the best performer — generated 3 Gnomes and dealt consistent ground damage.
- Earth-Cult Elemental ETB rolled 19 → forced opponent to sac Scion of Draco. Great value.
- T13 Coiling Rebirth reanimated Ureni — Ureni attack trigger cheated in Rith.
- T15 lethal: Scion copied Earthquake Dragon (10/10 flying) for 10 CDMG (11→21).
- Mr. House never cast — game ended too fast.
- **Lesson:** Deck struggled without early dice engine. Need to protect Wyll or have backup dice rollers early. No flying blockers = Scion flies over freely.

## Matchup Notes
- Token/pinger strategy is vulnerable to well-timed board wipes. Don't wait for the engine to assemble — wipe proactively.
- **vs. Dargons/Scion:** No flying defense is a major problem. Scion copies 10/10 flyers for lethal commander damage. Need early pressure + removal for Scion before it snowballs. Reanimation (Coiling Rebirth, Virtue of Persistence) brings back dragons — exile-based removal preferred.
