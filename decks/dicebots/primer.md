# Dicebots / Mr. House — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Mr. House, President and CEO | Colors: Red/White/Black (Mardu)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Mr. House is the engine:** dice rolls (d20 / 2d6) make Robot tokens + Treasures. Counter or kill Mr. House and the avalanche never assembles — there is no real backup spawner (Game 96).
- **High-roll lock — Wyll, Blade of Frontiers + Barbarian Class L1:** two "+1 die, ignore lowest" replacement effects stack → d20 ≈ 3-dice-take-highest (~95% ≥15). Every Mr. House activation and every removal-on-roll (Myrkul's Edict, Contraband Livestock, Earth-Cult Elemental, Channeler) hits max mode. All pieces 1-3 mana; this is the actual win engine vs non-counter decks (Game 99).
- **Pings, NOT combat, are the damage.** Ground Robots get fully blocked. The clock is creature/token-ETB pingers — Warleader's Call, Reckless Fireweaver, Impact Tremors, Kambal — amplified by **Ojer Axonil** (1 dmg → 4). Maximize ETBs; don't count on alpha connecting.
- **Ojer Axonil is the must-protect amplifier.** Without it, pings are 1 each and the clock breaks (Games 96, 114).
- **Ragavan** dashed early exiles opp's top-of-library removal — game-defining vs singleton RGW / removal-pile decks (Games 88, 99).
- **Library mining:** Wand of Wonder / Chaos Channeler exile-cast opp's instants/sorceries (their removal pile) and our own threats — strips their answers and deploys our gas ahead of curve (Games 88, 99).
- **Closers / saves:** Ancient Copper Dragon + Brass Dragon are flying closers once opp's answer pile is depleted (must-answer when they connect); Agility Bobblehead makes a beater unblockable; **Lae'zel's Acrobatics** phases the engine out under a board wipe (4 mana beats an 8-mana Avatar — Game 88).
- **Attrition** — sac a creature to destroy target *nonblack* creature. Does NOT hit black creatures (dead vs Ghave / black commanders).

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Fly over it — the deck has NO flying defense.** Robots can't block flyers and reach is rare. Air clocks (Bonehoard Dracosaur, Stormtide Leviathan) and flying commander-damage (Scion, Zinnia) win nearly unopposed (Games 13, 59, 96, 114). Its only outs are its own 7-drop flyers — race them down or kill them.
- **Board wipe before the pinger engine fires** (Damnation T10 won Game 7). Don't wait for it to fully assemble.
- **Counter or kill Mr. House** — no Mr. House on board, no Robot avalanche (Game 96).
- **Kill / neutralize Ojer Axonil** — without the amplifier, pings drop to 1 and the clock stalls.
- **No interaction vs combo/inevitability.** The deck races but can't stop Enter the Infinite → Omniscience, Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond, etc. Out-inevitable it (Games 115, 70).
- **Mill strips its single-copy removal pile** (Deadly Rollick, Attrition, Myrkul's Edict) — Game 96.
- **Mana-screw it / go fast** — a slow Dicebots hand with no Sol Ring / early ramp folds to a deck that simply curves out (Game 114).
- **Attrition can't touch black creatures** — black commanders dodge its main removal (Game 7).

### Game 115 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Fblthp (Human)
**Result:** Human (Fblthp) wins T14 via Enter the Infinite → Omniscience combo. Final life: Human 17, Claude 40. Dominance 2.

**Game flow:**
- Good aggressive curve-out: T3 Ragavan (hardcast), T5 Warleader's Call (anthem + ping-on-creature-ETB), T7 Mr. House, T9 Swarming Goblins (d20=14 → 2 Goblins + Robot + Treasure), T11 Bobblehead + Delina, T13 Havoc Jester. Every drop forced through while opp was tapped out — opp tapped out EVERY one of their early turns, so Mr. House + Warleader's Call landed counter-free.
- **Ragavan traded into Malcolm T5** — killed their card-selection/dig engine for a 1-drop. Correct.
- Warleader's Call did the work: pinged opp from 40 → 17 via creature/token ETBs + a couple of unblocked alpha swings (opp can fully block my ground board, so the **pings were ~all the real damage**).
- Opp ramped past me anyway: Sol Ring, Extraplanar Lens (Islands tap double), Apprentice Wizard, Throne of Eldraine, Arcane Signet, Mm'menon (3/4 flyer; artifacts tap for U to cast from non-hand zones), The Reality Chip (play/cast off top of library), Tezzeret Cruel Captain (untap artifact / tutor). By T12 they had ~20+ mana.
- **They DIDN'T need to combo until forced — they were DIGGING, not stalling.** Opp's hand was clogged with lands/ramp; the mana was already there, so the only productive line was deploying draw engines (Reality Chip, Sensei's Top, Mm'menon, Throne) to find the combo pieces. T12 they dug + bounced my Delina with Unsubstantiate. Once I got them to 17 and was threatening a 19-power T15 alpha, they had what they needed and comboed T14: 22 mana → Enter the Infinite from top of library (Reality Chip) = the ultimate dig (draw the whole deck) → Omniscience → all 3 win-cons in hand. Claude tapped out, zero interaction. No response.

**Key lessons (Dicebots pilot — me):**
- **This matchup is a pure race with a hard deadline (~T12-14 combo) and NO interaction in the Dicebots maindeck to stop Enter the Infinite / Omniscience / Thassa's Oracle.** I played the race about as well as the draw allowed (40→17) but a 17-life cushion + their inevitability beat me. To actually win this, I need to be at lethal a full turn earlier, OR draw artifact removal for Lens/Sol Ring to slow their mana.
- **Pings are the damage, not combat.** Fblthp fields cheap chump blockers (Fblthp 0/1, Apprentice 0/1) + Mm'menon 3/4 + flash Disruptor of Currents 3/3 — my ground alpha gets fully blocked. Maximize creature/token ETBs (Warleader's Call) and Mr. House robot rolls; don't count on combat connecting.
- **Mulligan/early game: prioritize the Treasure/Bobblehead engine to enable a BIG Mr. House dice turn sooner.** Without a Treasure base, Mr. House activations are 1 die (~0.5 robot) — too slow. I never assembled the explosive dice turn because I had no Treasure pile; Hoarding Ogre (treasures on attack) never got to swing.
- **Don't over-respect their tapped-out windows by slow-rolling — but DO keep forcing irreplaceable pieces (Warleader's Call) through them.** That part worked; losing Warleader's Call to a counter would have ended the race instantly.
- **Realistically need a faster kill or sideboard-style artifact hate (Hull Breach / Krosan-class) to beat Fblthp.** Racing from 40 isn't fast enough vs a 22-mana T14 combo. Look for the Delina-copy + Mr. House Treasure-flood burst a turn earlier; if it's not assembling by ~T11, the race is likely lost.

**Result:** Human wins T19 by Claude concession. Sides 19. Human life 38, Claude life 7. Dominance 3.

**Loss path:**

The whole early game was dead.

But it was far too late — Rampaging Brontodon attacks as a **17/17 trample** off ~10 lands (Itlimoc ramp), and a single chump leaves 13+ trampling. Conceded at 7 facing ~31 across trample + flying.

**Key lessons:**

- **The "dominant" Atla matchup is dominant only WITH the lock pieces (Wyll/Barbarian Class/Ragavan early, removal-on-roll).** This hand had none of them early and got mana screwed. Without Ragavan-T2/Channeler/early removal, Atla's deck just ramps into Itlimoc + fat dinos and runs you over. Mulligan harder for Sol Ring / 2-mana ramp + an early threat; a clunky 2-land 6 with no accelerant is a trap keep.
- **No flying defense, AGAIN.** Bonehoard (5/5 flyer) clocked me unanswered the entire game. Ancient Gold Dragon was my only out and it was perpetually 1 mana short on the turn it mattered (mana screw). Deck wants Sol Ring/Signet in the opener to reach the 7-drop flyer before the air clock buries it.
- **Ojer → Temple of Power on death is a silver lining (free land), but losing the amplifier guts the ping plan.** Without Ojer, Hedron/Fireweaver pings are 1 each — not a clock. Protect Ojer or don't lean the gameplan on it vs a deck with Contest of Claws / Ram Through / Savage Stomp (3+ ways to kill a 4-toughness body).

### Game 99 (Human): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T14 by Claude concession. Sides 14. Human life 40, Claude life 8. Dominance 3.

**Game flow:**
- T2 Mountain → T3 Ragavan dash. **Ragavan T3 connect exiled Claude's Swords to Plowshares** (top of library) — pre-emptively removed Claude's best 1-mana removal piece before he had a board. Massive tempo.
- T5 Chaos Channeler {2}{R}{R} cast via Treasure ramp. Began the Wild Magic Surge → exile-cast loop. Channeler rolled 18 + 15 + 17 + 16 across 4 attack turns — never below 10-19 mode (2 cards exiled + cast each time). Channeler-cast cards across the game: Charisma Bobblehead, Hoarding Ogre, Lae'zel's Acrobatics, Maddening Hex, plus 5+ free lands. **Effectively drew us 10+ extra cards.**
- T7 Mr. House cast. T9 Wyll, Blade of Frontiers cast. **All three "+1 die ignore lowest" effects (Barbarian Class L1 + Mr. House + Wyll) — wait, Mr. House doesn't have that effect; Barbarian Class L1 + Wyll are the two replacement effects. They stack: d20 = 3-dice-take-highest (~95% chance ≥15); 2d6 = 4-dice-take-highest-2 (avg 9-11 of max 12).** Every Mr. House activation and Wild Magic Surge after this point hit max-mode.
- T9 Myrkul's Edict {1}{B} d20=19 → forced Claude to sac Atla. Atla → CZ.
- T13 Contraband Livestock {1}{W} d20 nat 20 targeting Atla → Atla exiled, Claude got useless 0/1 Goat token. Second Atla removal in 4 turns, both at instant-speed 2-mana cost.
- T13 attacks (Channeler + Ragavan + 3 Robots) for 12 face damage. Hoarding Ogre cast from Channeler's exile.
- T14 (Claude's turn): Claude conceded at 8 life. Had no creature + Akroma's Will combo within 8 mana max. We had Ruinous Ultimatum in hand ready to wipe his 9 lands if he stuck a threat.

**MVP winner: Mr. House + Barbarian Class + Wyll triple-stack engine.** All 3 came down by T9. Once stacked, every die roll = trivial high-roll = guaranteed Robot/Treasure generation. Compounded with Channeler's "cast for free from your own library" effect, opp had infinite gas + removal for the entire game.

**MVP loser (theirs): Atla Palani.** Couldn't stick. First Atla T8 → Myrkul edict T9 (1-turn life). Recast Atla T12 → Contraband Livestock T13 (1-turn life). Never activated Atla once. Engine never came online.

**Key lessons (Dicebots pilot — me):**
- **Wyll + Mr. House + Barbarian Class is the lock combo against non-counter decks.** Atla's RGW colors have no counterspells. All 3 engine pieces are 1-3 mana cheap. Once deployed, opp's removal-on-dice cards (Myrkul, Contraband Livestock, etc.) become 2-mana single-target removal at instant speed with ~95% hit-rate on max mode. This is the matchup pattern to repeat.
- **Ragavan T2-T3 connect to steal opp's removal pile is game-defining vs RGW decks.** Atla, Wakening Sun's Avatar, Hatcher all rely on the removal pile holding to clear path. Steal Path/STP/Beast Within early = engine free reign. **Pickup play:** Always dash Ragavan on the earliest possible turn vs RGW decks; don't save him for a better board state.
- **Channeler's exile-cast effectively replaces ~half our drawn cards with bonus plays.** Over a 12-turn game with 5 attacks, Channeler "drew" 10 cards from our library. The cards exiled were our threats (Hoarding Ogre, Charisma Bobblehead) — meaning we DEPLOYED them earlier than mana would normally allow. **Pickup play:** protect Channeler with chump blockers if necessary. Each Channeler attack is worth ~2 cards of value.
- **Myrkul's Edict scaled by dice replacement = "almost always" instant-speed Atla removal.** d20 = 3-dice-take-highest. 19 came up T9 — but even 15-18 hits the "each opp sacs" mode. Pair with Contraband Livestock for layered Atla removal. **Pickup play:** keep Myrkul + Contraband + Deadly Rollick as the 3-piece removal pile for sticky commanders.
- **The dice engine made Wand of Wonder unnecessary this game.** Last Dicebots-vs-Atla matchup (Game 88), Wand was the MVP for mining opp's library. This game, Channeler's exile-cast did the same job at attack speed without the Wand activation cost. Both packages combined = even stronger.

- T15 Human: Mr. House cast from CZ ({R}{W}{B}). Claude countered with Drown in the Loch. Mr. House +2 tax — never recast.
- T16 Claude: Mirko Vosk + Hexing Squelcher deployed. Wrexial steal of Danse Macabre — Claude sacced Hedron Crab (toughness 2), I sacced Neyali (lowest impact since Ojer was gone). d20 roll 7 + 2 = 9 (<15, return 1). Claude returned Neyali to his side permanently. My token DS engine offline.
- T17 Human: cast Ancient Copper Dragon + Anim Pakal but had to keep them sick. Attacked with 1 Soldier to trigger Pakal's Gnome attacker — Claude blocked both with Cat + Squelcher trivially. Dragon never got a swing.

**Key lessons (Dicebots pilot — me):**

- **Ojer Axonil is the must-protect piece in this matchup.** Without Ojer, Roastmaster Alliance is 1 dmg per ETB — manageable. With Ojer it's 4. Lost it to a Wrexial-stolen Deadly Rollick T14 because there was no protection. Hold Lae'zel's Acrobatics if drawn; consider Heroic Intervention-class effects in the maindeck.
- **Mr. House countered = the deck doesn't fire.** Without Mr. House on the battlefield, dice triggers don't make Robots. The Bobblehead activations and Pakal trigger still make tokens but the avalanche from Mr. House dice payoff never assembles. **Have a backup engine slot** — second copy effect, or a hard-to-counter token spawner.
- **No flying defense vs Stormtide Leviathan is fatal.** Ancient Copper Dragon was my one out and it got Farideh-Fireball'd via Wrexial steal the same turn Stormtide came down. Need 2+ flyers or reach in the deck for the Stormtide/Wrexial/Mirko matchup specifically.
- **Anim Pakal's Gnome-on-attack engine is cute but doesn't break locks.** Gnomes are 1/1 ground tokens — they sit in Stormtide's lock the same as everything else. Pakal needs Akki Lavarunner-class haste tokens or flyer-makers to actually push through.

### Game 88 (Human): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T22 (sides 22) via Brass Dragon lethal. Human life 9, Claude life 5. Comeback. Dominance 2.
- **T2-T6 setup:** Sol Ring T4 + Wand of Wonder T4 + Mr. House T6 + Reckless Fireweaver T6. Pinger engine online by T8 (Fireweaver damages Claude on every artifact ETB).

In response, Mr. House activated Wand of Wonder again (rolled 12 → exile chain) — but the actual save came from earlier-resolved Lae'zel's Acrobatics on the stack: phased out Mr. House, Fireweaver, Hexing Squelcher (all non-token creatures) before Avatar resolved. Tokens (robots + knights + treasure) were destroyed by Avatar but the engine creatures survived in exile + life locked until next turn. Phased back T20.
- **T17-T19 Claude rebuild:** Claude cast Greater Good T17 to dig 7 cards (sac Avatar). T19 free turn (TP life-lock removed Brass attack threat for 1 turn): Atla recast + Ashnod's Altar + Eggs sac chain → 2 Atla flips (Ghalta + Silverclad) + Ghalta ETB cheated 3 creatures from hand (Ojer Taq + Etali + Brontodon). 9-creature board out of nowhere.
- **T20 Brass Dragon cast:** {5}{B}{B} 7/6 flying. Sick T20, ready T22. Claude has no flyer/reach defenders.
- **T21 Claude alpha attack:** 9 attackers, 64 power, 44 trample. **Robot wall absorbed it** — 6 robots on Brontodon (perfect 18 tough vs 20 power = 2 trample), 4 on Ghalta (12 tough vs 12 = 0 trample), 2 on Etali (6=6, 0), 2 on Token (6=6, 0). Squelcher chumped Ojer. Fireweaver + Brass cleaned up smaller attackers. Total face: 12. Human 21 → 9.

Hex pinged him on every noncreature spell. Combined with Mr. House dice triggers, Claude took 30+ chip damage over the game.
- **Lae'zel's Acrobatics MVP play:** Single 4-mana instant that phased out the entire engine before an 8-mana wipe = traded 4 mana for 8 mana of opponent's commitment. **Pickup play:** ALWAYS hold Acrobatics in matchups vs board wipe decks (Atla Avatar, Boros Charm, Cyclonic Rift, etc.).
- **Key lessons:**
  - **Wand of Wonder is a recurring 1-of-many removal pile** if opponent's deck has any instants/sorceries. Atla deck has 4-5 instants/sorceries (STP, Stroke of Midnight, Ram Through, Savage Stomp, TP). Wand mined all of them across 4 activations.
  - **Mr. House dice activation chain compounds.** T17 4d6 (3 treasure mana) made 4 robots + 2 treasures. T22 in-combat 4d6 made 3 robots + 2 treasures. 14+ robot tokens by end of game.
  - **Brass Dragon is the closer vs Atla deck WHEN ANSWERS ARE DEPLETED.** Atla has Zetalpa, Bonehoard, reach dinos, and 5-6 instant removal pieces — but Wand of Wonder pre-mined the removal (STP, Stroke, Ram Through) before I cast Brass. Strategy: keep stripping their library with Wand activations, then close with a flyer once their stack of answers is in opp's exile/graveyard.
  - **Robot wall vs trample:** With 14+ 3/3 robots blocking 4 tramplers (20+12+6+6 = 44 power), you can absorb each trampler with EXACT toughness (3-toughness blockers in groups of 6/4/2/2) for 0-2 trample face per attacker. Mathematically tight but lethal-shutting.
  - **Phasing > board wipe** when trade is 4 mana for 8 mana opponent. Lae'zel's Acrobatics paid for itself many times over.

### Game 90 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Human (Dicebots) wins T20 by Claude (Zinnia) concession. Sides 20. Human life 35, Claude life 14. CDMG 5. Dominance 2.
- T7 Kambal + T9 Mr. House + T9 Warleader's Call locked Zinnia's offspring plan early.
- T14 Claude Time Wipe wiped Kambal + Mr. House + Ancient Gold Dragon — bought ~4 turns of recovery.
- T17 Impact Tremors stacked the lock back up to 2 dmg per ETB. Hoarding Ogre + Lightfoot Rogue assembled the rolling treasure/dice engine.
- T19 Earth-Cult Elemental nat 20 forced Claude to sac 2 Spirit tokens, dropping Zinnia from 5/3 to 1/3 flying.
- T19 alpha (HO+LR+Robot, 2 more Robots from attack triggers) brought Claude to 14, with 8-attacker T21 alpha incoming. Concede T20.
- **MVPs:** Kambal (early game lock), Earth-Cult Elemental (lategame closer with the nat 20).
- **Lesson:** Kambal-based punishment, even on a non-commander, is enough to destroy precon-strength deck builds that lean hard on tokens. Backed up by WC + IT, the lock is unanswerable without enchantment removal in 3 colors.
