# Dicebots / Mr. House — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Mr. House, President and CEO | Colors: Red/White/Black (Mardu)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Mr. House** creates Robot tokens and triggers pinger effects.
- **Agility Bobblehead + Saddle + Sword of Hours** is a must-answer package — unblockable + growing counters = fast clock.
- **Brazen Dwarf + Delina + Hedron Detonator** — pinger engine that deals damage on token creation and dice rolls.
- **Attrition** — sacrifice a creature to destroy target nonblack creature. Note: does NOT work on black creatures (relevant vs Ghave).

## How to Beat This Deck
- Board wipe (Damnation) before the pinger engine fires — once Brazen Dwarf + Hedron Detonator are online with Robot tokens, damage adds up fast.
- Answer Agility Bobblehead or Sword of Hours before counters accumulate.
- Attrition is strong but has the nonblack restriction — exploit this with black commanders/creatures.

## Matchup Notes
- Token/pinger strategy is vulnerable to well-timed board wipes. Don't wait for the engine to assemble — wipe proactively.
- **vs. Dargons/Scion:** No flying defense is a major problem. Scion copies 10/10 flyers for lethal commander damage. Need early pressure + removal for Scion before it snowballs. Reanimation (Coiling Rebirth, Virtue of Persistence) brings back dragons — exile-based removal preferred.
- **vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (1-1):** Revel in Riches creates a "damned if you block" dynamic — Squirrel deaths generate Treasures. But Squirrel Food has Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond combo as a race-ending finisher. Night of Sweets' Revenge turns Foods into {G} mana, accelerating both engine AND combo assembly. Path to Exile is risky — the free land can enable combo mana. Prioritize enchantment interaction or winning before T17. Attrition (in their deck) can answer non-black creatures.
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever/Go-Shintai:** Shrine deck is slow to set up. Aggro with unblockable Robots + Agility Bobblehead outraces the shrine engine. Ancient Copper Dragon + Wyll boosted rolls = Treasure avalanche. Only real out is Farewell — if shrine player sacs a land instead of a shrine to Earth-Cult Elemental, they don't have it.
- **vs. Zinnia Precon (1-1):** Kambal Profiteering Mayor is a HARD COUNTER to Zinnia's offspring/token plan — every token Zinnia makes gives the human a free tapped copy + drains 1 life. Stack Warleader's Call + Impact Tremors on top and every opp-controlled creature ETB drains Zinnia 2 life. Earth-Cult Elemental on a high d20 roll forces 1-2 permanent sacs, often the Spirit/Soldier tokens fueling Zinnia's +X/+0. Mr. House dice activation generates Robots that ALSO trigger WC+IT for 2 dmg each. Race plan: assemble Kambal + WC + IT + ECE to lock the deck into discarding tokens for life. Time Wipe is the deck's only out — wipes ECE/HO/LR but leaves WC+IT enchantments. Deck has no flying defense (still relevant when Zinnia survives), but Zinnia at 1/3 with 0 base-power-1 buffs is a 16-turn clock — easy to outrace.

### Game 90 (Claude): Dicebots/Mr. House vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Human (Dicebots) wins T20 by Claude (Zinnia) concession. Sides 20. Human life 35, Claude life 14. CDMG 5. Dominance 2.
- T7 Kambal + T9 Mr. House + T9 Warleader's Call locked Zinnia's offspring plan early.
- T14 Claude Time Wipe wiped Kambal + Mr. House + Ancient Gold Dragon — bought ~4 turns of recovery.
- T17 Impact Tremors stacked the lock back up to 2 dmg per ETB. Hoarding Ogre + Lightfoot Rogue assembled the rolling treasure/dice engine.
- T19 Earth-Cult Elemental nat 20 forced Claude to sac 2 Spirit tokens, dropping Zinnia from 5/3 to 1/3 flying.
- T19 alpha (HO+LR+Robot, 2 more Robots from attack triggers) brought Claude to 14, with 8-attacker T21 alpha incoming. Concede T20.
- **MVPs:** Kambal (early game lock), Earth-Cult Elemental (lategame closer with the nat 20).
- **Lesson:** Kambal-based punishment, even on a non-commander, is enough to destroy precon-strength deck builds that lean hard on tokens. Backed up by WC + IT, the lock is unanswerable without enchantment removal in 3 colors.
