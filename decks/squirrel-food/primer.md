# Squirrel Food / Chatterfang — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Chatterfang, Squirrel General | Colors: Black/Green (Golgari)

## Strategy Overview
BG Aristocrats/Token deck built around Food and Squirrel tokens. The deck generates massive token value through Chatterfang's doubling (any token creation also makes Squirrel tokens), Food synergies for life/mana, and sacrifice outlets for aristocrat drains. Wins through infinite combos or overwhelming board presence.

## Key Combo Lines

### Infinite Life Drain
- **Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond** (or **Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose**): Any life gain triggers opponent life loss, which triggers life gain → infinite loop. Any Food sacrifice or combat damage starts it.
- Exquisite Blood ({4}{B}) + Sanguine Bond ({3}{B}{B}) = 9 mana total. Vito ({2}{B}) is cheaper substitute for Sanguine Bond.

### Infinite Tokens/Mana (Aristocrat Engine)
- **Chatterfang + Pitiless Plunderer + Ashnod's Altar**: Sac a Squirrel to Altar ({C}{C}) → Plunderer makes Treasure → Chatterfang makes a Squirrel (token was created) → sac new Squirrel → repeat. Infinite colorless mana + infinite death triggers. Add Meathook Massacre or any drain payoff to win.
- **Chatterfang + Academy Manufactor + any sacrifice**: Each Food/Clue/Treasure creation becomes all three, and Chatterfang adds a Squirrel on top. Massive value avalanche.

### Token Doublers
- **Peregrin Took**: Each token creation also adds a Food.
- **Chatterfang**: Each token creation also adds Squirrels equal to the number of tokens created.
- **Academy Manufactor**: Each Clue/Food/Treasure becomes one of each.
- Stacking these multiplies exponentially.

## Key Cards by Role

### Sacrifice Outlets
- Ashnod's Altar (mana), Attrition (creature removal), Chatterfang's {B} ability (creature removal using Squirrels)

### Token Producers
- Gilded Goose, Bumbleflower's Sharepot, Experimental Confectioner, Pippin, Provisions Merchant, Cache Grab, many Food-creating spells

### Payoffs
- Meathook Massacre (drain on death), Vito/Sanguine Bond/Exquisite Blood (life drain loops), Honored Dreyleader (grows with Squirrels/Food), Syr Ginger (grows with artifact deaths)

### Removal
- Kindred Dominance (one-sided wipe choosing Squirrel), Spider Food (artifact/enchantment/flier removal), Bake into a Pie (creature removal + Food), The Meathook Massacre (X-based wipe)

### Ramp
- Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Commander's Sphere, Birds of Paradise, Cultivate, Night of the Sweets' Revenge (Foods tap for {G})

## Key Rulings
- **Chatterfang forestwalk**: Can't be blocked if defender controls a Forest. Check opponent's lands.
- **Chatterfang's {B} sac ability**: "{B}, Sacrifice X Squirrels: Target creature gets +X/-X until end of turn." This is instant-speed removal using Squirrel tokens.
- **Academy Manufactor replacement**: Replaces the creation, not triggers off it. If you'd create 2 Foods, you create 2 of each (Clue+Food+Treasure × 2).
- **Peregrin Took**: "those tokens plus an additional Food token" — the Food is IN ADDITION to the normal tokens.

## Matchup Notes

### vs. Dicebots / Mr. House (Games 64, 70)
- **Results: 1-1. Game 64 LOSS as Claude (T14, lethal combat). Game 70 WIN as Human (T17, Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond combo).**
- Dicebots generates Robots passively from dice rolls — goes wide fast. Revel in Riches punishes chump blocking (Squirrel deaths = Treasures). Must answer Revel or accept damage.
- Night of Sweets' Revenge is the mana accelerator that enables the 10-mana combo (Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond). Foods tapping for {G} is critical.
- Beast Within is premium removal vs Ancient Copper Dragon (d20 Treasures on hit threatens Revel win). The 3/3 Beast is negligible cost.
- Attrition handles non-black creatures (Mr. House, Robots, Ancient Copper Dragon). Strong in this matchup.
- Path to Exile on your creatures gifts a land — can backfire if it enables combo mana. Beware.
- **Key takeaway:** Combo finish (Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond) outraces Revel in Riches. Prioritize assembling combo over fighting the token war.

### vs. Bears / Ayula (Games 42, 60)
- **Results: 0-2. Game 42 LOSS (T12, CDMG 22). Game 60 LOSS (T12, CDMG 22+).**
- Ayula's fight trigger is the primary problem. Any Bear can fight (not just Ayula) — the second mode is "target Bear you control fights target creature you don't control." Once Ayula has 4+ power, she one-shots Chatterfang (3 toughness). Every replacement creature met the same fate.
- Game 60: Deployed Chatterfang T5 before Ayula had counters — got 6 CDMG in (good). But T10 triple bear chain (Alpine Grizzly + Ulvenwald Bear Morbid + Bear Cub) wiped entire board through fight triggers. Cookbook play to trade Bear Token for Chatterfang was correct (sac Squirrel for +1/-1, both die) but couldn't stop the avalanche.
- Chatterfang's forestwalk is unblockable but only 3 damage per swing — too slow to race Ayula's snowball.
- Spider Food can't hit creatures (only artifacts/enchantments/flyers). Deck desperately needs instant-speed creature removal (Go for the Throat, Bake into a Pie) in the opening hand vs Ayula.
- **Key takeaway:** Against Ayula, deploy Chatterfang early (before counters stack) OR hold until you have removal. The middle ground (deploy into a 4/4+ Ayula) is death.
- **Key takeaway:** Cast token/Food generators BEFORE combat so Chatterfang's doubling creates Squirrels for sac ability defense. (Game 60: Rapacious Guest cast post-combat, missed a Food+Squirrel trigger.)
- **Key takeaway:** Earth King gives Bears repeatable land ramp — expect the deck to accelerate hard in the mid-game.

- **Error: Chatterfang should have been T2 play** (Forest + Sol Ring = {2}{G}). Deployed Honest Rutstein instead for cost reduction. Lost 2 turns of unblockable pressure. Always deploy forestwalk commander when opponent controls a Forest.

- **Error: T7 bad damage assignment** — ordered Brimaz first, left 1/1 Cat alive. Kill what you can kill.

### vs. Quit Hitting Yourself / Wayta (Game 80)
- **Result: 0-1. Game 80 LOSS as Claude (T6, Wheel + Gideon's Sacrifice + Reckoner reflection).**
- The kill turn requires only Reckoner + 4 mana ({2}{R} Wheel + {W} Gideon's Sacrifice). Once Reckoner is on board, the lethal threshold is frighteningly low — T4-T5 minimum.
- **Reckoner is a priority kill target, not a bystander.** Even without Wayta out, the combo with Wheel + Gideon's Sacrifice is live.
- Feed the Cycle on Reckoner ({1}{B} + sac Food for forage) is the cleanest answer. Bake into a Pie also works. Save these for Reckoner over lesser bodies.
- Don't fight Reckoner with creatures — it reflects the damage. Only "destroy" or "exile" effects safely remove it.
- **Mana discipline:** keep at least 2 black sources untapped on opp's main phases when Reckoner is live. Land drops matter — every missed land could be the missing point of mana for a key removal spell.

### vs. Eldrazi / Zhulodok (Game 71)
- **Result: Squirrel Food WIN as Human (T19, lethal combat). Dominance 3.**
- Attrition is the MVP — every Eldrazi dies to {B} + 1 Squirrel (except Breaker of Creation which has hexproof from colors). Deploy Attrition + start saccing immediately.
- Honored Dreyleader is a monster in this matchup. Eldrazi has no efficient creature removal and Dreyleader grows off every Food/Squirrel entering. Reached 13/13 trample.
- Kindred Dominance choosing Squirrel is the finisher. Eldrazi creatures are never Squirrels. One-sided wipe that keeps the entire engine.
- **Spider Food on Metalworker was the true game-winning play.** Metalworker and Forsaken Monument are Eldrazi's two critical ramp pieces — killing either before they activate delays the cascade engine by 3-4 turns. Prioritize holding Spider Food for these targets over lesser artifacts.
- Breaker of Creation (hexproof from colors) is the one card Attrition/Chatterfang can't answer. Need Kindred Dominance, Meathook Massacre, or combat to deal with it.
- Eldrazi without fast mana is very slow. Zhulodok didn't land until T12. Pressure early with Chatterfang beats + grow Dreyleader before cascade engine starts.

### Deck Improvement Suggestions
- **Lack of flying blockers/reach**: 3 games now lost to flying damage with zero answers. Consider Ishkanah, Grafwidow (reach + spider tokens), Silklash Spider, or Hornet Queen (flying deathtouch tokens + Chatterfang doubles).
- **Instant removal is thin**: Bake into a Pie ({2}{B}{B}) and Go for the Throat are the main creature removal. Against must-answer threats like Scion, drawing one of two cards isn't reliable. Consider Beast Within ({2}{G}, hits any permanent), Deadly Rollick (free with commander), or Assassin's Trophy ({B}{G}).
- **Enchantment removal**: Spider Food is the only maindeck enchantment removal. Consider Haywire Mite (artifact creature, sac to exile artifact/enchantment, gains life for Vito).
- **Board wipes needed earlier**: Kindred Dominance and Meathook Massacre are the main wipes but both are expensive. Consider Toxic Deluge ({2}{B}, pay life for -X/-X) — synergizes with Vito (if you gain life afterward).

### vs. Food and Fellowship / Frodo+Sam (Game from 2026-05-10)
- **Result: Squirrel Food WIN as Human (T18, concession). Dominance 1 (Human missed lethal 2 turns earlier — forgot Vito grants lifelink via activation).**
- **THE LINE: Vito + Ygra under Vito-lifelink-activation = unanswerable kill.** Once Vito is on board AND opp has 5 mana for {3}{B}{B} activation, attacking with Ygra (huge from Food deaths) into ANY chump = opp gains 30+ life from lifelink → Vito triggers → opp drains opp's life total → lethal regardless of opp's life.
- **Don't forget Vito's activation.** Vito is not just a passive drain — the {3}{B}{B} grants ALL your creatures lifelink UEOT. Combined with Ygra's huge body (no trample needed — chump still takes full lifelink dmg without trample), this is a one-card-on-board kill button.
- The Food/Fellowship deck was forced to chump Ygra every turn (Sam→CZ redirect, Pippin/Halflings/Samwise as fodder). Each chump triggered Ygra's growth from Food→GY. Ygra reached 30/30.
- Chatterfang's CDMG clock got the deck to 18 CDMG by T17, forcing Claude to StP Chatterfang T18 (Chatterfang to CZ via commander redirect). Lethal Vito-Ygra line was still live next turn.
- Vito's drain trigger also punishes opp's lifelink (Gumdrop) and Food sacs — passive value even before activation.
- **Key takeaway:** ACTIVATE VITO when you have a fat attacker and a chump opponent. The activation is the win, not the passive trigger.
