# Fun Guys / Ghave — Deck Primer
Commander: Ghave, Guru of Spores | Colors: White/Black/Green (Abzan)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Infinite drain combo:** Ghave + Parallel Lives + Ashnod's Altar + Zulaport Cutthroat (or Blood Artist). Remove counter from Ghave ({1}) → 2 Saprolings (Parallel Lives) → sac 1 to Altar ({C}{C}) → sac other to Ghave for counter ({1}) → Zulaport drains 2 per loop. Mana-neutral, counter-neutral, infinite.
- **Ghave + Parallel Lives + Ashnod's Altar is mana/counter-neutral, NOT infinite by itself.** Need a payoff (Zulaport Cutthroat, Blood Artist, or similar aristocrat) to convert the loop into a win.
- **The Mycotyrant** generates tokens that can spiral — protect it or it becomes a must-answer threat for opponents.

## Key Rulings
- **Ghave is BLACK — immune to Attrition** ("destroy target nonblack creature").
- **Kambal, Profiteering Mayor** copies opponent tokens only once per turn. After the first batch, subsequent token creation in the same turn is NOT copied. Ghave loops are only taxed once.

## Game History

### Game 5 (as opponent): Dargons/Scion (Claude) vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Human)
**Result:** Claude (Scion) wins T16 via commander damage.
- The Mycotyrant was killed T6 before generating tokens — protect key pieces.
- Scion → Ureni blanked all Abzan removal (Path, black kill spells).

### Game 7 (piloted): Fun Guys/Ghave (Claude) vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T20 via infinite Zulaport Cutthroat drain.

**Key plays:**
- T10: Damnation wiped Mr. House + Brazen Dwarf + Delina + Hedron Detonator before the pinger engine fired.
- T12: Ghave deployed, immediately made 2 Saprolings.
- T14: Parallel Lives online. Ghave attacked for 4 commander damage (8 total across 2 swings).
- T18: Ashnod's Altar deployed — combo piece 3 of 4 in place.
- T20: Topdecked Zulaport Cutthroat at 9 life. Infinite loop activated for the win.

**Key lessons:**
- **Mana tracking on opponent's turn:** Permanents don't untap until YOUR untap step. Don't overcount available mana on opponent's turn.
- **Damnation is premium against token/pinger strategies** — use it before the engine fires, not after.
- **No summoning sickness violations this game.** No hidden information violations this game.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Scion of the Ur-Dragon:** Scion → Ureni blanks Path to Exile and black removal. Need exile effects that don't target (board wipes, sacrifice effects) or colorless removal.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House:** Agility Bobblehead + Saddle + Sword of Hours is a must-answer package (unblockable + growing counters = fast clock). Damnation before the pinger engine assembles.
