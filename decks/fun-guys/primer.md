# Fun Guys / Ghave — Deck Primer
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Ghave, Guru of Spores | Colors: White/Black/Green (Abzan)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Primary win — infinite drain combo:** Ghave + Parallel Lives + Ashnod's Altar + Zulaport Cutthroat (or Blood Artist). Remove counter from Ghave ({1}) → 2 Saprolings (Parallel Lives) → sac 1 to Altar ({C}{C}) → sac other to Ghave for counter ({1}) → Zulaport drains 2 per loop. Mana-neutral, counter-neutral, infinite.
- **Ghave + Parallel Lives + Ashnod's Altar is mana/counter-neutral, NOT infinite by itself.** Need a payoff (Zulaport Cutthroat, Blood Artist, or similar aristocrat) to convert the loop into a win.
- **Backup win — stack doublers, then alpha with pumped saps (Game 82, 207 lethal).** Doubling Season + Mondrak + Vorinclex makes token/counter effects ~4x and each new piece exponentially scales the others — stack ASAP regardless of board pressure. **Moldervine Reclamation is the enabler** (every sap death = +1 card +1 life → combo finds itself). **Pallid Mycoderm is the finisher** (instant-speed sac saps for +1/+1 to all fungi/saps); **Deathspore Thallid (-1/-1) is the flexible sac sink** (remove blockers vs power through). Beseech the Mirror bargains 4 MV creatures (e.g. Pallid) for free — bargain works on tokens.
- **Without Pallid Mycoderm or Slimefoot, saprolings are 1/1 chumps** — they don't scale and can't race counter-based go-wide boards.
- **The Mycotyrant** generates tokens that can spiral — protect it or it becomes a must-answer threat for opponents. Needs descend triggers (a sac outlet) to ramp; minimal output without one.

### Piloting notes (recurring)
- **Combo is inconsistent — deck frequently whiffs on Parallel Lives / Ashnod's Altar / Zulaport** (Games 9, 31, 45). Lean on Skullclamp draw (clamp saprolings) and tutor discipline to dig.
- **Protect Ghave; don't over-rely on the commander.** Repeatedly Path'd/removed before generating value (Games 5, 9, 24, 31). +2 tax compounds — recast only when nothing else does the work. Deploy commander-independent token gens (Mycoloth, Mycotyrant, Saproling Migration, Nemata) so the board survives Ghave's death.
- **Mulligan for WBG fixing.** Ghave is {2}{W}{B}{G}; landing it T8+ is often fatal (Games 31, 48, 100). Mull hands with no white access even on 3 lands; mull aggressively for ramp vs fast combo/aggro.
- **Board wipes are essential** vs counter-based go-wide and token/pinger decks (Damnation, Toxic Deluge) — use BEFORE the engine fires, not after.
- **Almost no graveyard recursion — never sac a key piece to dodge a shuffle/bounce** (Game 9); redraw or retutor instead. When saccing to Ghave, prefer remove-counter (make a Saproling) over add-counter — a lone counter does nothing, saps have many uses.
- **Tutor discipline:** save Demonic Tutor for a combo finisher or stabilizer unless you're missing land drops/colors.
- **Mondrak is fragile** (4 toughness, no innate protection) — hold until Privileged Position is online. Parallel Lives is likewise fragile; one Naturalize dismantles the plan.

## Key Rulings
- **Ghave is BLACK — immune to Attrition** ("destroy target nonblack creature").
- **Kambal, Profiteering Mayor** copies opponent tokens only once per turn. After the first batch, subsequent token creation in the same turn is NOT copied. Ghave loops are only taxed once.
- **Indestructible does NOT save from 0 toughness or -X/-X.** Deathspore Thallid (-1/-1 stack) and setting toughness ≤ 0 kill indestructible/0-tox bodies via SBA (killed an indestructible UG in Game 82; needed vs Brash Taunter in Game 100). "Destroy" wipes (Damnation) do NOT kill indestructibles — use Toxic Deluge / Deathspore for those.
- **Skullclamp kills Darksteel Mutation (or any 0/1) targets.** +1/-1 → 0 toughness → SBA; indestructible doesn't save. Opp draws 2 and recasts commander, so Mutation is weaker into Skullclamp decks.
- **Imprisoned in the Moon on Ghave is game-ending.** Unlike Darksteel Mutation, there's no way to sac the imprisoned permanent for value, and the deck has almost no enchantment removal (consider Nature's Claim / Krosan Grip).

**Result:** Human wins T26 via combat damage (76 total attack power). Claude at -30 life.

**Key plays:**

**Key lessons:**
- **Munda's Vanguard is must-kill.** Repeatable +1/+1 counters on ALL creatures compounds exponentially. Prioritize removal on Munda over Sokka.
- **Mondrak is fragile.** At 4 toughness with no innate protection, it dies to most removal. Consider holding it until Privileged Position is online.
- **Don't sac to avoid shuffle-into-library.** This deck has almost no graveyard recursion. If an opponent tries to shuffle a key piece back into your library, LET IT HAPPEN — you can redraw or tutor for it later. Saccing it to Ghave makes it gone forever. And if you do sac to Ghave, prefer making a Saproling (remove counter) over adding a counter (sac creature) — saprolings have 10+ uses in this deck, a lone counter does nothing.
- **Saproling armies can't race counter-based go-wide.** 1/1 tokens are outclassed when every opponent creature grows by +1/+1 per turn cycle.
- **Need board wipes against Allies.** Damnation, Toxic Deluge, or similar would have reset the Munda snowball.
- **Combo pieces never assembled.** Drew no Parallel Lives, Ashnod's Altar, or Zulaport Cutthroat. Consider adding more tutors or card draw.
- **Tutor discipline:** Unless you're missing land drops, save Demonic Tutor for a combo finisher (Blood Artist / Zulaport Cutthroat) or a stabilizer (Dictate of Erebos). Ghave builds board presence slowly but reliably — once you have a wide board, a single tutor for the right piece can flip the game. Tutoring for ramp is valid when you're stuck on colors or missing drops, but if the mana will come naturally, hold the tutor for something that wins.
- **No summoning sickness violations.** Multiple hidden information violations early (hand evaluation reasoning leaked).

### Game 24 (piloted): Fun Guys/Ghave (Claude) vs. Boros Equipment/Kellan (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T14 via combat damage (lethal on board). Claude 16 life, Human 40.

**Key plays:**
- T6: Darksteel Mutation on Kellan — strong tempo play, shut down commander.
- T7: Opponent used Skullclamp on mutated Kellan (0/1 + 1/-1 = 0 toughness, indestructible doesn't save from SBA). Drew 2, Kellan to command zone. Darksteel Mutation wasted.
- T9: Bruenor Battlehammer deployed. Foundry Inspector reduced all artifact costs by {1}.
- T10: Claude held 5 mana open (Ghave mana) to hold Swords to Plowshares. Telegraphed interaction by not casting Ghave.
- T11: Opponent cast Haystack (protection piece). **Claude let it resolve while holding Swords — critical error.** Should have Swordsed Bruenor in response to Haystack on the stack. Instead waited, opponent activated Haystack to phase out Bruenor in response to Swords. Swords fizzled.

**Key lessons:**
- **Use removal BEFORE protection resolves.** When holding instant-speed removal and opponent casts a protection piece (Haystack, Lightning Greaves, etc.), respond while it's on the stack. Once it resolves, the window is gone. Treat protection pieces like counterspells — they signal your last clean shot.
- **Don't telegraph interaction.** Having exactly commander mana and passing without casting anything screams "I have removal." Either cast Ghave and find removal later, or Swords immediately at the start of opponent's turn before they can deploy protection.
- **Skullclamp kills Darksteel Mutation targets.** 0/1 + Skullclamp (+1/-1) = 0 toughness. Indestructible doesn't prevent SBA from 0 toughness. Opponent draws 2 and recasts commander. Darksteel Mutation is less effective when Skullclamp is on the field.
- **Foundry Inspector + Bruenor is the explosive engine.** Discounted equipment + free equips = multiple equipment per turn. Board snowballs fast. Must be disrupted early.
- **Insidious Roots mana engine never came online.** Ghave kept getting removed before saprolings could accumulate. Need to protect Ghave or deploy token generators that don't depend on the commander.

### Game 100 (piloted): Fun Guys/Ghave (Claude) vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T17 (33 sides) via concession facing lethal. Claude 16 life, Human 38. Dominance 1 (mana screw — Claude drew 0 lands T8-T16).

**Key plays:**

- T6: Germinator deployed as only early body. Dead T9 to Wayta-fight Neyith.
- T8: Mondrak deployed unprotected (no sac fodder for indestructible counter).
- T10: Stroke of Midnight on Wayta — sent it to CZ with +2 tax. **Wrong target.** Should have held for a reflector.
- T11: Brash Taunter deployed (indestructible reflector) — no answer in hand.
- T12: Symmetry Matrix.
- T14: Nemata deployed (anti-removal: exile opp's dying creatures, +saps). Doesn't help vs indestructible Brash.
- T15: Wayta recast + Outrider en-Kor (combo keystone for chain).
- T16: Brash-fight Mondrak with Wayta amp = 8 dmg/fight. Plus another Brash-fight via Wayta-fight = 8 more. **16 dmg in one turn.**
- T17 lethal: opp threatened repeat (Brash + Wayta-fight twice = 16 dmg). 0 mana, 0 instants, no exile/-X/-X for Brash. Concession.

**Key lessons:**
- **Brash Taunter + Wayta amp is the kill condition. Each Brash-fight reflects 4 dmg AMPED TO 8 (Wayta doubles the trigger). Two fight activations per turn (Brash's own + Wayta-fight targeting Brash) = 16 dmg/turn from one indestructible body. Lethal in 2-3 turns from full life.**
- **Stroke of Midnight is the WRONG removal for Brash Taunter.** Stroke is "destroy" — Brash indestructible, only the 1/1 token effect happens. Need exile (Path/Swords/Banishing Light) or -X/-X (Toxic Deluge, Deathspore Thallid sac) or sacrifice/edict (Diabolic Edict, Innocent Blood). Without one of those, Brash is permanent.
- **Nemata's exile-on-death is useless vs Brash.** Brash is indestructible and never dies via destroy effects. Nemata only triggers when opp's creature actually dies — Brash needs -X/-X or sacrifice to die. Nemata is great vs the REST of their board but not the keystone.
- **Mondrak indestructible counter doesn't save you from reflect.** Reflect damage goes to ME as a player, not Mondrak. Mondrak's protection is irrelevant.
- **Stroke of Midnight retargeting priority vs Wayta deck:** (1) Reflectors when castable (Brash, Stuffy Doll, Spitemare, etc. — but Brash specifically is INDESTRUCTIBLE so Stroke is wasted here). (2) Pariah/Pariah's Shield (lock pieces). (3) en-Kor (chain keystone). (4) Wayta only as last resort (recast tax is only +2). Sanctum Weaver as ramp removal.
- **Bring exile removal vs Wayta.** Path to Exile, Swords to Plowshares, Banishing Light, Eldrazi Monument's sac engine (no, that doesn't exile opp's stuff). Best answers: Path/Swords (for indestructible reflectors), Damnation (sweeps everything except indestructibles), Toxic Deluge (sets toughness via -X/-X, kills indestructibles past their toughness). **Damnation can't kill Brash.** Toxic Deluge can.
- **Mulligan aggressively for ramp vs Wayta.** Brash + Wayta deploys T11-T12 with normal ramp. Need to race or assemble combo by then. 1-land/turn drops aren't fast enough.
- **Land draws were the structural failure.** Drew no lands T8-T16 (9 turns no land). Stuck at 4 mana while opp ran 7-9 mana. Sporesower/Sporeoloth/Sporemound/Vorinclex/Eldrazi Monument all rotted in hand for needing 5+ mana or 2G access.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (0-1):** Atla + Palani's Hatcher + Marauding Raptor = self-sustaining engine that flips free hasty creatures every combat. Remove Atla on sight. Damnation is the best answer — resets the entire Dino board. Dictate of Erebos punishes their Egg sacrifices. Parallel Lives needs protection before deploying (Privileged Position). Saproling chump blocks buy time but tramplers (Rampaging Brontodon, Trumpeting Carnosaur) push through regardless.
- **vs. Scion of the Ur-Dragon (0-1):** Scion → Ureni blanks Path to Exile and black removal. Need exile effects that don't target (board wipes, sacrifice effects) or colorless removal.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (1-0):** Agility Bobblehead + Saddle + Sword of Hours is a must-answer package (unblockable + growing counters = fast clock). Damnation before the pinger engine assembles.
- **vs. Avatar Allies/Sokka (0-2):** Kill Munda's Vanguard on sight — cohort snowballs permanently. Sokka giving menace to all Allies makes chump blocking difficult (need 2 blockers per attacker). Deploy Privileged Position early to blank targeted removal and ETB damage (Momo). Board wipes are essential; 1/1 saprolings can't keep up with growing Allies. **Katara, Water Tribe's Hope is an independent win condition** — waterbend X sets all creatures to X/X, lethal with any board. Must remove Katara or keep opponent's creature count low. Razor Rings is efficient removal against our big creatures (softens for combat cleanup). Prioritize color fixing in opening hand — Ghave needs WBG and coming down late is often fatal.
- **vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (0-1):** Mystic Forge is the #1 removal target — it gives perfect cascade sequencing. Platinum Angel blanks the entire Blood Artist/Zulaport drain combo; must have removal (Swords, Path, Stroke of Midnight) before committing to combo. Annihilator 4 + Bane of Bala Ged exile triggers compound to strip 6 permanents per attack — wide boards of saprolings help absorb annihilator but not exile. Eldrazi ramp (Sol Ring + Mana Vault + Metalworker) can deploy Zhulodok T5-7; need early interaction or accept being on the back foot. Damnation is premium — resets their expensive board. Aura Shards with saprolings can dismantle their artifact ramp.
- **vs. Fblthp (1-1):** Fblthp's combo doesn't need to interact with your board — Privileged Position is wasted here. Path to Exile on Fblthp is a trap (shuffle trigger fizzles Path). Save removal for Magus of the Future and One with the Multiverse — those are the engines that enable free-cast ETI. Aura Shards is the best answer if you can get it out before their artifact/enchantment engine comes online. Ghave's infinite drain combo is faster than Fblthp if it assembles, but Fblthp's consistency is the problem — they have 9 combo pairings and multiple cheat-into-play paths.
- **vs. Mill-Steal/Wrexial (0-1):** NEVER play Swamps or Islands — Wrexial has both landwalk types. Use non-basic duals without basic land types. Imprisoned in the Moon on Ghave is game-ending; need enchantment removal (Nature's Claim, Krosan Grip — consider adding). Traumatize + Wrexial GY steal = opponent casts your best instants/sorceries for free. Breach the Multiverse steals your own creatures — Vorinclex under their control is devastating. Prioritize: deploy threats while opponent is tapped out (Wrexial costs 6), hold enchantment removal for Imprisoned/Stasis Field, race to combo before mill-out. Nemesis of Reason mills 10 on ATTACK declaration (blocking doesn't prevent it). Mirelurk Queen + Vorinclex = exponential growth. Aura Shards destroys their artifacts (Sapphire/Jet Medallion, signets).
- **vs. Boros Equipment/Kellan (0-1):** Aura Shards is the MVP — every saproling made destroys an equipment. Deploy it before Kellan assembles voltron. Expect Birthright Boon T2 tutoring Sword of the Animist. Equipment survives Damnation so prioritize Aura Shards / Stroke of Midnight over board wipes. Kellan's double strike + flying equipment can go over saproling tokens. Darksteel Mutation on Kellan works but beware Skullclamp (kills 0/1 mutated creature, draws 2, commander returns). Foundry Inspector + Bruenor is the explosive engine — disrupt early or they deploy 3+ equipment per turn. Haystack gives repeatable phasing protection — remove it or use removal BEFORE it resolves. Race to combo if they're ramping faster.
- **vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (0-1):** Brash Taunter + Wayta amp is the kill condition (16 dmg/turn via 2 Brash-fights with amped reflect). Stroke of Midnight is USELESS on Brash (indestructible — only the 1/1 token half of Stroke resolves). MUST bring exile removal (Path/Swords/Banishing Light) or -X/-X (Toxic Deluge, Deathspore Thallid sac stack) or edict (Diabolic Edict, Innocent Blood). Damnation alone doesn't kill Brash (indestructible). Nemata's exile-on-death doesn't help vs indestructible reflectors (they don't die normally). Stroke priority: hold for Pariah/Pariah's Shield (lock pieces) or non-indestructible reflectors (Spitemare, Screaming Nemesis, Boros Reckoner), NOT Wayta (only +2 tax) and NOT Brash (indestructible). en-Kor is the combo keystone for chain-redirect — also a high-priority Stroke target. Mondrak's indestructible counter doesn't save you from reflect damage (it goes to YOU as a player, not Mondrak). Pressure opp's life total fast — they need ~T11+ to assemble Brash + Wayta + en-Kor, race to combo before then.
