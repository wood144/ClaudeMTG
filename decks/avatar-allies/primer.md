# Avatar Allies / Sokka — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Sokka, Tenacious Tactician | Colors: Blue/Red/White (Jeskai)

## Strategy & Key Mechanics
- **Sokka** (3/3 menace, prowess) gives all other Allies menace + prowess. Every noncreature spell creates a 1/1 Ally token.
- **Gameplan:** Cast noncreature spells to generate Ally tokens, then pump the board with prowess triggers and permanent counters via Munda's Vanguard cohort.
- **Waterbend** is a recurring mechanic — tap creatures/artifacts to help pay costs. Enables big plays with a wide board.
- **Katara, Water Tribe's Hope** — vigilance, ETB creates Ally, Waterbend X gives all creatures base P/T X/X. Major combat trick.
- **Munda's Vanguard** — cohort (tap + Ally) puts +1/+1 counter on ALL creatures. Snowballs fast.
- **Momo, Rambunctious Rascal** — 1/1 flying Ally, ETB deals 4 to tapped creature. Evasion threat with menace from Sokka.

## Key Interactions
- Noncreature spells trigger BOTH Sokka's token generation AND prowess on all Allies simultaneously.
- Munda's Vanguard cohort is repeatable every turn — permanent counters compound quickly.
- Acrobatic Leap / Octopus Form / Firebending Lesson are cheap noncreature combat tricks that each generate an Ally + prowess triggers across the whole board.
- Airbender's Reversal can Airbend own creatures to reset ETBs (e.g., Katara for another Ally token).

### Game 28 (piloted by Claude): Avatar Allies vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T12 (23 sides) via Gideon's Sacrifice + Fiendlash reflection. Claude life 17→-7, Human life 8.
- Sokka cast 4 times total (T7, T13, T15, T19). Removed by Savage Twister, Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, and Wayta fight+Sunsteel. 5th cast would have been 12 mana.
- T10: Successfully disrupted Guilty Conscience + Heroic Intervention combo by casting Firebending Lesson on Wayta in response to HI (before indestructible resolved). Wayta died, GC went to GY. Took 12 damage from the loop but dismantled the combo.
- T20: Drew Acrobatic Leap off Commander's Sphere sac in response to fight. Leap buffed Sokka to 5/7, killing Wayta in the fight. But Sunsteel+Fiendlash triggers killed Sokka too.
- T21: Warring Triad activated as 5/5 flying trample (GY at 11 cards). Sea Gate Loremaster cast for card draw engine. Dealt 7 (opp 15→8).
- **T23 (death):** Swung for exactly 8 with 4 creatures into Gideon's Sacrifice. Fiendlash triggered 4 times at power 6 = 24 damage. Lethal from 17.
- **Lesson:** Fiendlash deals POWER, not reflected damage amount. Multiple small attackers into Gideon's Sacrifice = multiple Fiendlash triggers, each at full power. Should have drawn cards with Loremaster instead of going for the kill — was at 8 vs 17, had time to draw and verify the coast was clear.
- **Deck suggestion:** Need instant-speed artifact/enchantment removal (Wear // Tear, Disenchant) to strip equipment before attacking into reflection. Also counterspells to stop combo assembly.

**Result:** Human wins T18 (18 sides) via flying token swarm. Claude life 8, Human life 33. Commander damage: 3.
- Mulliganed 1-land hand. Kept 4-land hand with EKJ and Sea Gate Loremaster.

- T9: Opponent recast Zinnia + Murmuration. Murmuration became the engine — every spell created 2/3 flying vigilance Storm Crows.
- T10: Sea Gate Loremaster drew 3 cards (haste from WP). Traded Storm Crow in combat. Opponent at 26.
- T11: Opponent deployed Selfless Spirit (with offspring) + Arcane Signet + Path to Exile (killed Loremaster). 3 Storm Crows from Murmuration end step.
- T12: Appa deployed (7/7 flying vigilance haste). All-flying attack with 15 power. Sacced EKP to save Appa from double block. Lost WP. Traded Appa for Zinnia in T13 combat block.
- T14: Recast Sokka + Lightning Strike (killed SS token) + Standstill. But Standstill was a mistake — opponent already had lethal on board without casting.
- T15: Inferno Titan ETB killed Sokka (3 damage = exact lethal). Titan's 3 damage perfectly answered Sokka every cast.
- T16: Recast Sokka + Standstill again. T17: Titan attack trigger killed Sokka again. 16 flying damage → 8 life. Conceded T18.
- **Lesson:** (1) Standstill is terrible when behind — opponent doesn't need to cast to win, and you can't catch up without breaking it yourself. Should have spent that mana on threats. (2) Murmuration must be answered immediately or the flying army snowballs beyond recovery. Deck lacks enchantment removal — Price of Freedom hits artifacts/lands only. (3) Sokka at 3/3 is trivially answered by Inferno Titan's 3-damage trigger. Need to hold instant-speed protection or pump when Titan is on board. (4) Zinnia being a Bird means Murmuration gives her +1/+1 and vigilance — she grows fast with base-power-1 tokens.
- **Deck suggestion:** Needs enchantment removal badly (Disenchant, Wear // Tear, Return to Dust). Also needs more flying threats or reach creatures to contest the air.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Cats/Mirri:** Mirri's combat lockdown (1 blocker when attacking, 1 attacker while tapped) is the main threat. Shield of the Oversoul makes her nearly unkillable — bounce spells (Boomerang Basics) are the best answer since the Aura goes to graveyard permanently. Honest Work shuts down Feline Sovereign's lord effect AND combat-trigger enchantment removal for just {U}. Priority: neutralize Feline Sovereign early (Honest Work, bounce, exile) to protect Whirlwind of Thought and Sokka's Charge. The deck's weakness is aura/equipment dependency — strip the buffs and the creatures are small. Sokka's Charge + prowess stacking creates devastating alpha strikes once the Cat board is weakened.
- **vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (2-0):** Ghave's token engine is dangerous if Mondrak/Parallel Lives come online. Priority targets: token doublers and Ghave itself. Path to Exile and Watery Grasp are key answers. Munda's Vanguard outscales Ghave's 1/1 saprolings quickly. Katara waterbend is a reliable finisher — even without Munda snowball, a wide board + waterbend 9 is lethal. Razor Rings + combat cleans up devoured Mycoloth efficiently. Firebending Lesson on Skullclamp targets denies their draw engine and creates an Ally. Sokka being removed twice didn't matter — Katara closed independently.
- **vs. Da Bears/Ayula:** 1-0. Ayula's fight triggers + Roaming Throne doubling is the main threat — can snowball counters fast. Priority: exile Ayula early and often (StP, Path to Exile). Without Ayula on board, Roaming Throne's doubling is useless and bears are just vanilla bodies. Vigor is dangerous (damage → counters) but secondary to the engine. Menace is the deck's biggest advantage — bear tribal tends to go tall with few large creatures, which can't block menace solo. Sokka's Charge makes this lethal: double strike + lifelink on an unblockable menace board. EKP or similar sacrifice-for-indestructible effects are key to surviving Ayula fight triggers aimed at Sokka.
- **vs. Dargons/Scion (1-0):** Scion on T3 is scary but manageable if you can strip their color fixing (Angel of the Ruins exiling mana rocks is huge). Rowdy Snowballers' stun counter buys a critical turn by neutralizing Scion. Sokka's menace makes the whole board unblockable when opponent only has 1-2 flyers. Katara snowballs fast with end-step counters. Key: deploy Suki early for anthem, go wide before Scion can copy Scourge of Kher Ridges (2 damage to all non-flyers). If Scion is on board, watch for Drakuseth activation (4+3+3 damage on attack), Ureni (pro W/B, unblockable by Angel), or Scourge activation. Keep creature count high so Scourge activation doesn't wipe you completely.
- **vs. Fblthp (0-1):** Fblthp's combo is hard to interact with — Leyline of Anticipation + Magus/OwtM gives instant-speed access to the entire library. Menace + prowess clock is the main advantage; got opponent to 4 life by T17 but couldn't close. Counterspell on Urza was correct (denied artifact engine). Key vulnerability: Unsummon/bounce from top of library can save them at the last second. Need to hold at least one creature back for Cresting Mosasaurus bounce recovery. Prioritize killing planeswalkers (Tezzeret untaps Throne for 4 mana) and artifact mana doublers. If they resolve Omniscience + OwtM, the game is over — counter Omniscience or the enabler that leads to it.
- **vs. Zinnia Precon (0-1):** Murmuration is the #1 threat — generates 2/3 flying vigilance Storm Crows for every spell cast. Zinnia is a Bird (gets Murmuration +1/+1 and vigilance) and scales with base-power-1 tokens. Inferno Titan's 3-damage ETB/attack trigger perfectly kills Sokka every time — need instant-speed pump or protection when Titan is on board. Selfless Spirit + offspring = two indestructible activations protecting the whole air force. Deck has almost no enchantment removal (Price of Freedom only hits artifacts/lands). Priority: answer Murmuration immediately or race before it snowballs. Appa is the best answer for air defense (7/7 flying vigilance) but can be double-blocked.
- **vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta:** 0-2 in the matchup. The reflection engine is the core threat in multiple forms: (1) Guilty Conscience loops (disrupt by dealing damage before indestructible resolves — sequence matters, respond to the SECOND spell). (2) Blazing Sunsteel + Fiendlash on ANY creature creates dangerous reflection — Fiendlash deals POWER per trigger, not damage amount. Multiple attackers = multiple triggers. (3) Gideon's Sacrifice turns your own lethal swing into your death — never alpha strike into open {W} mana without artifact/enchantment removal first. (4) Neyith draws cards off fights AND gains reach from Fiendlash, blocking flyers. **Critical rule:** Count Fiendlash triggers before committing to combat. Each separate damage source = one trigger at full power. 4 attackers into Gideon's Sacrifice = 4 × power damage. Strip the equipment or don't attack. Sokka's menace is the key advantage — forces 2 blockers, preventing single-creature reflection blocks. But Sokka is fragile at 3/3 and gets taxed out quickly.
