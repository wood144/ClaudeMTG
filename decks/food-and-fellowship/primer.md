# Food and Fellowship — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Food and Fellowship | Colors: White/Black/Green (Abzan)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- Abzan midrange/value deck with removal suite including Path to Exile, Mortify, and potentially Beast Within / Anguished Unmaking.
- Food token generation for life gain and card advantage.

## Key Rulings
- **Mortify can't hit artifacts** — need dedicated artifact removal for mana doublers like Extraplanar Lens.
- **Path to Exile** shuffles the creature into the library — useful for disrupting graveyard-based combos but gives opponent a land.

## Game History

### Game 6: Food and Fellowship (Claude) vs. Fblthp (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T11. Enter the Infinite → Jace, Wielder of Mysteries +1 for the win.

**What happened:**
- Opponent ramped fast: Extraplanar Lens (exile Island) + Gauntlet of Power (blue) = each Island produces {U}{U}{U}. 4 Islands = 12 mana by T9.
- T9: Enter the Infinite for 12 mana. Drew entire library, put Island on top, passed (tapped out).
- T11: Opponent drew Island. Cast Thassa's Oracle → I responded with Path to Exile on Fblthp (shuffled into library, making library=1) + Mortify on Oracle (destroyed, removing blue devotion). Oracle ETB resolved: devotion 0 < library 1. **Combo stopped!**
- But then: Jace Wielder of Mysteries for {1}{U}{U}{U} (had 12+ mana). Jace +1 mills Fblthp, draws from empty library → Jace static wins.

**Key lessons:**
- **Against Fblthp, need planeswalker removal** (Beast Within, Anguished Unmaking, Generous Gift) not just creature/enchantment removal. Path + Mortify can stop Oracle but not Jace.
- **Must destroy mana doublers early.** Extraplanar Lens + Gauntlet of Power in mono-color = explosive mana.
- **Hidden information violation:** Evaluated opponent's Extraplanar Lens out loud, revealing hand check. Must stop doing this.

**No summoning sickness or major rules mistakes this game.**

### Game 52: Food and Fellowship/Frodo+Sam (Human) vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T12 (12 sides) via lethal combat damage. Human 50, Claude -4. Dominance 3.

**Key plays:**
- T4: Sam deployed. Food engine online immediately.
- T8: Pippin → Merry chain. Merry makes Soldiers off artifact ETBs. Board width exploding.
- T10: Fell the Mighty targeting Sam (power 2) — surgical wipe cleared 7-power Zhulodok while preserving entire board.
- T12: Motivated Pony (haste) + Food trigger = +3/+3 anthem to all attackers. 7 creatures swung for 32 lethal against 28 life.

**Key observations:**
- Food engine (Sam) + Merry (Soldiers off artifacts) + Frodo (card draw when 3+ life gained) = snowballing advantage every combat.
- Motivated Pony is the closer — haste + guaranteed +3/+3 with Sam's Food.
- Pippin's 4-Food activation (+3/+3 haste to others) was one turn away from being online — would have been +6/+6 with Pony.
- Ring tempted 3 times by T12: Frodo was unblockable by power >1, looting on attack, and forcing sacs on damage.

### Game 67: Food and Fellowship/Frodo+Sam (Claude) vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T19 (19 sides) via commander damage (Zinnia 23 CDMG). Human 19, Claude 29. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**
- T9: Shire Shirriff sacced Food to exile Zinnia (first removal). Zinnia to CZ.
- T11: Path to Exile on Zinnia (second removal). Zinnia to CZ with +4 tax.
- T13: Feasting Hobbit devoured 3 Foods → 11/11 unblockable. Bombadil's Song saved it from Shield Broker steal (hexproof). Hobbit's Sting killed Shield Broker during combat to save Sam from triple-block lethal.
- T15: Feasting Hobbit swung for 11 (opponent 25→14). But Rapid Hybridization topdeck destroyed Hobbit, replacing with 3/3 Frog Lizard. Clock gone.
- T18: Sun Titan with offspring rebuilt opponent's board (recurred Circuit Mender + Thopter Engineer). Zinnia climbed back to 6/3 flying.
- T19: Selfless Spirit with offspring + Sun Titan recurrence loop = permanent indestructible. Zinnia at 7/3 = CDMG lethal.

**Key lessons:**
- **Feasting Hobbit is the finisher** but needs protection. Bombadil's Song saved it once but {U} instant removal is always lurking.
- **CDMG is a real threat from Zinnia.** Flying + growing power from base-1 creatures. Need flying/reach blockers or reliable removal.
- **Merry stolen by Shield Broker** crippled the Soldier engine. Getting Merry back (damage to remove shield counter) should be priority.
- **Sam's Food engine** is strong but slow without Merry for Soldier generation.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Zinnia Precon (0-1):** Zinnia's flying CDMG is the main threat — must hold instant removal for her. Offspring doubles everything; killing Thopter Engineer removes haste from artifact tokens. Shield Broker stealing Merry shuts down the Soldier engine — prioritize removing the shield counter (any damage source works). Sun Titan + Selfless Spirit is a near-unbeatable loop — need enchantment/artifact removal or exile effects. Feasting Hobbit with 3+ Foods is the best clock but needs instant-speed protection.
- **vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (1-0):** Eldrazi needs Zhulodok online to function — without cascade the hand is full of uncastable 7+ MV spells. Fell the Mighty is perfect since our creatures are low-power. Go wide fast, Food engine keeps life high and Frodo drawing. Motivated Pony closes. Don't let them reach cascade turns — pressure early and hold Fell the Mighty / removal for Zhulodok.
- **vs. Fblthp (blue combo):** Fblthp has 3 library removers (Enter the Infinite, Leveler, Mirror of Fate) and 3 empty-library wins (Thassa's Oracle, Laboratory Maniac, Jace Wielder of Mysteries) + backup Triskaidekaphile. Answering one win condition isn't enough — they hold all three post-Enter the Infinite. Need planeswalker removal and artifact removal for mana doublers. Proper Fblthp play is Teferi/Tidal Barracuda BEFORE combo to cut instant-speed interaction.
