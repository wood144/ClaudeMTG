# Zinnia Precon — Deck Primer
Commander: Zinnia, Valley's Voice | Colors: Blue/Red (Izzet)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Inferno Titan pump** is a regular activated ability (uses the stack), not a mana ability — activate during declare attackers step while attack trigger is on stack.
- **Sun Titan ETB** can only return PERMANENT cards (creatures, artifacts, enchantments, lands) — NOT instants or sorceries like Path to Exile.
- **Bident of Thassa** draws at end of COMBAT, not end of turn — use the Main 2 window to play lands/spells before discarding.

## Key Rulings
- **Shield counter removal mid-combat:** When a creature with a shield counter is dealt damage, the counter is removed instead — control effect ends immediately, even mid-combat.
- **When stealing a creature, equipment stays attached but is still controlled by original owner** — they can re-equip it to their own creatures.

### Game 33: Zinnia Precon (Human) vs. Every Shrine Ever (Claude)
**Result:** Human concedes T16. Farewell wiped the Martial Coup token army, and Honden engine (3 draws + 3 forced discards/upkeep) was unrecoverable.

**Key moments:**
- **Martial Coup X=8** was the right play — wiped Claude's board and created 8 Soldiers. But enchantment-based engines (Hondens) survived.
- **Arthur + Shield Broker** combo was effective — Arthur's attack trigger finding Shield Broker to steal Go-Shintai of Hidden Cruelty was a strong tempo swing.
- **Curiosity Crafter + offspring tokens** generated good card draw in the mid-game.
- **Lost to enchantment resilience** — creature-based wipes don't answer Shrine enchantments.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever:** Shrine deck's strength is enchantment-based engines that survive creature wipes. Martial Coup/board wipes don't touch Hondens. Need enchantment removal (Heliod's Intervention, Austere Command choosing enchantments) to shut down the Honden draw/discard engine. Shield Broker stealing enchantment creatures is strong but Hull Breach breaks it. Prioritize killing Honden of Seeing Winds — without the draw engine, shrines are slow to rebuild.
- **vs. Boros Equipment:** Equip abilities are sorcery-speed — opponents cannot equip in response to your spells. Bruenor Battlehammer gives +2/+0 per equipment attached to HIM (not just any creature), plus free equip once per turn for controller.
