# Zinnia Precon — Deck Primer

> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Zinnia, Valley's Voice | Colors: Blue/Red (Izzet)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Inferno Titan pump** is a regular activated ability (uses the stack), not a mana ability — activate during declare attackers step while attack trigger is on stack.
- **Sun Titan ETB** can only return PERMANENT cards (creatures, artifacts, enchantments, lands) — NOT instants or sorceries like Path to Exile.
- **Bident of Thassa** draws at end of COMBAT, not end of turn — use the Main 2 window to play lands/spells before discarding.
- **Primary win path — Zinnia flying CDMG:** Zinnia gains +1/+1, vigilance and the Bird type from Murmuration, plus +X/+0 from every base-power-1 token; offspring on each creature doubles every deploy's buff. Most opponents have no flying blockers, so the 21 commander-damage clock closes games.
- **Sun Titan + Selfless Spirit** is the best engine: Sun Titan recurs Selfless Spirit each turn for repeatable indestructible attacks.
- **Devilish Valet + wide go** (offspring tokens / Martial Coup / Bident creature dumps): each token entry is an Alliance trigger that doubles Valet's power — 4 triggers = ×16. Exponentially lethal alpha.
- **Murmuration + Bident of Thassa:** every spell makes a token (Murmuration) and every connect draws cards (Bident) — self-sustaining once both online; opp needs enchantment removal to break it.
- **Arthur attack trigger** cheats a creature in tapped + attacking. Best with Skyclave Apparition (ETB exiles a ≤4-MV permanent, returns to hand EOC for a repeat) or Shield Broker (steal an engine piece).
- **Zinnia is cheap to recast** (7+ mana sources by mid-game absorb the tax) — but never redeploy her into a board-anchored repeatable removal that's always in her toughness range (e.g. Hidden Cruelty) without instant-speed protection.

## Key Rulings
- **Shield counter removal mid-combat:** When a creature with a shield counter is dealt damage, the counter is removed instead — control effect ends immediately, even mid-combat.
- **When stealing a creature, equipment stays attached but is still controlled by original owner** — they can re-equip it to their own creatures.
- **Offspring on legendary creatures is wasted** — the legend rule kills the token immediately. Cast legends (Arthur, etc.) without paying offspring.
- **Skyclave Apparition vs a commander forces a choice:** exile it (opp gets a 3/3 Illusion) or redirect to CZ (+2 tax) — either way the commander leaves the board mid-combat.

### Game 68: Zinnia Precon (Claude) vs. Every Shrine Ever (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T9 cycle (17 sides) by concession. Claude 10, Human 34. CDMG 3. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**

- **Starnheim Courser reducing shrine costs** accelerated the engine by 1-2 turns. Early removal on Courser would have bought time.

### Game 93: Zinnia Precon (Human) vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T23 (22 sides) via lethal alpha. Human life 21, Claude life 27 (conceded into 120-power lethal alpha). Dominance 2.

**Loss path for Claude:**
- Cruelclaw connected ONCE (T14, Boots-equipped, unblocked into empty board post-Damnation) → flipped Dread Defiler from top. DD activation drained 4 + 6 swing = 10 face dmg in one turn. This was Claude's only Cruelclaw connect.
- Plague Wind T18 (one-sided wipe) cleared Zinnia + 3 Storm Crows + CC. But Murmuration + Arcane Signet survived and opp rebuilt in 1 turn via Aether Channeler (bouncing DD!) + Junk Winder + Stolen by the Fae (X=7 → 7 Faerie tokens).
- T19 Stolen by the Fae bounced DD + 7 Faerie tokens was the backbreaker — Claude lost his only flying defender AND opp got a wide token army in one card.
- T21: Zinnia recast (Murmuration buff = 2/4 flying vigilance, +X buff from tokens). Bident of Thassa cast same turn → 5 attackers connect → opp draws 5 cards. Massive refill.
- Claude's Chaotic Transformation T22 swapped Murmuration/Bident/Junk Winder for Echoing Assault/Ornithopter/Inspiring Overseer — Echoing Assault was a brutal replacement (attack-token copy + token menace).
- T23: opp cast Devilish Valet + offspring + Martial Coup X=4 → 4 Soldier tokens → DV doubled 4x → 32 power. Lethal alpha incoming was 120+ damage, 0 mana / 0 instants / 0 blockers for Claude.

**Key plays:**
- **Bident of Thassa T17** — combined with Murmuration's "spell-cast tokens" trigger to refill repeatedly. Single most-impactful card.
- **Stolen by the Fae T19** — perfect timing: bounced Cruelclaw deck's only big body AND filled board with 7 flying tokens to power Zinnia's +X.
- **Devilish Valet + offspring + Martial Coup combo** — exponential power scaling. Token-entry-triggered Alliance turned 1 power into 32+ in one turn.

**Lessons:**
- **Murmuration + Bident of Thassa is a hard combo** — every spell makes a token (Murmuration) AND every connect draws cards (Bident). Once both online, opp self-sustains indefinitely. Need a Reclamation Sage or equivalent to break one.
- **Stolen by the Fae is the deck's tempo blowout card** — it's a 1-card swing that bounces a creature AND drops X tokens. Especially devastating vs decks like The Claw that rely on a single key creature with evasion equipment.
- **Echoing Assault matchup notes** — it's a token menace + attack-copy enchantment. Hard to answer without enchantment removal.
- **Devilish Valet doubles on Alliance triggers** — Martial Coup X=4 was 4 separate triggers = power × 16. Any wide-go combo (offspring tokens, Martial Coup, Bident-fueled creature dumps) makes DV exponentially lethal.

### Game 103: Zinnia Precon (Claude) vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T10 (20 sides) via concession. Claude life 39, Human life 23. CDMG 2. Dominance 3.

**Key plays:**

**Key lessons:**
- **Arthur + Skyclave Apparition is a high-EV cheat line.** Arthur trigger drops Apparition tapped+attacking, Apparition ETB exiles ≤4 MV permanent. Returns to hand EOC. Recast Apparition next turn for second exile. One Apparition = 2 exiles in 3 turns.
- **Skyclave Apparition + commander = forced choice for opp.** Either lose commander to exile permanently (and give me +3/3 Illusion penalty) or redirect to CZ (+2 tax). Either way, commander off board mid-combat.
- **Don't pay offspring on legendary creatures.** Legend rule kills the token immediately. Wasted 2 mana. Cast Arthur for {2}{U}{R}{W} not {4}{U}{R}{W}.
- **Mana sequencing matters with Sol Ring.** Casting Apparition T10 using Sol Ring for the 1 generic burned a floating mana I needed for Rowdy Research M2. Should have used Mountain (R as generic) for Apparition and saved Sol Ring for M2.

### Game 90: Zinnia Precon (Claude) vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (Human)
**Result:** Claude concedes T20 (sides 20). Claude life 14, Human life 35. CDMG 5. Dominance 2.

**Loss path:**
- Mana-screwed early (2 lands T6, no land draws T8). Fortune Teller's Talent T8 was the only play.
- Kambal T7 + Warleader's Call T9 + Mr. House T9 hard-locked the offspring plan. Each of my tokens = -2 life and a tapped copy for human.
- Time Wipe T14 was a 4-card swing (Kambal+Mr. House+Ancient Gold Dragon all wiped). Bought 4 turns of recovery.
- T17 Impact Tremors stacked on top of Warleader's Call → 2x life loss per opp creature ETB. T19 Earth-Cult Elemental rolled nat 20 → forced 2 sacs (lost both Spirit tokens, dropping Zinnia from 5/3 to 1/3) + Mr. House Robot trigger from the d20.
- T19 attack: HO+LR+Robot, 2 more Robots from attack triggers, -8 life including blocks. 14 life going into T20.

**Lessons:**
- **Kambal+Warleader's Call+Impact Tremors triple-threat:** Every token made is -2 to -3 life. Refuse to pay offspring or play token-makers until the lock is broken. Aether Channeler bounce (no offspring, choose bounce mode) is the cheapest answer.
- **Color screw on UU effects:** Pull from Tomorrow needs 2 U sources. Glacial Fortress + Island (or basics) are both required. Without Evolving Wilds → Island, Pull is uncastable for the entire game.
- **Don't grind lost positions:** When 14 life vs 8-attacker alpha is unanswerable, concede. Saved tokens for blocking didn't matter — Zinnia clock was 4+ turns away from lethal CDMG, opp kills me in 1.
- **HE+offspring needs Tetsuko first:** I cast HE+offspring T18 without Tetsuko, so the 4 base-power-1 creatures became chumps instead of unblockable threats. Tetsuko before HE+offspring would have made the swarm unblockable AND grown Zinnia.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever (0-2):** Enchantment engines (Hondens, Ancient Wars) survive creature wipes — Martial Coup/board wipes don't touch them. MUST find enchantment removal (Heliod's Intervention, Austere Command choosing enchantments) or lose to trigger damage; mulligan aggressively for it. Hidden Cruelty hard-counters Zinnia (3 toughness, always in range) — never recast Zinnia into active Cruelty without instant-speed protection. Shield Broker steal is temporary: Honden of Infinite Rage pops the shield counter (clean 2-for-1). Ancient Wars + Honden = 12+ dmg/turn once online. Kill Starnheim Courser early to slow shrine deployment. Prioritize Honden of Seeing Winds (their draw engine).
- **vs. Boros Equipment:** Equip is sorcery-speed — opp can't equip in response to your spells. Bruenor Battlehammer gives +2/+0 per equipment attached to HIM (not any creature) plus one free equip/turn. Don't discard interaction early (e.g. Aetherize vs a lifelink swing).
- **vs. Avatar Allies/Sokka (1-0):** Murmuration is the key card — Sokka goes wide on the ground with Ally tokens but has almost no flying interaction. Inferno Titan's 3-damage trigger perfectly kills Sokka (3/3 base). Selfless Spirit + offspring = two indestructible activations to protect the token army. Zinnia's Bird type means Murmuration buffs her too. Their weakness is enchantment removal — they can't answer Murmuration once it lands.
- **vs. Food and Fellowship/Frodo+Sam (1-0):** No flying/reach blockers — Zinnia's CDMG clock is almost unstoppable. Shield Broker stealing Merry shuts down their Soldier engine. Feasting Hobbit with devoured Foods is their best threat — hold instant removal (Rapid Hybridization, bounce). Sun Titan + Selfless Spirit loop closes. Offspring engine rebuilds fast through multiple Zinnia removals.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (1-1):** When they stay pure ground+robots, Zinnia flies over for free CDMG. But Kambal + Warleader's Call + Impact Tremors HARD-COUNTERS the offspring/token plan — every token = a free tapped body for them AND -2 to -3 life to you per ETB. Don't pay offspring or play token-makers while ANY of {Kambal, Warleader's Call, Impact Tremors} is on board. Bounce/wipe Kambal immediately (he's not their commander — goes to hand, recast at base mana); Aether Channeler bounce (no offspring) is the cheapest answer. Time Wipe is a strict swing card here. Earth-Cult Elemental on a high d20 = blowout (forced sacs + drains).
- **vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (1-0):** Evasion package (Boots, menace) answered by stacking flying blockers — Faeries + Storm Crows wall menace via mass chump-blocks; double-block menace every turn and don't let Boots make Cruelclaw unblockable. Their only out is connecting once to flip a top-deck threat (e.g. Dread Defiler). Murmuration + Bident of Thassa self-sustains (token per spell + draw per connect) — they have no enchantment removal that doesn't also hit Murmuration. Stolen by the Fae is the tempo blowout (bounce their big body + drop X Faerie tokens). Devilish Valet + Martial Coup / Echoing Assault = exponential lethal alpha.
- **vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (1-0):** Zero flying defense — Zinnia + Skyclave Apparition + Arthur all fly/cheat past their ground board. Arthur + Apparition answers Chatterfang (MV 3) AND Nuka-Cola (MV 3) — both engine pieces in range; hold Apparition for the highest-MV ≤4 target. Without black mana their {B} Chatterfang sac removal is dead. Race the air clock before opp assembles Vito/Exquisite Blood (~T15+).
