# Zinnia Precon — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Zinnia, Valley's Voice | Colors: Blue/Red (Izzet)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Inferno Titan pump** is a regular activated ability (uses the stack), not a mana ability — activate during declare attackers step while attack trigger is on stack.
- **Sun Titan ETB** can only return PERMANENT cards (creatures, artifacts, enchantments, lands) — NOT instants or sorceries like Path to Exile.
- **Bident of Thassa** draws at end of COMBAT, not end of turn — use the Main 2 window to play lands/spells before discarding.

## Key Rulings
- **Shield counter removal mid-combat:** When a creature with a shield counter is dealt damage, the counter is removed instead — control effect ends immediately, even mid-combat.
- **When stealing a creature, equipment stays attached but is still controlled by original owner** — they can re-equip it to their own creatures.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever:** Shrine deck's strength is enchantment-based engines that survive creature wipes. Martial Coup/board wipes don't touch Hondens. Need enchantment removal (Heliod's Intervention, Austere Command choosing enchantments) to shut down the Honden draw/discard engine. Shield Broker stealing enchantment creatures is strong but Hull Breach breaks it. Prioritize killing Honden of Seeing Winds — without the draw engine, shrines are slow to rebuild.
- **vs. Boros Equipment:** Equip abilities are sorcery-speed — opponents cannot equip in response to your spells. Bruenor Battlehammer gives +2/+0 per equipment attached to HIM (not just any creature), plus free equip once per turn for controller.
- **vs. Avatar Allies/Sokka (1-0):** Murmuration is the key card — Sokka's deck wants to go wide on the ground with Ally tokens, but has almost no flying interaction. Inferno Titan's 3-damage trigger perfectly kills Sokka (3/3 base). Selfless Spirit + offspring gives two indestructible activations to protect the token army. Zinnia's Bird type means Murmuration buffs her too. The deck's weakness is enchantment removal — they can't easily answer Murmuration once it's on board.

### Game 68: Zinnia Precon (Claude) vs. Every Shrine Ever (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T9 cycle (17 sides) by concession. Claude 10, Human 34. CDMG 3. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**
- Shield Broker T13 stole Hidden Cruelty — temporarily stopped the removal engine and picked off a Shrine Token.
- Honden of Infinite Rage targeting the shield counter on Hidden Cruelty was the key play — reclaimed the removal engine cleanly.
- Ancient Wars (6/turn end step) + Honden (6/turn upkeep) = 12 damage per cycle, lethal in 2 turns.
- Zinnia killed twice by Hidden Cruelty before she could attack. Commander tax spiraled to +{4}.
- No enchantment removal drawn all game. Fatal against shrine engines.

**Lessons:**
- **Hidden Cruelty is the #1 threat.** It kills Zinnia every time she enters. Must hold instant-speed protection or never deploy Zinnia while Cruelty is active.
- **Shield Broker steal is vulnerable to damage-based removal.** Honden of Infinite Rage targeting the shielded creature is a clean 2-for-1. Shield Broker is better when opponent lacks direct damage triggers.
- **Mulligan aggressively for enchantment interaction** vs shrine decks. Without it, the engine snowballs unchecked.
- **Starnheim Courser reducing shrine costs** accelerated the engine by 1-2 turns. Early removal on Courser would have bought time.

## Matchup Notes Updates
- **vs. Every Shrine Ever (0-2):** Hidden Cruelty hard-counters Zinnia (3 toughness, always in range). Shield Broker steal is temporary — Honden of Infinite Rage pops the shield counter. Must find enchantment removal (Heliod's Intervention, Austere Command) or lose to trigger damage. Ancient Wars + Honden = 12+ damage/turn once online. Kill Starnheim Courser early to slow shrine deployment. Don't recast Zinnia into active Hidden Cruelty without protection.
- **vs. Food and Fellowship (1-0):** Food deck's weakness is flying — no flying/reach blockers means Zinnia's CDMG clock is almost unstoppable. Shield Broker stealing Merry shuts down their Soldier engine. Feasting Hobbit with devoured Foods is their best threat — hold instant removal (Rapid Hybridization, bounce). Sun Titan + Selfless Spirit loop closes games. Even through multiple Zinnia removals, the offspring engine rebuilds fast.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (1-0):** Dicebots has no flying defense — Zinnia flies over Robots freely for CDMG. Devilish Valet + offspring creatures is the kill engine. Jacked Rabbit + Valet is a combo finish (6 Rabbit tokens = 64x Valet power). Even if opponent removes Zinnia, recast with tax is affordable. Mr. House generates Robots but they're ground-only blockers. Keep deploying offspring creatures to feed Valet and Zinnia's +X/+0.
