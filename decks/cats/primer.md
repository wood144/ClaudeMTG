# Cats / Mirri — Deck Primer
Commander: Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist | Colors: Green/White (Selesnya)

## Strategy Overview
GW Cat tribal go-wide/go-tall deck. Mirri provides combat control — limits opponent blocking to 1 creature when attacking, and limits opponent attacks to 1 creature while Mirri is tapped. The deck uses Cat lords, +1/+1 counter synergies (especially Felidar Retreat), and efficient Cat creatures to overwhelm opponents.

## Key Synergies
- **Felidar Retreat + any land drop**: Either creates a 2/2 Cat Beast OR puts +1/+1 counters on all creatures + vigilance. Counter mode snowballs fast with multiple creatures.
- **Skyknight Squire + ETB creatures**: Grows with every creature entering. At 3+ counters gains flying — becomes a massive evasive threat.
- **Sovereign Okinec Ahau + countered-up creatures**: On attack, adds counters equal to the difference between current power and base power. Doubles existing counters effectively.
- **Feline Sovereign + combat damage**: Destroys target artifact/enchantment whenever Cats deal combat damage. Lord effect (+1/+1 to Cats) is also strong.
- **Leonin Warleader**: Creates 2 lifelink Cat tokens on attack, each triggering Skyknight Squire.
- **Mirri + vigilance**: Vigilance from Retreat lets Mirri attack (restricting opponent blocks) while staying tapped to restrict opponent attacks on their turn.

## Key Cards
- **Mirri** (3/2 first strike): Combat lockdown commander
- **Felidar Retreat**: Counter/token engine off landfall
- **Skyknight Squire**: Snowball flyer
- **Sovereign Okinec Ahau**: Counter doubler on attack
- **Feline Sovereign**: Lord + artifact/enchantment removal
- **Trouble in Pairs**: Card advantage vs opponents who double-spell, multi-attack, or extra-draw
- **For the Ancestors**: Instant-speed dig for Cats, with flashback

## Matchup Notes

### vs. Squirrel Food / Chatterfang (Game 16)
- **Result: Cats WIN (T15, combat damage)**
- Felidar Retreat + Skyknight Squire was the game. Skyknight grew to 10/10 flying.
- Path to Exile on Chatterfang mid-combat removed the forestwalk threat and Vraska draw engine.
- Feline Sovereign destroyed Vraska Joins Up on combat damage before being killed by Chatterfang's sac ability.
- Trouble in Pairs taxed Claude's double-draws and double-spells effectively.
- Chatterfang's forestwalk was the main threat — opponent's Radiant Grove being a Forest enabled unblockable attacks.

## Game History

### Game 16 (piloted by Human): Cats/Mirri vs. Squirrel Food/Chatterfang (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T15 via lethal combat damage.
- T5: Mirri deployed. Early Skyknight Squire started accumulating counters.
- T9: Felidar Retreat online. Double landfall from Cultivate + land drop pumped entire board.
- T11: Trouble in Pairs deployed, taxing Claude's card draw and spell output.
- T13: Feline Sovereign + Jewel Thief deployed. Massive counter-fueled attack. Path to Exile removed Chatterfang mid-combat.
- T14: Claude recast Chatterfang, deployed Vito, killed Feline Sovereign with sac ability, but fell behind on board.
- T15: For the Ancestors refilled hand. Sovereign Okinec Ahau + Forest landfall pushed Skyknight to 10/10 flying. Lethal swing.
