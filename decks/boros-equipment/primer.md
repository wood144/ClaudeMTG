> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Kellan, the Fae-Blooded // Birthright Boon | Colors: Red/White (Boros)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Kellan, the Fae-Blooded ({2}{R}):** 2/2 double strike. Other creatures get +1/+0 per Aura/Equipment on Kellan. Adventure ({1}{W}) tutors an Aura or Equipment.
- **Mantle of the Ancients ({3}{W}{W}):** Mass recursion — returns ALL Auras and Equipment from GY attached to enchanted creature. +1/+1 per attachment. Win condition with a loaded graveyard.
- **Puresteel Paladin ({W}{W}):** Draw on Equipment ETB. Metalcraft: equip costs {0}. Card advantage engine.
- **Colossus Hammer ({1}):** +10/+10, loses flying. Equip {8} normally but trivial with Puresteel Metalcraft or Sigarda's Aid.
- **Gingerbrute ({1}):** Haste, {1}: can't be blocked except by haste creatures. Excellent voltron target.
- **Sword of Once and Future / Sword of Forge and Frontier:** Protection swords with on-damage value.
- **Kaldra Compleat ({7}):** Living Weapon, +5/+5, first strike, trample, indestructible, haste, exile on combat damage.
- **Cloudsteel Kirin ({2}{W}):** Reconfigure equipment — grants flying and "you can't lose the game." Hard answer to combo/mill-out — DEPLOY AND RECONFIGURE onto a pro-blue body the turn opp stabilizes; do NOT hold it. As a creature (not reconfigured) it does NOT grant "can't lose."
- **Blackblade Reforged ({2}):** +1/+1 per land. Scales into mid/late game. Sword of the Animist + Blackblade = each attack ramps AND buffs it.
- **Wrecking Ball Arm + Blacksmith's Talent (L2):** Proven Kellan finisher — {2} WBA + {3} legendary equip sets Kellan to 7/7 (Sword's +1/+1 stacks → 8/8), double strike, unblockable by ≤2 power = ~16–22 CDMG in one turn. Confirmed across multiple games.
- **Haystack ({1}{W}):** Phasing protection — phase Kellan/key creature out in response to targeted removal (StP, Murder, Stasis Field, Boomerang). Recast cheaply after bounce. Locks out single-target interaction.
- **Haunted Cloak ({3}):** Haste enabler — Kellan attacks the turn he's cast, before sorcery-speed auras/removal can answer him. Unsung MVP.
- **Vorrac Battlehorns ({2}):** "Can't be blocked by more than one" + Kellan menace = unblockable (rules contradiction). Breaks multi-block kill plans.
- **Foundry Inspector + Bruenor Battlehammer:** Best non-commander engine — cost discount + free equips = overwhelming board in 1–2 turns.
- Equipment-heavy strategy with lifelink potential via Loxodon Warhammer (lifelink swings win the life race outright).

## Core Gameplan — Ramp is Everything
- Boros has almost no natural ramp. **Sword of the Animist is the #1 priority** — it fixes the deck's biggest weakness by fetching a basic land on every attack.
- **Turn 1-2 play pattern:** Cast Birthright Boon (Kellan's adventure, {1}{W}) to tutor Sword of the Animist if it's not already in hand. This should be the default line in almost every game.
- Attach Sword to any cheap creature (Gingerbrute, Ornithopter) and start attacking ASAP to accumulate lands. Every swing is a free ramp spell.
- Without Sword of the Animist or early mana rocks (Boros Signet, Sol Ring), the deck falls behind fast. Mulligan decisions should weight early ramp access heavily.

## Key Rulings
- **Equip abilities are sorcery-speed** — opponents cannot equip in response to your spells.
- **When stealing a creature, equipment stays attached but is still controlled by original owner** — they can re-equip it to their own creatures.
- **Mantle of the Ancients + Living Weapon:** Equipment returned by Mantle enters attached, but Living Weapon ETB triggers create Germs and move the equipment to them. Nettlecyst and Kaldra Compleat will leave the Mantle creature.
- **Cloudsteel Kirin in GY:** It's typed "Artifact Creature — Equipment" so Mantle DOES return it.
- **Puresteel Paladin + Mantle:** All equipment enters simultaneously, triggering Puresteel once per equipment. Massive draw.
- **Bruenor Battlehammer:** +2/+0 per equipment on EACH creature you control (not just him). Free equip once per turn.
- **Equipment persists when Kellan dies** — board wipes and creature removal leave equipment on the battlefield unattached. Re-equip Kellan (legendary equip is cheap) or pivot to a backup voltron (Danitha Capashen) next turn.
- **Vorrac Battlehorns + menace = unblockable:** "can't be blocked by more than one" and Kellan's menace ("must be blocked by 2+") contradict → Kellan can't be blocked at all.
- **Kellan is a fragile 2/2** — vulnerable to X-damage sweepers that spare 3+ toughness (Starstorm). The deck has no double-strike clock without him, so consider holding him if opp has mana open for a sweeper; don't lose him cheaply.
- **Protection from blue (Sword of Once and Future) does NOT stop non-targeted effects** — overloaded Cyclonic Rift and mass-bounce (Cresting Mosasaurus) go through. Also useless if Kellan is bounced before the equip resolves (protection not yet granted). Pace equipment incrementally to avoid exposing big casts to a single counter.

**Result:** Human wins T20 (sides 20) via combo. Final life: Human 5, Claude 40. Dominance 2 (comeback — Human stabilized from 5 under repeated lethal).

**Key plays (Claude/Boros):**

Anim trigger fires on **declare attackers = uncounterable**, so attacking BEFORE equipping locked in value even against open bounce mana. Good discipline.
- Built a 7/7 unblockable hexproof Mabel (Cragflame + Brotherhood legendary-equip {1} + Sword token + Champion's Helm) + flying Foundry (Maul). Got Human to 5.
- **Sword of Once and Future on Foundry = 8/7 flying, protection from blue/black → unblockable AND un-bounceable by mono-U.** This is the single best card vs Fblthp's bounce shell. Their Snap/Unsummon/Disperse/Boomerang all bounced off it.

**How Claude lost:**
- Human chained bounce/tempo to fizzle every lethal attack: Snap, Boomerang (strip hexproof), Unsummon-through-ward, then **Cresting Mosasaurus flashed via Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir** (their creatures gain flash) — a *non-targeted* mass bounce that bypasses protection-from-blue and removed the pro-blue Foundry from combat.
- **The Endstone** (life becomes 20 at their end step) reset Human to 20 every turn → "getting them low" stopped mattering; needed **20 damage in a single turn**.
- Endgame: The Endstone draw-engine + ~30 mana off Extraplanar Lens/Gauntlet of Power → Omniscience → Enter the Infinite = all win-cons in hand. No interaction available.

**Lessons (Boros pilot) — critical:**
- **Cloudsteel Kirin ("equipped creature: opponents can't win the game") is the hard answer to Fblthp's combo. DEPLOY AND RECONFIGURE IT ONTO A PRO-BLUE CREATURE the turn you see them stabilizing (The Endstone / Teferi / mass card draw) — do NOT hold it.** Drew it T19, kept it in hand to rebuild Foundry instead, and lost to the combo a turn later. On a pro-blue (Sword of Once and Future) body, only a mass bounce (Mosasaurus/Rift) removes the lock, and those are one-shots — re-establish each time.
- **Protection from blue does NOT stop non-targeted effects: Cresting Mosasaurus mass-bounce and overloaded Cyclonic Rift go through.** Don't treat a pro-blue creature as Rift/Mosasaurus-proof.
- **Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir gives THEIR creatures flash** — expect flashed Mosasaurus/blockers/bouncers on your combat. Kill/avoid Teferi or play around the flash window.
- **vs The Endstone: race math changes to "20 in one turn."** Don't durdle chipping them down — build one alpha that does 20 through blockers, or win via the can't-win lock + mill/deck-out, not life.
- This is fundamentally a race you must win by ~T11-13, before they assemble Endstone/Omniscience/ETI. Once they have 8+ mana sources and card draw online, only the Cloudsteel Kirin lock keeps you alive.

- **Stack equipment incrementally — don't tap out for everything at once.** Sword T3, Fiendlash T5, Cloak T15, Warhammer T17, Battlegear T21 — paced over the game without exposing mana to a single counter spell. Sokka, Swordmaster got countered T11 because it was a big cast — equipment is cheaper and avoids single-spell counter targets.

- **Lifelink (Warhammer) wins the life race.** Opp at 40, Kellan attacks for 16 lifelink = +16 life = 56 by T19. Claude can't out-race even if they find Wrexial.
- **Vulshok Battlegear is the closer.** Cheap ({3}), no requirement, +3/+3 stacks with everything. Once Kellan is at 8+ power with trample, +3 more = lethal CDMG single attack.

**Claude piloting errors that helped:**
- Kept 1-land hand on mulligan, then DISCARDED ramp pieces (Jet Medallion T6, Sky Diamond T8) — left Claude mana-screwed for the entire game (3 lands by T12).
- Deployed Ruin Crab T4 and milled equipment into our GY (Mountain, Colossus Hammer, Hero's Blade, Brotherhood Regalia) — fueled Mantle of the Ancients potential. Mill is actively harmful in this matchup.
- Spent Arcane Denial on Sokka (correct) but Cloak T15 had no answer left. Should have held Denial for the bigger threat — Kellan via Cloak haste was the actual wincon trigger.

Push lethal in one turn if you have it; don't grind. T11's 14-damage swing should have been timed with a follow-up answer in hand, not relying on the next turn's draw.
- **vs. Atla matchup priority: kill the Raptor first.** Raptor's discount + ping-Eggs role makes it the linchpin of the engine. STP > Bolt > Path for cheap creature removal. Without Raptor, Atla's Egg engine needs an extra sac outlet (Pyrohemia, Altar) which costs additional mana — slowing the engine by 2-3 turns.
- **Wrecking Ball Arm + Talent L2 is the strongest Kellan finisher line in this deck.** 7/7 DS unblockable by ≤2 power = lethal-in-2 against most boards. Confirmed.

### Game 111 (pilot): Boros Equipment (Claude) vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T6/side 11 via 49-power alpha. Human 40, Claude 0. Dominance 3. **Atla Palani never cast** — deck won entirely off the dino-cheat shell.

**How Claude lost:**
- Mulled to 5 (two 1-land/no-white hands), kept 3-land hand. Clean T4 Birthright Boon → Sword of the Animist (default line). T6 Sram + Diamond Pick-Axe (draw engine online). T8 Sword + Endless Atlas. T10 Sokka + dig.
- **The blowout: opp ramped (Farseek, Cultivate, Hulking Raptor) then T9 Savage Order sac'd Raptor to put Gishath onto the battlefield with haste. Gishath connected for 7 → flip trigger revealed 7 and dumped FOUR free dinos: Wakening Sun's Avatar 7/7, Bonehoard Dracosaur 5/5 flyer, Regisaur Alpha 4/4 (+3/3 token, gives dinos haste), Silverclad Ferocidons 8/5.** From an empty board to ~34 power in one attack.
- T11: Etali, Primal Conqueror ({5}{R}{R}) ETB free-cast Zetalpa (4/8 flying DS). Regisaur haste turned the whole board live → 49-power all-out. Claude at 33, two blockers, 13 flying unblockable + 36 ground. Unsurvivable. Concede.

**Lessons (Boros pilot):**
- **vs Atla deck, the Gishath/Savage Order line is FASTER and more explosive than the Hatcher-cascade line (Game 95).** Savage Order ({2}{G}{G}, sac power-4+) puts Gishath straight onto the battlefield; one unblocked Gishath hit = 4+ free dinos. With Regisaur Alpha among them, everything has haste and swings the SAME turn it lands. The clock is T9–T11, not T13+.
- **This deck has no answer to that board.** No removal, no sweeper, no flyers/reach, no instants — pure sorcery-speed voltron. Once Gishath connects, Boros loses. The only counterplay is to not let it connect or to have already won.
- **Building card engines (Endless Atlas) into a ramp deck that can highroll Gishath is too slow.** Should have weighted T8–T10 toward fastest possible Kellan pressure (deploy Kellan + equip + race) rather than Sram/Atlas value, to threaten lethal before their explosion — even though realistically the highroll still outraces it. Don't durdle vs Atla.
- **Kellan never deployed** — correct not to chump him into the board, but it means Boros put zero pressure on a 40-life opponent across 5 turns. Against a deck that does nothing until it does everything, slow setup = death. Mulligan harder for a fast aggressive curve, or accept Boros is just behind in this matchup and needs the nut draw.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs Mill decks:** Mantle of the Ancients turns mill into a win condition. Hold Mantle and let them fill your GY. Cloudsteel Kirin prevents mill-out loss.
- **vs Dimir (U/B):** Sword of Once and Future grants protection from both their colors. Near-total lockout on the equipped creature.
- Bruenor + equipment + lifelink is a strong clock. Prioritize removing Bruenor or key equipment pieces early (when opposing).
- **vs Rakdos (The Claw!):** Opponent lacks artifact removal. Damnation hurts but doesn't touch equipment. Rebuild with Kellan + Sword and voltron up. Commander damage is the fastest kill — double strike makes it 2-3 swings.
- **vs Fblthp (Mono-U combo, 0-2):** Race to win by ~T11–13 — Kellan's early pressure (double strike + equipment) is the best weapon. Sword of the Animist is critical for ramp; without it, can't rebuild after board wipes. Protection from blue (Sword of Once and Future) is unreliable — overloaded Rift bypasses targeting, mass-bounce (Cresting Mosasaurus) bypasses it, and bounce catches Kellan before equip resolves. **The Endstone resets opp to 20 each end step → chipping stops mattering; you need 20 in ONE turn, or win via the Cloudsteel Kirin "can't win" lock + mill/deck-out, not life.** Cloudsteel Kirin reconfigured onto a pro-blue body is the hard answer — deploy it the turn they stabilize, don't hold it. Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir gives THEIR creatures flash (expect flashed mass-bounce on your combat). Close before Omniscience / One with the Multiverse / Enter the Infinite. Hold Disenchant/Wear for enchantment engines.
- **vs Fun Guys/Ghave (Abzan tokens/combo):** AURA SHARDS is the #1 threat — every creature/token ETB destroys an artifact or enchantment. If Aura Shards resolves with Ghave online, all equipment dies. Hold Disenchant/Deconstruction Hammer for it. Darksteel Mutation shuts down Kellan. Flying goes over saproling tokens. Equipment survives Damnation — rebuild faster. Race the infinite combo (Ghave + Parallel Lives + Ashnod's Altar + Zulaport). Be aggressive.
- **vs Faeries/Tegwyll (1-0):** Vorrac Battlehorns + Kellan menace = unblockable lock. Stack Maul + Infantry Shield + Vorrac on Kellan for the unblockable lethal voltron. Faeries' quad-block kill plan (Tegwyll DT + lord-stack power on Naughty/Snaremaster) dies to Vorrac. Annul / counter risk: hold tempo with cheap equipment (Sword of the Animist, Maul) before committing the big pieces. Equipment persists when Kellan dies — pivot to Danitha Capashen for backup voltron (especially with Maul + Infantry Shield for unblockable mobilize). Lofty Denial only hits one big play — once spent, you can chain equipment freely. Watch for Scion-shroud flash on Tegwyll (counter Scion if possible, else save target removal for Naughty/Messenger instead). Faeries have no answer to lethal unblockable swings — race once Vorrac equips.
- **vs Atla Palani (Naya dino-cheat, 0-2):** Bad matchup — Atla is a RED HERRING; the real deck is a Gishath/Savage Order/Etali "cheat fat dinos into play" shell, and removing Atla does nothing. Two kill lines: (a) Hatcher-cascade — Marauding Raptor + Atla + any Egg generator triggers free flips, so kill the RAPTOR first (StP > Bolt > Path) the turn it lands; (b) Savage Order/Gishath — one unblocked Gishath dumps 4+ free dinos with Regisaur haste = ~34–49 power in a single swing (clock is T9–T11). Boros has no removal/sweeper/flyers/reach/instants to answer that board. Only counterplay: race for lethal before they explode, or don't let Gishath connect. Don't durdle on value engines (Sram/Endless Atlas) — weight toward fastest possible Kellan pressure (WBA + Talent line).
- **vs Dargons/Scion (1-0):** Zero instant interaction; Crux of Fate ({3}{B}{B}) is their only realistic Kellan answer and it's sorcery-speed. Race before T10 — WBA + Talent line lands lethal CDMG by T9.
- **vs Bello precon (Gruul animate):** Bad matchup. Bello animates CMC 4+ enchantments as 4/4 indestructible hasty creatures that draw on hit. Gratuitous Violence doubles ALL creature damage including Warstorm Surge ETB triggers. Pyreswipe Hawk flies, has haste, and steals artifacts on expend 6. StP Bello is the best answer but they recast from CZ easily. Need enchantment removal for GV/Surge — equipment answers don't help. Race in the air before the damage engine comes online.
