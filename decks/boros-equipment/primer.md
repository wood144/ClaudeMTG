# Boros Equipment — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
Commander: Unknown (Boros Equipment theme) | Colors: Red/White (Boros)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Bruenor Battlehammer:** +2/+0 per equipment attached to HIM (not just any creature). Free equip once per turn for controller.
- Equipment-heavy strategy with lifelink potential via equipped creatures.

## Key Rulings
- **Equip abilities are sorcery-speed** — opponents cannot equip in response to your spells.
- **When stealing a creature, equipment stays attached but is still controlled by original owner** — they can re-equip it to their own creatures.

## Game History

### Game 2 (opponent): Boros Equipment vs. Zinnia Precon (Claude)
**Result:** Claude (Zinnia) wins T17. Bruenor's lifelink attacks were a threat but Inferno Titan pump sealed it.

## Matchup Notes
- Bruenor + equipment + lifelink is a strong clock. Prioritize removing Bruenor or key equipment pieces early.
