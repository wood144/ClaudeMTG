# The Claw! / Cruelclaw — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
Commander: The Infamous Cruelclaw | Colors: Black/Red (Rakdos)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves** is the must-answer package. Lightning Greaves gives shroud (can't target) AND haste. Access Tunnel makes it unblockable.
- **Butcher of Malakir** — any board wipe or blocking that kills opponent's creatures triggers forced sacrifice for you. Factor this into combat math — blocking to kill a creature can backfire.
- **Dread Defiler's drain ability has no tap cost** — can activate multiple times per turn at instant speed. With big creatures in opponent's GY, this is a fast clock independent of combat.

## Piloting Guide (from deck owner)
- **Getting Cruelclaw connecting is ALL that matters.** The entire deck revolves around the combat damage trigger.
- **Your hand is discard fuel, not a casting plan.** Don't evaluate opening hands by mana curve — evaluate by: can I get Cruelclaw hitting fast?
- **Mulligan evaluation is simple — can you cast Cruelclaw by T3?**
  - Sol Ring + both colors → auto keep
  - 2 lands + a signet → auto keep
  - 3 lands with both colors → auto keep
  - Bonus: evasion pieces (Access Tunnel, Trailblazer's Boots, Rogues' Passage) make a keepable hand great
- **Everything else in hand is discard fuel.** Creatures are premium discards because they become reanimation targets — if Cruelclaw flips a reanimate spell, it chains into a free creature.
- **Don't try to hardcast expensive cards.** They exist to be discarded for Cruelclaw triggers or cheated in via reanimation.

## How to Beat This Deck
- Priority: destroy Lightning Greaves with artifact removal (Hull Breach) ASAP.
- Be wary of board wipes when Butcher of Malakir is on the battlefield — each creature death triggers sacrifice.
- Don't let Dread Defiler stick with creatures in your graveyard.

## Game History

### Game 4 (opponent): The Claw/Cruelclaw vs. Every Shrine Ever (Claude)
**Result:** Cruelclaw wins ~T15. Trigger engine snowballed out of control.
- Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves came online and couldn't be answered — shroud prevented targeted removal, unblockable prevented chump blocking.

### Game 20 (pilot): The Claw! (Claude) vs. Boros Equipment (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T15 via commander damage. Kellan stacked with Sword of the Animist, Loxodon Warhammer, Chitinous Cloak, and Blackblade Reforged for lethal commander damage in one swing.
**Lessons:**
- Opening hand had no creatures to discard — all expensive noncreature spells. Should have mulliganed for creatures (discard fuel + reanimate targets) and evasion enablers, NOT for castable curve.
- Damnation on T8 was correct — wiped Puresteel Paladin + Anim Pakal engine. But opponent rebuilt faster with Kellan + equipment.
- Cruelclaw's combat damage trigger (free cast by discarding) is strong value but too slow against Boros voltron's explosive equip turns.
- Immersturm Predator was the MVP — flying evasion, graveyard hate, and indestructible option. But couldn't race Kellan's commander damage clock.
- Commander damage reached 18 after just two Kellan hits (6 + 12). Double strike doubles the commander damage pressure.
- Blackblade Reforged scales terrifyingly with Sword of the Animist ramp — each attack fetches a land, making Blackblade bigger.

## Matchup Notes
- The Cruelclaw trigger engine is the primary win condition. Must answer the enablers (Lightning Greaves, Access Tunnel) not the commander itself (which has shroud).
- **vs Boros Equipment:** Kellan's double strike makes commander damage lethal fast. Prioritize artifact/equipment removal over creature removal. Damnation doesn't answer equipment — need cards like Vandalblast, Shatterstorm, or targeted artifact destruction. Trailblazer's Boots is useless if opponent runs mostly basics early.
