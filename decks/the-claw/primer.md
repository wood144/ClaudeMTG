# The Claw! / Cruelclaw — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: The Infamous Cruelclaw | Colors: Black/Red (Rakdos)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **WIN PATH IS DUAL: Cruelclaw connect OR a backup bomb.** Cruelclaw is the nominal engine, but in recorded games it is frequently answered — countered (G106), walled by flyers (G93/G27), tapped down (G27), or locked out (G30). The deck has won anyway on **backup threats**: Grave Titan (G106), the Crabomination → Fiery Emancipation cheat-chain (G94), Dread Defiler drain (G93). Don't read "Cruelclaw didn't connect" as "deck lost."
- **vs counter-heavy control, lead with a bait threat (Grave Titan), NOT Cruelclaw.** Resolve a board-builder first; let Cruelclaw absorb the counter. Put Greaves on the bait body, not the commander (G106).
- **Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves** is the must-answer package. Lightning Greaves gives shroud (can't target) AND haste. Access Tunnel makes it unblockable.
- **MENACE IS NOT UNBLOCKABLE.** Greaves/Boots give hexproof/shroud + haste only — NOT evasion. Wide flying boards (Zinnia, Faeries) wall menace permanently. Real unblockability comes only from Access Tunnel / Rogues' Passage / Whispersilk Cloak. Without one in hand, plan to win on bombs, not the commander.
- **Premium Cruelclaw flips (free cast via discard):** **Dread Defiler** (6/8, no-tap instant-speed drain — strongest flip seen, G93); **Crabomination** in 3+ color matchups (ETB hand-exile steals opp's best engine piece and casts it on your side, G94); **Desolation Twin** (two 10/10s for one discard, G40); **Fiery Emancipation** as a hardcast closer (triples all damage after the cheat-chain builds a board, G94).
- **Sac-based closers / lock-breakers:** **Shadowgrange Archfiend** (ETB sac highest-power — finisher and removal, G40) and **Flare of Malice** (edict on greatest MV — premium vs big single commanders like Wrexial, G106). These are the ONLY way through Pariah's Shield / indestructible-redirect locks where combat triggers are dead (G30).
- **Butcher of Malakir** — any board wipe or blocking that kills opponent's creatures triggers forced sacrifice for you. Factor this into combat math — blocking to kill a creature can backfire.
- **Dread Defiler's drain ability has no tap cost** — can activate multiple times per turn at instant speed. With big creatures in opponent's GY, this is a fast clock independent of combat.

## Piloting Guide (from deck owner)
- **Getting Cruelclaw connecting is ALL that matters.** The entire deck revolves around the combat damage trigger.
- **Your hand is discard fuel, not a casting plan.** Don't evaluate opening hands by mana curve — evaluate by: can I get Cruelclaw hitting fast?
- **Mulligan evaluation is simple — can you cast Cruelclaw by T3?**
  - Sol Ring + both colors → auto keep
  - 2 lands + a signet → auto keep
  - 3 lands with both colors → auto keep
  - Bonus: evasion pieces (Access Tunnel, Trailblazer's Boots, Rogues' Passage) make a keepable hand great
- **Everything else in hand is discard fuel.** Creatures are premium discards because they become reanimation targets — if Cruelclaw flips a reanimate spell, it chains into a free creature.
- **Don't try to hardcast expensive cards.** They exist to be discarded for Cruelclaw triggers or cheated in via reanimation.

## How to Beat This Deck
- Priority: destroy/steal the equipment enablers (Lightning Greaves, Boots) with artifact removal. Pyreswipe Hawk stole Greaves AND destroyed Boots in one turn = disaster for the deck (G76). Vandalblast/Abrade/Hull Breach are live.
- **Counter or kill Cruelclaw is NOT enough** — the deck wins on backup bombs (Grave Titan, Dread Defiler, the Crab/Fiery Emancipation chain). Answer the board, not just the commander.
- **Wall menace with flyers.** Greaves/Boots don't grant evasion; 2+ flying blockers (or any chump wall) shut Cruelclaw off entirely. Bounce its enablers/threats at instant speed (Stolen by the Fae bounced Dread Defiler, G93).
- **Pariah's Shield + indestructible (Darksteel Plate) = full lock.** Combat damage triggers (Cruelclaw, Scytheclaw) never fire; only the deck's sac effects or artifact removal break it — deny those (G30).
- Be wary of board wipes when Butcher of Malakir is on the battlefield — each creature death triggers sacrifice.
- Don't let Dread Defiler stick with creatures in your graveyard. Self-mill HELPS this deck (every milled fatty is an Olivia/Dread Defiler target) — don't try to mill it out.

## Game History

### Game 106 (pilot): The Claw!/Cruelclaw (Claude) vs. Mill-Steal/Wrexial (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 (16 sides) via concession. Final life: Claude 40 (untouched), Human 22. Dominance 2.

**Game flow:**
- Kept a no-red hand: Access Tunnel + Swamp + Thought Vessel + 3 fat creatures (Grave Titan, Butcher, Olivia) + Chaotic Transformation. Color-screwed on RED until T12 (drew Mountain) — stalled at 5 mana / mono-B for ~5 turns, unable to cast anything. Ruin Crab milled ~16 over the game.
- **The mill backfired on the Wrexial pilot:** Ruin Crab stocked CLAUDE's GY with Dread Defiler (6/8 drain), Orca, Necropolis Regent, Talisman of Indulgence, Rakdos Signet — Olivia reanimation targets + would-be ramp. Self-mill is upside for this deck (Olivia recurs fatties).
- T12: Mountain online. Hardcast **Grave Titan** + equipped pre-deployed Lightning Greaves (shroud + haste) → attacked for 6, made 4 zombie tokens. Greaves shroud made the Titan immune to Dimir's spot removal/bounce/steal.
- T14: Attacked for 12 (opp to 22), then cast Cruelclaw as **counter-bait** in M2 — opp Counterspell'd it (Archmage drew them a card). Cruelclaw → CZ. **Deck won on the BACKUP plan**, never connected Cruelclaw once.
- T15 opp: cast Wrexial, tapped out.
- T16: **Flare of Malice** (edict, sac greatest-MV) forced sacrifice of **Wrexial itself** (MV 6 > Archmage 4 > Crab 1) while opp had 1 mana up. Facing the edict + an 18-power board, opp conceded.

**MVP winner: Grave Titan** — 6/6 deathtouch + 4-8 zombie tokens was the entire wincon. Carried after the commander engine was countered out.

**Lessons:**
- **vs counter-heavy control, lead with a bait threat (Grave Titan), NOT Cruelclaw.** Worked perfectly: Titan resolved and built a board, Cruelclaw absorbed the Counterspell, board still won. Greaves on the bait body (not the commander) keeps it removal-proof.
- **Flare of Malice is premium vs Wrexial** — the edict's "greatest mana value" clause names Wrexial (MV 6), so it's clean sacrifice removal that bypasses 8 toughness AND the command-zone-redirect tempo (still +2 tax). Fire it while they're tapped out post-Wrexial.
- **Mill-steal mostly HELPS this deck** — every fat creature milled is an Olivia/Dread Defiler target. Don't fear the self-mill; race the slow durdle.
- **No-red keep was risky but fine vs a slow control deck** — survived 5 turns of red-screw because mill/steal has no early clock. Would NOT survive this keep vs aggro.

### Game 94 (opponent): The Claw!/Cruelclaw vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T12 (12 sides). Claude conceded at 31 life facing 57-power lethal alpha. Human life 40 (untouched). Dominance 1.

**Game flow:**
- T6: Cruelclaw on curve. T7: Claude Beast Within → Cruelclaw to CZ +2 tax, 3/3 beast token to Human.
- T8: Cruelclaw recast for 5 mana (3BR). Cruelclaw + Beast attacked. Cruelclaw still summoning sick — only Beast Token connected (3 dmg).
- T10: Cruelclaw + Beast attacked. Cruelclaw connect → exile top until nonland hit **Crabomination** → discarded **Kokusho** to cast Crab for free. Crab ETB exiled Claude's top card (Harrow), random GY card (Beast Within), random hand card (**Palani's Hatcher**). Cast Hatcher from exile for free → 2 Egg tokens on Human's side. **THE BACKBREAKER:** Crabomination's hand-exile pulled Claude's engine piece and turned it into mine.
- T12: Hardcast **Fiery Emancipation** {3}{R}{R}{R}. Triple-damage on every Human source.
- T12 combat math (post-FE): Cruelclaw 9 + Beast 9 + Crab 15 + Hatcher 15 + 3/3 Dino Token (from Hatcher sac trigger) 9 = **57 face damage**. Claude at 31 life → concede.

**MVP winner: Crabomination.** Single ETB exiled opp's library top + random GY card + random hand card, and let me free-cast Hatcher from their hand. Stole the engine piece. Without this, Cruelclaw's free spell would've been just one big creature — not a cascade-into-Hatcher chain.

**MVP loser (theirs): Beast Within.** Correct play T7 but 1-turn delay only (+2 tax → 5 mana recast next turn).

**Lessons:**
- **Crabomination is a top-tier Cruelclaw flip in 3+ color matchups.** Its "exile from hand" mode is the snowball: targets the opp's BEST engine card. Atla pilot held Hatcher as their key combo piece — Crab pulled it directly and let me cast it on my side, denying their engine AND building mine.
- **Fiery Emancipation off Cruelclaw cheat is the dream chain.** Cruelclaw connect cheats in expensive bombs (Crab 7 MV, Hatcher 5 MV cast-for-free), then FE hardcast triples all damage. Atla deck couldn't race to find Avatar (8 mana sorcery, 3W sources needed) in time.
- **Atla matchup: race the Egg engine.** Atla's flip-from-library is asymmetric — every Egg death is a free creature. Cruelclaw connect BEFORE Atla resolves means cheating in our own bombs and dealing lethal before they can flip. T10 connect was on curve; Atla wasn't even cast yet (mana-stuck). Pressure pays off if Atla's ramp stalls.
- **Atla deck's mana base is fragile.** 3 colors (RGW) means red sources can stay buried 10+ turns. Without R, no Atla cast, no engine. Don't slow-play this matchup expecting wipes — race it.

### Game 93 (opponent): The Claw!/Cruelclaw vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T23 (22 sides). Claude conceded at 27 life into 120+ power lethal alpha. Human life 21, CDMG: Cruelclaw 3, Zinnia 10. Dominance 2.

**Game flow:**
- Opening hand: Cruelclaw, CHG, 2 Mountain, Swamp, Damnation, Chaotic Transformation, Swiftfoot Boots. Keep — 3 lands BR, Cruelclaw castable T3+, Boots as evasion bonus, Damnation insurance.
- T6 Cruelclaw on curve. T8 Boots equip. But opp had Plumecreed Escort (flash flyer) + 1/1 offspring token by T7 — 2 flying blockers shut down menace permanently.
- T12: Damnation wiped opp's Zinnia + Escort + Aether Channeler + token. -Cruelclaw + 4 opp creatures. Both commanders to CZ.
- T14: Recast Cruelclaw {3BR}, equip Boots, attack into empty board → 3 face + flipped **Dread Defiler** (6/8 devoid drain engine). Discarded CHG for free cast.
- T16: DD activation exiled CHG → 4 drain. DD + Cruelclaw attacked, opp double-blocked Cruelclaw (Sol Sim + token), -2 opp creatures, DD 6 face = 10 dmg in one turn. Cruelclaw to CZ +4 tax.
- T17 opp: Murmuration + Arcane Signet + Combat Celebrant + 3 Storm Crows EOT. Setup for alpha.
- T18: Plague Wind one-sided wipe killed Zinnia, 3 Storm Crows, CC. DD attacked unblocked for 6 (opp 20 life).
- T19 opp: Stolen by the Fae X=7 bounced DD + dropped 7 Faerie tokens. Cast Junk Winder (affinity for tokens). Massive tempo swing — Claude lost only flying body + opp got wide token board in one card.
- T20: Sneak Attack {3R} + 2x activate (drop DD + Butcher of Malakir) — Butcher's death trigger on EOT sac = -4 Faeries total via chump + sac triggers.
- T21 opp: Recast Zinnia + Bident of Thassa. Attacked with JW + 2 Storm Crows + 2 Faeries — Bident drew 5 cards from connects.
- T22: Chaotic Transformation {5R} exiled Murmuration + Bident + Junk Winder. Replacements: Echoing Assault + Ornithopter of Paradise + Inspiring Overseer. Bad luck — Echoing Assault is a brutal swap (attack-trigger creates 1/1 token copy, all opp tokens gain menace).
- T23 opp: Devilish Valet (+offspring) → 1 Alliance trigger from offspring token → DV 1→2. Then Martial Coup X=4 → 4 Soldier tokens → 4 more Alliance triggers → DV 2→4→8→16→32 power. Lethal alpha imminent.

**MVP winner side:** **Devilish Valet** — exponential scaling via Alliance triggers on token entries. With wide-board tokens online, DV becomes lethal in one turn.

**MVP loser side:** **Dread Defiler** — only meaningful damage source. Drain + attack = 10 dmg T16. Bounced T19 and never returned to play.

**Lessons:**
- **Cruelclaw menace insufficient against wide flying boards.** Plumecreed Escort + offspring token (T7) immediately walls off menace. Even after Damnation T12 wiped the board, opp redeployed flyers within 2-3 turns. Need real unblockability (Access Tunnel, Rogues' Passage, Whispersilk Cloak) — Boots gives hexproof+haste but NOT unblockable.
- **Aether Channeler bounce is a HARD COUNTER to Sneak Attack-on-board plays.** T11 it bounced Sneak Attack from BF→hand. Permanent-based engines need to resolve into bigger threats than opp can answer.
- **Stolen by the Fae is the matchup-defining card.** Single card bounces a 6+ MV creature AND drops X flying tokens. Both effects feed Zinnia's +X buff AND remove your best blocker. Must respect opp casting it at 9+ mana.
- **Bident of Thassa post-alpha is the cards-draw refill that closes games.** With Murmuration making tokens every turn, Bident draws 5+ off any wide attack. Combination should be a top priority CT/discard target if possible.
- **Chaotic Transformation random replacements are too variable to rely on.** Got Echoing Assault for Murmuration (worse — token menace + attack-copy engine) and Devilish Valet ended the game. CT is a hope-and-pray card when desperate, not a reliable answer.
- **Plague Wind hardcast is a 9-mana liability** in matchups where opp rebuilds in 2 turns. Damnation cycle was more efficient (4 mana, both-sides wipe = mutual reset). Two wipes in a single game (T12 + T18) were not enough.
- **Dread Defiler is the strongest Cruelclaw flip seen so far** — 6/8 body + drain engine. Worth discarding CHG to fuel one immediate drain. Higher priority than Plague Wind flip (sorcery, can't reanimate).

- Pyreswipe Hawk is a DISASTER MATCHUP for this deck. It single-handedly undid both equipment pieces (steal Greaves, destroy Boots) in a 7-mana turn. ### Game 27 (pilot): The Claw! (Claude) vs. Faeries/Tegwyll (Human)

### Game 30 (opponent): The Claw/Cruelclaw vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (Claude)
**Result:** Claude wins T13 (25 sides). Claude life 4, Human life 25→dead. Dominance 3.

Cruelclaw's trigger completely neutralized — Pariah's Shield is a replacement effect, combat damage never reaches player, so "deals combat damage to a player" never fires.
- Crabomination stole Wrathful Raptors from opponent's GY — strong play but couldn't break the lock.
- Scytheclaw attempted as life-loss angle — doesn't work either, same replacement effect prevents the combat damage trigger from firing.

- **Near miss:** Shadowgrange Archfiend was on top of library — one Erebos draw away from forcing sacrifice of Wayta (greatest power on the board after equipment), breaking the lock entirely. The Scytheclaw misplay cost a draw step that would have found it.

## Matchup Notes
- The Cruelclaw trigger engine is the nominal win condition, but expect it to get answered — back-up bombs (Grave Titan, Dread Defiler, Crab/Fiery Emancipation) win games Cruelclaw never connects. Must answer the enablers (Lightning Greaves, Access Tunnel) not the commander itself (which has shroud).
- **vs Mill-Steal/Wrexial (1-0):** Slow durdle with no early clock — race it. Their mill HELPS us (every fat creature in our GY is an Olivia/Dread Defiler target); don't fear self-mill. vs counter-heavy control, lead with a bait threat (Grave Titan) and let Cruelclaw eat the counter — won on the backup, Cruelclaw never connected. **Flare of Malice is premium here:** the "greatest MV" edict names Wrexial (MV 6), clean sac removal that bypasses 8 toughness; fire it while they're tapped out post-Wrexial. A no-red keep survived 5 turns of color-screw vs this deck — viable here, NOT vs aggro.
- **vs Every Shrine Ever (1-0):** Slow, durdly build-around. Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves comes online and can't be answered (shroud + unblockable) — classic engine lock wins before shrines snowball.
- **vs Bello precon (0-1):** Wide Chariot + Cat-token engine outpaces a 3-damage menace clock — needs an early sweeper (Damnation/Blasphemous Act) before the board goes wide; Shadowgrange's sac-one-creature does nothing vs many small bodies. **Pyreswipe Hawk is a disaster card:** its expend 6 steals Lightning Greaves while Abrade kills Boots — both enablers gone in one turn. Want redundant enablers (Access Tunnel, Rogues' Passage, Whispersilk Cloak) vs artifact-sensitive removal.
- **vs Zinnia Precon (0-1):** Token-flood matchup. Opp's Murmuration + offspring + Bident of Thassa = wide flying army + sustained card draw. Cruelclaw connects ONCE if at all (T14 if Damnation clears board), but menace through 2+ flying blockers (Faeries, Storm Crows) is impossible. Plague Wind + Damnation both work for tempo but Murmuration regenerates the army in 1-2 turns AND Bident draws 5+ cards off alpha. Chaotic Transformation random replacements are too variable (got Echoing Assault + Devilish Valet replacement = brutal exponential combo). Stolen by the Fae bounces Dread Defiler at instant speed AND drops 7 Faerie tokens — single-card tempo blowout. Need: enchantment removal (no copies in deck), instant-speed protection for DD/Cruelclaw, or commit to faster Cruelclaw connects T3-T5 before token board overwhelms.
- **vs Boros Equipment:** Kellan's double strike makes commander damage lethal fast. Prioritize artifact/equipment removal over creature removal. Damnation doesn't answer equipment — need cards like Vandalblast, Shatterstorm, or targeted artifact destruction. Trailblazer's Boots is useless if opponent runs mostly basics early.
- **vs Faeries/Tegwyll (0-1):** Faeries' flash package and dense counter suite completely lock Cruelclaw out without evasion enablers. Menace is insufficient — they have enough flying bodies to double-block. Damnation is double-edged (Tegwyll draws for each dead Faerie). DO NOT play monarch effects (Court of Ire) — flyers steal it trivially and the extra draws fuel their already-strong card advantage. Talion choosing 3 directly taxes Cruelclaw (MV 3, P 3, T 3). Must find evasion enablers or the game is unwinnable. Bontu's Monument + Deepfathom Skulker create an overwhelming value engine if left unchecked.
- **vs Dargons/Scion (1-0):** 5-color mana base is too slow to establish blockers before Cruelclaw connects. Menace goes uncontested when opponent has no early creatures. Desolation Twin is a premium Cruelclaw flip — two 10/10s for one discard. Shadowgrange Archfiend is a strong closer: forces sac of their biggest creature, then swing lethal.
- **vs Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (0-1):** Pariah's Shield + Darksteel Plate on any creature is a full lockout — combat damage triggers (Cruelclaw, Scytheclaw) are completely dead because damage never reaches the player. The lock must be broken with sacrifice effects (Shadowgrange Archfiend, Flare of Malice) or artifact destruction (Megaton's Fate), NOT more combat damage angles. Every draw step matters once the lock lands — Erebos draw is the best tool to dig for outs. Do NOT move Lightning Greaves off Cruelclaw unless absolutely necessary — it creates a removal window for instant-speed burn. Breath damage from Drakuseth is meaningless into indestructible + Pariah's Shield. Priority: find sacrifice/artifact removal before opponent finds their reflection finisher (Star of Extinction + Blazing Sunsteel = 40+ one-shot).
- **vs Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (1-0):** Race the Egg engine. Atla is 3-color (RGW) on a fragile mana base — R sources frequently stay buried. Cruelclaw connect by T6-T10 is the win con before Atla resolves. Crabomination's hand-exile mode is the cascade card: it pulls Atla deck's engine pieces (Hatcher, Atla itself, Wakening Sun's Avatar) directly from their hand and casts them on our side. Hatcher specifically gives US the Egg tokens (haste-dino engine in our favor). Fiery Emancipation hardcast (6 mana) tripling every source closes after the cheat-chain builds a board — Atla deck has no instant-speed answer to a 6-mana enchantment. Watch for: Wakening Sun's Avatar (one-shot wipe — kills Cruelclaw + non-dinos), Beast Within on Cruelclaw or Greaves, Brontodon sacrificing into our artifacts. Avatar is 8 mana 5WWW — they rarely get to cast it; race or Crab-exile it.
