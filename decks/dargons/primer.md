# Dargons / Scion of the Ur-Dragon — Deck Primer
Commander: Scion of the Ur-Dragon | Colors: WUBRG (5-color)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Scion + Atarka double strike combo:** Activate Scion after Atarka's "Dragon attacks → double strike" trigger fires. Become a high-power Dragon (Hellkite Overlord 8/8, or Ureni 10/10) with double strike for massive commander damage.
- **Scion → Ureni, the Song Unending = protection from white and black.** Blanks Path to Exile, Swords to Plowshares, and all black removal. The ultimate defensive Scion activation against Abzan/Orzhov/Mardu opponents.
- **Timeless Lotus** on curve (5 lands → tap all for {5}) enables explosive turns.

## Key Rulings
- Scion's activation puts the Dragon card into the graveyard, then Scion becomes a copy. The copy effect lasts until end of turn.
- Commander damage tracks the commander (Scion), not what it's a copy of — all combat damage from Scion counts as commander damage regardless of what Dragon it's copying.
