# I Call Him Big Booty / Doran — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **TWO DISTINCT DORANS — don't conflate them for combat math:**
> - **Doran, the Siege Tower** (static, SYMMETRIC): each creature — yours AND opp's — assigns combat damage equal to its **toughness** instead of power. Recompute all combat off toughness while it's on board.
> - **Doran, Besieged by Time** (commander, YOUR creatures only): on **attack or block**, the creature gets **+X/+X** where X = |power − toughness|. It does NOT change damage to toughness — pumped creatures still deal **power** damage (unless a Siege Tower-type effect is also out, in which case the now-larger toughness is what's assigned). Also makes your high-toughness creature spells cost {1} less.

Commander: Doran, Besieged by Time | Colors: White/Black/Green (Abzan)

## Win Path (mechanical)
Big-butt beatdown: get a "deals damage = toughness" effect online, then attack with high-toughness bodies (walls, behemoths) that would otherwise hit for nothing. Push it through with evasion (Suspicious Bookcase, Rogue's Passage, built-in fliers). Alt-win: **Jaws of Defeat** drains opp on creature ETBs.
**Proven primary closer = the air** — all recorded wins came through flyers (Betor, Zetalpa, Wojek, Welcoming Vampire) over ground-bound decks; race in the air while the walls hold. The deck can win **without Doran sticking**: the support package (Siege Tower + Jaws + Betor + high-toughness evaders) carries through commander tax/counters.

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Doran, Besieged by Time** (commander, {1}{W}{B}{G}, 0/5) — cost reduction on big-butt creature spells + on attack/block each of your creatures gets +X/+X (X = tough−power gap). A 0/5 swings as a 5/10. Stacks multiplicatively with a Siege Tower effect (bigger toughness → more toughness-damage).
- **Doran, the Siege Tower** ({W}{B}{G}, 0/5) + other static sources — "each creature assigns combat damage equal to toughness." This is the toughness-as-damage payoff; symmetric. Redundant with the commander's theme but a DIFFERENT effect — both can be on board.
- **High-toughness payoffs**: Ancient Lumberknot, Bedrock Tortoise, Inversion Behemoth, Perennial Behemoth, Toxicrene, Zetalpa — small/zero power, huge toughness = big damage under a Doran effect.
- **Evasion**: Suspicious Bookcase (unblockable-ish), Rogue's Passage (1 creature unblockable), plus several natural fliers for built-in evasion.
- **Jaws of Defeat** — alt-win drain: when a creature you control enters, target opp loses life equal to that creature's power/toughness *difference*. Big-butt bodies (high tough, low power) drain hard — direct synergy with the deck's whole theme.
- **Betor, Kin to All** (flyer) — breakout engine in multiple wins: with high board toughness, each end step draw + untap-all; at total toughness ≥40 the opp loses half life. Converts the toughness theme into cards + a finisher. Deploy and protect it.
- **Toughness pumps are finishers, not protection** — under Doran Besieged a +0/+X or fight-pump (e.g. Chelonian Tackle) is removal + burst in one card; sequence pump → fight away the last blocker → alpha.
- **Overrun** — toughness-relevant alpha finisher.
- **Ramp**: Sol Ring, Talismans (Resilience/Unity), Commander's Sphere, Fellwar Stone, mana dorks + Cultivate/Kodama's Reach/Farseek/Rampant Growth. Dorks have varied P/T that matter under Doran.
- **Protection**: Heroic Intervention, Surge of Salvation, Dawn's Truce, Solidarity — guard the board from wipes (key, since the deck wants to commit bodies).
- **Removal**: Anguished Unmaking, Banishing Light, Grasp of Fate, Planar Disruption, Fell the Mighty (asymmetric — hits high power, spares our low-power butts).

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Doran is symmetric** — if the opp has high-toughness blockers/low-power attackers, the toughness-damage rule can be turned against this deck. Wide go-wide power decks suffer; toughness/defender decks don't.
- **Board wipes** punish the commit-bodies plan — but watch for Heroic Intervention / Surge of Salvation held up.
- **Fell the Mighty** keys off power, not toughness — most of this deck's threats survive it, so it's a poor wipe to play around from the opp's side.
- Removing a single Doran isn't enough if a backup copy or the commander is available; pressure the recast tax instead. (Confirmed: it has won with Doran taxed to 8 and countered twice.)
- **Slow, greedy mana base** — dork/Talisman-light. The deck can stumble on land/color screw and is slow to assemble (one loss to pure screw; one win where Doran didn't land until T21). Apply early pressure while it's setting up.
- **Bounce/wipe the toughness-damage source** (e.g. Cyclonic Rift on Doran the Siege Tower) reverts combat to power damage and neutralizes the butts — but it's temporary; a backup Doran/recast undoes it.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs Mill-Steal/Wrexial:** Hoverstone Pilgrim ({2}, no tap) bottoms every steal target — keep {2} open through Wrexial combat and pre-bottom your own GY instants/sorceries at opp's end step. Wrexial + counters is ~unkillable; deny the steals and race (the air kills before 21 CDMG).
- **vs Squirrel Food/Chatterfang:** Almost no flying defense — race the air (Wojek/Betor/Zetalpa). Only air answers (~T13: Odd Acorn Gang reach, Voracious Fell Beast) — temp-exile/bounce their blocker pre-combat, then alpha. Crystal Barricade's player-hexproof shuts off their Vito drain.
- **vs Faeries/Tegwyll:** They chump-wall + dig for a wipe (Tegwyll's Scouring / Cyclonic Rift). Race before the wipe; go WIDE (deathtouch trades one attacker/turn). Anguished Unmaking on Tegwyll strips their lord + death-draw before a wipe window.
- **vs Dinosaur Eggs/Atla:** Lone loss was pure mana screw, not the matchup — no durable read yet.

### Game 107 (Human): I Call Him Big Booty/Doran (Human) vs Faeries/Tegwyll (Claude) — **DEBUT WIN**
**Result: Big Booty wins T16 by concession. Final: Human 32, Claude 3. Dominance 3.** First "retail" game after the deck was called done.

**Key plays:**
- Curved out clean: T1 Terramorphic→Plains, T3 Three Tree Rootweaver (dork), T5 Doran Besieged (commander), then Welcoming Vampire + **Doran the Siege Tower** by T7 — both Dorans online early = full toughness-damage engine.
- T7: first Doran Besieged swing = 5/10 dealing 10 (toughness). Faeries had no blocker, took 10 (CDMG).
- T9: **Anguished Unmaking exiled Tegwyll** (cracked the deathtouch wall) + Moonlit Lamenter. Alpha for 24 — Faeries bounced Doran the Siege Tower with Cyclonic Rift to drop it to 8 (reverting to power dmg).
- T11: **Betor, Kin to All** (5/7 flyer) — the breakout engine. Board toughness already ~22, so every end step: draw + untap-all. Faeries had no answer in-color.
- T13/T15: ground alphas under both Dorans (toughness 5–10 each). Faeries survived only by chump-walling on Tegwyll death-draws (each Faerie death = a card).
- T15: flipped **Catapult Fodder → Catapult Captain** (inevitability: sac a creature, opp loses life = its toughness — lethal vs a low-life opp regardless of blocks). Betor end step hit **44 toughness ≥ 40** → opponent lost half life (7→3).
- T16: Faeries conceded at 3 — no wipe found, facing 6 toughness-pumped attackers + Catapult drain. (Ben's next draw was **Find // Finality** — Finality's asymmetric wipe would've sealed it anyway.)

**What worked:** Both Dorans + Betor is the full package — Siege Tower makes the walls lethal, Besieged pumps them bigger, Betor converts the toughness into cards/untaps/half-life. Catapult Captain is the closer that ignores blockers. Anguished Unmaking on the opp's key wall-piece (Tegwyll) at the right moment broke their defense.

**Matchup notes vs Faeries/Tegwyll:** Their plan is chump-wall + Tegwyll death-draws + dig for a wipe (Tegwyll's Scouring) or Cyclonic Rift overload. **Race them down before they find the wipe** — Betor's half-life kicker + Catapult Captain close games faster than they can dig. Tegwyll's deathtouch trades with any single attacker, so go WIDE (5–6 bodies) and accept one deathtouch trade per turn; the rest get through. Welcoming Vampire is a liability into their flyers under Siege Tower (it trades down) — don't over-rely on it as a blocker. Anguished Unmaking / removal on Tegwyll strips their lord + death-draw engine; time it before a wipe-flash window.
