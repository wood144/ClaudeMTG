# Every Shrine Ever — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Go-Shintai of Life's Origin | Colors: WUBRG (5-color)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Honden of Seeing Winds** (drawing X per upkeep where X = shrines) is the card draw engine. Protect it and stack shrines fast.
- **Sanctum of All** (6 shrines = all shrine abilities trigger twice) is the true win condition — tutor for it early with Shrine Steward.
- **Go-Shintai of Life's Origin** costs {3}{G} (4 mana), not 5. Creates shrine tokens on each nontoken shrine entering — crucial for flooding the board quickly.
- **Sanctum of Shattered Heights** needs discard fodder — only land cards and shrine cards qualify. Plan ahead.

## Key Rulings
- **Northern Air Temple** triggers on ANY shrine entering (including tokens) — gains life on every shrine ETB.
- **Go-Shintai of Life's Origin trigger** is "nontoken Shrine" entering only — tokens don't chain-trigger it.
- **Drakuseth trigger** ("4 to any target + 3 to each of up to 2 OTHER targets") — in 1v1 with no other valid targets, only 4 damage fires (other targets must be DIFFERENT from first).
- **Grave Titan** creates TWO 2/2 Zombie tokens on ETB/attack, not one.

## Game History

### Game 4: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (Human)
**Result:** Human wins ~T15. Cruelclaw trigger engine snowballed out of control.

**Key mistakes to avoid:**
- **Shrine deck needs to survive long enough to draw into removal.** Every shrine matters for card draw AND Sanctum damage.

### Game 33: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 by concession. Farewell board wipe + Honden engine generating 3 draws/3 discards per turn proved unrecoverable.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Honden of Seeing Winds + Honden of Night's Reach** is a devastating combo — drawing 3 while opponent discards 3 each upkeep.
- **Hull Breach removes shield counters** — targeting an enchantment creature with a shield counter removes the counter instead of destroying it, breaking Shield Broker's control effect.
- **Go-Shintai of Hidden Cruelty** is critical removal vs token strategies. End step trigger scales with shrine count.
- **Survived Martial Coup (X=8)** by having enchantment-based engines that don't die to creature wipes.
- **Eternal Witness** to recur Hidden Cruelty was a key play rebuilding after the wipe.

### Game 39: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Cats/Mirri (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 (sides 31) by concession. Sanctum of Stone Fangs drain engine draining 7+ per turn.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Sanctum of Stone Fangs + high shrine count** is a legit win condition. At 7-8 shrines, draining 7-8 life per turn closes games fast.
- **Aurora Awakener** was the turning point — Vivid revealed 5 permanents including Shrine Steward, which tutored Sanctum of Stone Fangs. Massive board swing from a losing position.
- **Sanctum of Tranquil Light** kept Mirri in check all game. At 5+ shrines, costs just {W} to tap a creature. Key defense piece.
- **Sterling Grove sacrifice → cascade setup** was strong. Tutored Sanctum of Tranquil Light to top, cascaded into it off Jukai Naturalist for free.
- **Greater Auramancy** protects the shrine engine but also prevents self-targeting with Leyline Immersion. Be aware of the anti-synergy.
- **Farewell choosing "exile all creatures" only** removes indestructible threats while preserving enchantment engines.
- **Path to Exile — save for Feline Sovereign.** That card is an existential threat to shrine strategies, destroying an enchantment every combat.

### Game 41: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T7 (sides 13) by concession. Overwhelmed by dice-rolling token engine.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Hull Breach on Mr. House T4** was correct — delayed the engine by 2 turns. But commander tax of {2} was trivially paid with Sol Ring ramp.
- **Go-Shintai of Hidden Cruelty** killed Ancient Copper Dragon at exactly 5 shrines (toughness 5 ≤ 5). But the Dragon already connected for 20 Treasures.
- **No board wipe in hand was fatal.** Farewell was the only out once Treasures fueled the entire board. Earth-Cult Elemental sac test revealed the missing Farewell — opponent read the land sacrifice correctly.
- **Shrine deck too slow vs explosive ramp.** Sol Ring T1 + Bobblehead mana meant opponent was 2-3 turns ahead on development. Shrines need more early interaction or faster mana.
- **Agility Bobblehead is the key enabler** — makes any creature unblockable. Must be destroyed alongside Mr. House if possible.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Cats/Mirri:** Feline Sovereign is the #1 threat — destroys enchantments on Cat combat damage. Path/exile removal must be saved for it. Mirri with Lightning Greaves = shroud prevents Sanctum of Tranquil Light from tapping her. Respond when Greaves are moved off for Rogue's Passage. Pride Sovereign scales fast with Cat count — can become 8-10/8-10. Tap it with Sanctum before combat. Fumigate doesn't kill indestructible Mirri (Shielded by Faith). Farewell exile mode does. The drain engine (Stone Fangs + Northern Air Temple) outpaces Cat aggro once shrine count reaches 5+.
- **vs. Zinnia Precon:** Zinnia's offspring engine creates tons of base-power-1 tokens, pumping Zinnia into a massive flyer. Curiosity Crafter + tokens = card draw engine. Shield Broker (cheated in via Arthur) steals enchantment creatures with shield counters. Hull Breach is the answer — destroy your own stolen enchantment creature, shield counter absorbs it, control reverts. Farewell (exile creatures + artifacts, NOT enchantments) is the ideal wipe since Honden engines survive. Honden of Night's Reach shreds their hand post-wipe.
- **vs. Cruelclaw:** Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves is a must-answer package. Lightning Greaves gives shroud (can't target with Sanctum) AND haste. Access Tunnel makes it unblockable. Priority: destroy Lightning Greaves with Hull Breach ASAP. Butcher of Malakir + any board wipe = forced sacrifice — killing opponent's creatures triggers Butcher, costing you a creature too. Dread Defiler's drain ability has no tap cost — can activate multiple times per turn at instant speed.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House:** Explosive ramp (Sol Ring + Bobbleheads) means Mr. House comes down fast and generates Robots/Treasures immediately. Hull Breach answers Mr. House (artifact creature) but commander tax is cheap with their mana. Ancient Copper Dragon connecting = game over (d20 Treasures with Wyll boost). Agility Bobblehead makes creatures unblockable — must be destroyed. Farewell is the critical card; mulligan aggressively for it or early tutors. Hidden Cruelty can snipe the Dragon if shrine count ≥ 5.
