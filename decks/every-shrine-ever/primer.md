# Every Shrine Ever — Deck Primer
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Go-Shintai of Life's Origin | Colors: WUBRG (5-color)

## Strategy & Combo Lines
- **Proven win paths (pick whichever the board offers):** (1) **Drain** — Sanctum of Stone Fangs / Northern Air Temple drain 7-9+/turn at high shrine count, bypasses blockers (G39, G54, G55). (2) **Go-wide finisher** — Southern Air Temple end step puts +X/+X on EVERY creature (X = shrines); lethal in one swing at 10+ shrines (G55, G61). (3) **Monk swarm** — Crescent Island Temple makes a Monk per shrine ETB, each noncreature spell pumps all Monks via prowess; goes lethal in a turn off a 4-shrine board (G79, G110). (4) **Burn** — Ancient Wars + Honden of Infinite Rage = 12+ damage/turn (G68). Sanctum of All (6 shrines, double triggers) accelerates all of these.
- **Enchantress stacking IS the engine.** Sythis / Verduran / Mesa Enchantress / Enchantress's Presence / Spirit Oasis turn each shrine cast into 2-4+ draws. This out-draws and outpaces opponents' creature strategies — prioritize landing enchantresses alongside shrines (G55, G79, G110).
- **Hidden Cruelty is the primary repeatable removal.** End step destroys a creature with toughness ≤ shrine count; at 5-6+ shrines it kills almost anything non-indestructible. Recurs from GY via commander. Deploy early and keep it alive. Against indestructible threats (Zetalpa) it's dead — need exile (Swords/Path/Farewell-exile).
- **Commander first, shrines second.** When not under early pressure, prioritize ramping into Go-Shintai of Life's Origin before deploying shrines. Each nontoken shrine cast with commander out creates a token — effectively doubling shrine count. Deploying 3 shrines post-commander = 7 shrines (3 + 3 tokens + commander) vs 4 shrines without (3 + commander). The end step triggers scale with total count, so this is multiplicative value.
- **Honden of Seeing Winds** (drawing X per upkeep where X = shrines) is the card draw engine. Protect it and stack shrines fast.
- **Sanctum of All** (6 shrines = all shrine abilities trigger twice) is the true win condition — tutor for it early with Shrine Steward.
- **Go-Shintai of Life's Origin** costs {3}{G} (4 mana), not 5. Creates shrine tokens on each nontoken shrine entering — crucial for flooding the board quickly.
- **Sanctum of Shattered Heights** needs discard fodder — only land cards and shrine cards qualify. Plan ahead.
- **Sanctum of Tranquil Light is a hidden bomb — do NOT undervalue it.** Ability `{5}{W}: Tap target creature`, −{1} per Shrine. At 5+ shrines it's effectively **{W}: tap anything**, and crucially there's **NO {T} cost on the Sanctum**, so it's repeatable — tap N creatures/turn for {W} each at instant speed. Tap their attackers in the beginning-of-combat step every turn = soft-lock that shuts off any deck built on a few big threats (voltron, Doran/big-butt, a couple of beaters). Only beaten by a board so wide your white mana can't keep up, or instant-speed hexproof/protection. Fragile to enchantment removal (it's one permanent) — but if they Banishing Light / Anguished Unmaking it, activate it in response to drain a full combat's worth of taps first (vs Doran/Felothar this denied an entire ~34-dmg alpha).

## How to Beat This Deck (our weaknesses)
- **Board wipes are the nightmare — especially exile/colorless.** Farewell-exile (G61) or colorless wipes (Ugin, All Is Dust — G110) erase shrines, enchantresses, and Monks one-sided, and exiled shrines can't be recursed by the commander. Every shrine/enchantress/Monk is colored, so a colorless deck wipes us and we can't reciprocate.
- **Counterplay:** don't over-commit into open mana — hold a shrine or two in hand and land Sterling Grove / Teferi's Protection / a counterspell BEFORE fully deploying. One sweeper can undo a whole turn of development.
- **Too slow vs explosive ramp.** Sol Ring / Bobblehead / Gruul-Bello starts put opponents 2-3 turns ahead; the engine often comes online T10 against a board that's already lethal (G41, G74). Vs ramp decks, race: get Hidden Cruelty / a drain online and apply a clock early, and mulligan for a board wipe (Farewell/Cyclonic Rift) in those matchups.
- **Bounce undoes counter investment.** Bouncing Go-Shintai of Boundless Vigor (Sink into Stupor, Cyclonic Rift) erases all its +1/+1 counters (G54). Diversify counters across shrines (e.g. Southern Air Temple) or hold protection rather than dumping them on one body.

## Key Rulings
- **Northern Air Temple** triggers on ANY shrine entering (including tokens) — gains life on every shrine ETB.
- **Go-Shintai of Life's Origin trigger** is "nontoken Shrine" entering only — tokens don't chain-trigger it.
- **Drakuseth trigger** ("4 to any target + 3 to each of up to 2 OTHER targets") — in 1v1 with no other valid targets, only 4 damage fires (other targets must be DIFFERENT from first).
- **Grave Titan** creates TWO 2/2 Zombie tokens on ETB/attack, not one.

**Key mistakes to avoid:**

### Game 33: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Zinnia Precon (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 by concession. Farewell board wipe + Honden engine generating 3 draws/3 discards per turn proved unrecoverable.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Honden of Seeing Winds + Honden of Night's Reach** is a devastating combo — drawing 3 while opponent discards 3 each upkeep.
- **Hull Breach removes shield counters** — targeting an enchantment creature with a shield counter removes the counter instead of destroying it, breaking Shield Broker's control effect.
- **Go-Shintai of Hidden Cruelty** is critical removal vs token strategies. End step trigger scales with shrine count.
- **Survived Martial Coup (X=8)** by having enchantment-based engines that don't die to creature wipes.
- **Eternal Witness** to recur Hidden Cruelty was a key play rebuilding after the wipe.

### Game 39: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Cats/Mirri (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T16 (sides 31) by concession. Sanctum of Stone Fangs drain engine draining 7+ per turn.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Sanctum of Stone Fangs + high shrine count** is a legit win condition. At 7-8 shrines, draining 7-8 life per turn closes games fast.
- **Aurora Awakener** was the turning point — Vivid revealed 5 permanents including Shrine Steward, which tutored Sanctum of Stone Fangs. Massive board swing from a losing position.
- **Sanctum of Tranquil Light** kept Mirri in check all game. At 5+ shrines, costs just {W} to tap a creature. Key defense piece.
- **Sterling Grove sacrifice → cascade setup** was strong. Tutored Sanctum of Tranquil Light to top, cascaded into it off Jukai Naturalist for free.
- **Greater Auramancy** protects the shrine engine but also prevents self-targeting with Leyline Immersion. Be aware of the anti-synergy.
- **Farewell choosing "exile all creatures" only** removes indestructible threats while preserving enchantment engines.
- **Path to Exile — save for Feline Sovereign.** That card is an existential threat to shrine strategies, destroying an enchantment every combat.

### Game 41: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T7 (sides 13) by concession. Overwhelmed by dice-rolling token engine.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Agility Bobblehead is the key enabler** — makes any creature unblockable. Must be destroyed alongside Mr. House if possible.

### Game 54: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Mill/Steal/Wrexial (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T8 cycle (sides 15) by concession. Final life: Claude 47, Human 6. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**
- T8: Deployed Northern Air Temple + Go-Shintai of Boundless Vigor in one turn while opponent tapped out. NAT ETB drained 5 (4 shrines at resolution + token trigger). Vigor end step put 6 counters. Massive tempo swing.
- T10: Prismatic Undercurrents fetched Island + Forest, enabling WUBRG for Maelstrom Nexus T14.
- T12: Full attack for 5 after Sink into Stupor bounced Vigor (lost +11/+11 counters). Recast Vigor in Main 2, regrew to 6/6 by end step.
- T14: Maelstrom Nexus resolved (opponent tapped out for Wrexial). Attacked for 5 through Wrexial block on Vigor.

**Lessons:**
- **Mill without GY payoff is free cards for shrine recursion.** Glimpse + Mind Funeral + Fractured Sanity milled ~34 cards but found zero instants/sorceries for Wrexial's trigger. Meanwhile, GY stocked with shrine recursion targets (Hidden Cruelty, Lost Wisdom, 4 Hondens, Sterling Grove, etc.).
- **Bouncing Vigor is the opponent's best play.** Sink into Stupor erased 11 turns of +1/+1 counters. Always consider: can opponent bounce Vigor? If so, diversify counters across multiple shrines or hold mana for protection.
- **Northern Air Temple drain scales fast.** 5 drain on ETB + 2 per shrine cast after = significant life swing that bypasses blockers.
- **Wrexial's swampwalk is live via Godless Shrine.** Would have been unblockable 5/turn if game continued. Don't play Prismatic Omen — turns ALL lands into Islands/Swamps.

### Game 55: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T8 cycle (sides 15) by lethal combat. Claude 46, Human 31. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**
- T9: Sythis + Spirit Oasis in one turn drew 7 cards off enchantress/shrine triggers. Engine fully online.
- T11: Hidden Cruelty deployed, end step destroyed Bonehoard Dracosaur (5 toughness ≤ 6 shrines). Swords to Plowshares drawn off enchantress triggers.
- T13: Deployed Stone Fangs (drain 9/turn), Ancient Wars (9 damage/turn), Weaver of Harmony (+1/+1 lord). Opponent used Savage Stomp + Ram Through to kill Hidden Cruelty.
- T13 response: Swords to Plowshares exiled Zetalpa (indestructible) in response to Savage Stomp — saved Hidden Cruelty AND removed the biggest threat.
- T15: Stone Fangs drained 9 (opp 40→31). Southern Air Temple ETB: +14/+14 counters on every creature. ~200 power on board, lethal through 5 blockers.

**Lessons:**
- **Enchantress stacking is the real engine.** Verduran Enchantress + Sythis + Spirit Oasis = 4+ draws per shrine cast. This outpaced the opponent's creature cheating.
- **Hidden Cruelty scales with shrine count** and cleanly answers non-indestructible threats. At 6+ shrines it kills almost anything.
- **Southern Air Temple is a finisher.** At 13 shrines, +14/+14 on everything is instant lethal. Consider tutoring for it when shrine count is high.
- **Hold instant-speed removal for response windows.** Swords in response to Savage Stomp was a 2-for-1 (exile Zetalpa + fizzle the fight).
- **Opponent was one Plains away from Wakening Sun's Avatar** (destroy all non-Dinosaurs) which would have wiped the entire shrine board. Race the Naya deck before they find sweepers.

### Game 61: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Cats/Mirri (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T7 cycle (sides 14) by lethal combat. Claude 0, Human 25. Dominance 3.

**Key plays:**
- T5: Birds T1 into Vigor T3, Ancient Wars T5. Strong shrine curve with end step triggers online early.
- T9: Commander deployed via Tarnation Vista mana ability (3 colors = {G}{R}{W}). Shrine count hit 5. Ancient Wars dealt 5 damage, Vigor grew to 9/9 trample. Board looked dominant.
- T10: Farewell (exile enchantments) wiped ALL shrines — Vigor, Ancient Wars, Shared Purpose, commander, token. Total engine destruction. Commander to CZ with +{2} tax.
- T11: Rebuilt with Southern Air Temple. T13: Recast commander ({5}{G}), SAT counters made commander 5/6, Birds 3/4, token 3/3.
- T12-14: Arahbo + King of the Pride + Mirri (Greaves) built a 25-power board. Lethal T14 attack through Mirri's single-blocker restriction.

**Lessons:**
- **Farewell (exile enchantments) is the nightmare scenario.** Exile means no graveyard recursion with commander. Three key shrines permanently gone. Must hold Sterling Grove or Teferi's Protection against decks running Farewell.
- **Tarnation Vista's mana ability scales with monocolored permanents.** When shrines are exiled, it reverts to basic {R} tap. Don't plan mana around the ability post-wipe.
- **Cats' double lord stack (Arahbo + King of the Pride) turns tokens into 4/3s.** Combined with Mirri's single-blocker restriction, this is lethal even through a 5/6 commander blocker.
- **Opponent held Heroic Intervention with 3 mana open.** Even when appearing tapped out for aggro, Cats can protect the board. Don't assume safe attacks.
- **Greater Auramancy anti-synergy with Vigor counters matters.** Correctly held Auramancy to keep counter engine running, but engine got wiped before protection mattered. In hindsight, deploying Auramancy + Sterling Grove for mutual shroud protection before committing shrines might have forced opponent to Farewell the protection pieces first.

### Game 74: Every Shrine Ever (Claude) vs. Bello precon (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T6 cycle (12 sides) by concession. Human 40, Claude 5. Dominance 3.

**Key plays:**
- Sol Ring T1 into Burnished Hart T3 → sacced for 2 basics. Bello T7 on curve with 7+ mana.
- Claude T8: Commander deployed. T10: Jukai Naturalist + Hidden Cruelty (discounted). End step killed Bello (3 shrines ≥ 3 toughness).
- T9: Rolling Hamsphere animated by Bello — 7/7 indestructible, attack trigger killed shrine token + dealt 7. Ghalta for {G}{G} post-combat.
- T11: Bello recast + Unnatural Growth. Doubled combat: Ghalta 24/24 trample, Hamsphere 17/17 indestructible, UG 8/8 indestructible. Hamsphere trigger killed Hidden Cruelty. 28 damage through optimal blocks, survived at 5.
- T12: Drew land off Abundant Growth cantrip. No board wipe = concession.

**Lessons:**
- **Rolling Hamsphere + Bello is devastating.** Animated as 4/4 indestructible hasty, attack trigger creates 3 Hamsters + deals X damage. Grows itself with +1/+1 per Hamster. Must be answered before it attacks.
- **Unnatural Growth doubles EVERYTHING at beginning of each combat.** Combined with Bello's animation, creates 50+ power boards. Bello also animates Growth itself as 8/8 indestructible.
- **Hidden Cruelty killed Bello but opponent just recast it.** Commander tax (+{2}) is trivial with their ramp. Need repeatable removal or exile-based answers.
- **Shrine deck too slow vs Gruul ramp + Bello.** Engine came online T10 but opponent already had Ghalta + Hamsphere. Need Farewell or Cyclonic Rift in opener against this deck.
- **Block non-tramplers to absorb maximum damage.** Blocking Hamsphere (no trample, 17/17) with commander saved 17 damage. Always check for trample before assigning blocks.

### Game 79: Every Shrine Ever (Human) vs. Dargons/Scion (Claude)
**Result:** Human wins T6 cycle (11 sides) via lethal combat. Final life: Human 33, Claude 0. Dominance 2.

**Key plays:**
- T1-T7: Curve out Forest+Fertile Ground T3, Great Hall T3, Swamp T5, Go-Shintai of Life's Origin T5, Leyline Immersion T7 (5-mana ramp on Go-Shintai), Wildsear, Scouring Maw T7.
- T8: Claude's Scion of Draco entered (4/4 flyer for {2} via domain). Hit for 4.
- T9: Cast Crescent Island Temple → Wildsear's cascade into Northern Air Temple. NAT chain drained 5 (Go-Shintai trigger Shrine Token + NAT ETB at X=4 shrines). Crescent ETB created 6+1 Monks. Cast Hidden Cruelty (cascade into Abundant Growth → 1 free draw + Great Hall any-color). Hidden Cruelty EOT killed Scion of Draco.
- T10 (Claude): Scion → Atarka, 12 CDMG. PtE'd Hidden Cruelty.
- T11: WIN TURN. Cast Enchantress's Presence → Wildsear cascade into Weaver of Harmony (+1/+1 to enchantment creatures, copy-trigger ability). Cast Maelstrom Nexus → cascade into Mesa Enchantress. Cast Lost Wisdom → cascade into Sol Ring. 3 noncreature spells = +3/+3 prowess per Monk = 11 Monks at 4/4. Plus Weaver buffed Shrine Tokens to 2/2. Attack with everything (54+ power) for lethal.

**Lessons:**
- **Wildsear, Scouring Maw + Maelstrom Nexus is the engine.** Wildsear gives cascade to enchantment spells from hand. Maelstrom Nexus gives cascade to first spell each turn. Stacking cascade triggers chains free spells exponentially.
- **Monk tokens from Crescent Island Temple are the win condition vs. control/midrange.** Each shrine entering = +1 Monk via Crescent. Each noncreature spell = +1/+1 to all Monks via prowess. Goes lethal in one turn from a 4-shrine board.
- **Weaver of Harmony as cascade target is great.** Buffs Shrine Tokens from 1/1 to 2/2 + has copy-trigger ability for double-resolving NAT drains or Crescent Monks.
- **Mesa Enchantress + Enchantress's Presence is the card-draw engine.** Each enchantment cast = draw 2. Off Maelstrom Nexus's any-spell cascade, this finds combo pieces fast.
- **Claude's misplay was Atarka over Scourge.** With opp at 43 life and 14 small creatures on the wide board, the right Scion target was Scourge of Kher Ridges + activate {1}{R} wipe to clear Hidden Cruelty, Monks, and Shrine Tokens. Atarka's 12 CDMG looked decisive but didn't end the game and left the prowess swarm intact for T11 lethal.
- **vs. Dargons:** Scion of Draco's hexproof+lifelink+first-strike static makes Scion of Ur-Dragon hard to kill. Hidden Cruelty MUST kill Scion of Draco first to remove the static, then target Scion. Once both fall, Atarka activation pressure stops.

### Game 110: Every Shrine Ever (Human) vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Claude)
**Result:** Human loss, T6 (11 sides) by concession. Human 33, Claude 40. Dominance 3 (Claude).

**Key plays:**
- Curved out reasonably: Sythis T3, Sanctum of Tranquil Light + Go-Shintai by T4, Crescent Island Temple T5 → 5 shrines, 6 prowess Monks, Weaver of Harmony on board by T5.
- Claude ramped uninterrupted into Zhulodok T4, Coveted Jewel T5, then **Ugin the Spirit Dragon T6 −4 exiled the entire board** (Sythis, Go-Shintai, Weaver, both nontoken shrines, all 6 Monks, the mana dork). Only the colorless Shrine Tokens + lands survived. Conceded.

**Lessons:**
- **Ugin / All Is Dust / any colorless board wipe is the nightmare vs this deck** — every shrine, enchantress, and Monk is colored, so a colorless deck wipes us one-sided and we can't answer their colorless permanents in kind. Against Eldrazi specifically, assume Ugin is coming around T6-7.
- **Don't over-extend into open colorless mana.** I dumped Crescent + 6 Monks + Weaver onto the board with no protection; one card erased all of it. Hold a shrine or two in hand, or prioritize a Sterling Grove / Teferi's Protection / counterspell before fully committing.
- Need faster pressure or disruption — the shrine engine was on schedule but Eldrazi simply ramps faster and the payoff is a single-card blowout. Race signal: get Hidden Cruelty / drain online and apply a clock before their T6.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (0-1):** Colorless ramp + Zhulodok cascade engine (MV7+ colorless spells double-cascade into free permanents). **Their entire board is colorless → our removal is fine but Ugin the Spirit Dragon / All Is Dust wipe US one-sided and we can't reciprocate.** They ramp into Zhulodok T4-5 and a cascade bomb (Ugin/Kozilek/Flayer) T6-7. We must apply a real clock before then — Hidden Cruelty can't reach Zhulodok until 4 shrines (it's 7/**4**), and Sanctum of Tranquil Light tap-lock only handles a couple of beaters, not a wide cascade board. Don't over-commit; hold shrines back and prioritize protection (Sterling Grove/Teferi's Protection) since one Ugin undoes a whole turn of development. Coveted Jewel they cast is steal-able only if we have an unblocked attacker — usually they keep blockers up, so don't count on it.
- **vs. Zinnia Precon (2-0):** Hidden Cruelty hard-counters Zinnia (3 toughness, always in range at 3+ shrines). Deploy Cruelty ASAP and kill Zinnia on sight. Zinnia's offspring engine spawns base-power-1 tokens that pump Zinnia into a massive flyer; Curiosity Crafter + tokens is their card draw. Shield Broker (cheated in via Arthur) is the main threat — steals enchantment creatures with shield counters. Hull Breach destroys your own stolen creature (shield counter absorbs it, control reverts) OR Honden of Infinite Rage pops the shield counter — flexible removal + face damage. Starnheim Courser accelerates the shrine engine. Farewell in exile-creatures+artifacts mode (NOT enchantments) is the ideal wipe — Honden engines survive; Honden of Night's Reach then shreds their hand. Ancient Wars + Honden = 12+ damage/turn once engine is online.
- **vs. Mill/Steal/Wrexial (1-0):** Mill is largely harmless — deck runs very few instants/sorceries so Wrexial's combat trigger finds nothing. Mill actually helps by stocking GY with shrine recursion targets for commander ability. Wrexial has swampwalk via Godless Shrine — unblockable 5/turn. Race is favorable: NAT drain bypasses blockers, Vigor grows exponentially. Main threat: Sink into Stupor / Cyclonic Rift bouncing Vigor erases all +1/+1 counters. Consider spreading counters or holding protection. Archmage Emeritus + Mocking Sprite is their card draw engine — kill Emeritus with Hidden Cruelty if possible. DO NOT play Prismatic Omen — turns all lands into Islands/Swamps for Wrexial.
- **vs. Cats/Mirri (1-1):** Feline Sovereign is the #1 threat — destroys enchantments on Cat combat damage. Path/exile removal must be saved for it. Mirri with Lightning Greaves = shroud prevents Sanctum of Tranquil Light from tapping her. Respond when Greaves are moved off for Rogue's Passage. Arahbo + King of the Pride double lord stack turns Cat tokens into 4/3s — combined with Mirri's single-blocker restriction, this creates lethal boards fast. Farewell (exile enchantments) is catastrophic — exiled shrines can't be recursed by commander. Must hold Sterling Grove / Teferi's Protection / counterspell against Farewell. If engine gets wiped, rebuild is too slow against Cat aggro. Drain engine (Stone Fangs + NAT) outpaces Cat aggro once shrine count reaches 5+, but must get there before the wipe.
- **vs. Cruelclaw (0-1):** Cruelclaw + Access Tunnel + Lightning Greaves is a must-answer package. Lightning Greaves gives shroud (can't target with Sanctum) AND haste. Access Tunnel makes it unblockable. Priority: destroy Lightning Greaves with Hull Breach ASAP. Butcher of Malakir + any board wipe = forced sacrifice — killing opponent's creatures triggers Butcher, costing you a creature too. Dread Defiler's drain ability has no tap cost — can activate multiple times per turn at instant speed.
- **vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (0-1):** Explosive ramp (Sol Ring + Bobbleheads) means Mr. House comes down fast and generates Robots/Treasures immediately. Hull Breach answers Mr. House (artifact creature) but commander tax is cheap with their mana. Ancient Copper Dragon connecting = game over (d20 Treasures with Wyll boost). Agility Bobblehead makes creatures unblockable — must be destroyed. Farewell is the critical card; mulligan aggressively for it or early tutors. Hidden Cruelty can snipe the Dragon if shrine count ≥ 5.
- **vs. Bello precon (0-1):** Rolling Hamsphere + Bello = 7/7+ indestructible hasty that generates Hamsters and deals scaling damage on attack. Unnatural Growth doubles everything at combat start — Bello animates Growth itself as 8/8 indestructible. Hidden Cruelty kills Bello (3 toughness) but recast is cheap with Gruul ramp. Need Farewell or Cyclonic Rift — mulligan for board wipes. Shrine engine too slow to race without early wipe. Block non-tramplers (Hamsphere, animated enchantments) to absorb maximum damage. Ghalta comes down for {G}{G} with Bello's board — trample is the real killer.
- **vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (1-0):** Atla + Palani's Hatcher is the engine — Hatcher auto-sacs Eggs at beginning of combat, Atla flips creatures from library. Kill Atla or Hatcher with Hidden Cruelty (prioritize Hatcher — it's not a commander and permanently gone). Descendants' Path gives free creatures on upkeep. Xenagos doubles power + haste — force multiplier. Zetalpa is indestructible — MUST have exile removal (Swords, Path to Exile, Farewell exile mode). Hidden Cruelty can't touch it. Wakening Sun's Avatar (destroy all non-Dinosaurs) is their best answer to a developed shrine board — race before they find it.
