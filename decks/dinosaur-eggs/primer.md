# Dinosaur Eggs / Atla Palani — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Atla Palani, Nest Tender | Colors: Red/Green/White (Naya)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Atla Palani** creates Egg tokens that, when they die, reveal cards from the top of the library until hitting a creature — that creature enters the battlefield.
- Sacrifice engines + Atla Palani = free big creatures from the library.
- Toolbox strategy: the quality of creatures in the deck determines the ceiling of Atla's ability.

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Steal effects are devastating** — stealing Atla Palani turns her sacrifice ability against the opponent (their eggs flip your creatures instead).
- Remove Atla Palani before she can generate and sacrifice Egg tokens.

## Matchup Notes
- Atla Palani is the engine — without her, the deck is just fair creatures. Prioritize commander removal.

### vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Game 45)
- Ghave + Parallel Lives is the main threat. Destroying Parallel Lives is high priority — without it, Ghave's token generation is fair.
- Slimefoot punishes trading with Saprolings (1 damage per death). Avoid killing Saps unnecessarily.
- Brightcap Badger turns Saprolings into mana dorks — accelerates Ghave activations significantly.
- Opponent's best outs: Damnation (board wipe resets the Dino army), Dictate of Erebos (forces sacs on creature death), Glare of Subdual (tap down attackers), Eldrazi Monument (indestructible + flying blocks everything).
- Temple Altisaur is critical — it makes the Dino army nearly unkillable in combat. Opponent correctly targeted it with Ghave block.

### vs. Every Shrine Ever/Go-Shintai (Game 55)
- Shrine deck's enchantress engine (Verduran Enchantress + Sythis + Spirit Oasis) draws 4+ cards per shrine cast. Snowballs fast once online T9+.
- Hidden Cruelty is the shrine deck's main removal — scales with shrine count. Kill it before it reaches 5+ shrines or it picks off your best creatures at end step.
- Sanctum of Stone Fangs drains X life per turn (X = shrines). At 9+ shrines, that's a 2-3 turn clock even without combat.
- Southern Air Temple is a lethal finisher — +X/+X counters on everything at high shrine count. Must be countered or the board is instantly lethal.
- Savage Stomp + Ram Through are good removal for shrine creatures but check opponent's graveyard for instants first — Swords to Plowshares in response to Savage Stomp exiled Zetalpa AND fizzled the fight.
- Wakening Sun's Avatar (destroy all non-Dinosaurs) is the best answer to a developed shrine board. Prioritize finding it or Atla-flipping into it.
- Xenagos resolved but too late to matter without enough combat steps. Deploy the force multiplier earlier.

### vs. Cats/Mirri (Game 34)
- Mirri's first strike makes her a strong blocker against damaged creatures (Raptor's 2 damage on ETB matters).
- Regal Caracal is the force multiplier — lifelink + lord effect. Removing it early collapses the board.
- Cats deck needs early interaction to stop the Atla + Raptor engine. Without removal, the egg flips snowball fast.
- Prowling Felidar + landfall can grow large, but a single big Cat can't stop multiple tramplers.

### vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (Game 77)
- Brash Taunter is the lynchpin of the reflection win con. Path to Exile is the cleanest answer (bypasses indestructible). Save white removal for Brash specifically.
- Wrathful Raptors is THE answer card to opp's Star of Extinction wipe plan. Once down, opp can't safely wipe — the reflection backfires for 20 dmg per dino. Keep Wrathful Raptors protected (or at least bait Banishing Light onto something else first).
- Akroma's Will both modes is a panic finisher — Wayta is multicolored and can't block protection-from-each-color attackers. Save it for the swing turn.
- Greater Good + Marauding Raptor is the engine: each dino entry buffs Raptor +2/+0 UEOT, so saccing Raptor mid-explosion-turn draws double-digit cards. Ghalta from Mosswort + Greater Good response is the ideal panic combo.
- Wayta's ability ("if a creature you control being dealt damage causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time") doubles Brash Taunter's reflection — be aware that any fight or damage event involving Brash deals 2x damage to you. Don't volunteer to fight Brash.
