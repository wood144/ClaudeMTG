# Dinosaur Eggs / Atla Palani — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Atla Palani, Nest Tender | Colors: Red/Green/White (Naya)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Atla Palani** creates Egg tokens that, when they die, reveal cards from the top of the library until hitting a creature — that creature enters the battlefield.
- Sacrifice engines + Atla Palani = free big creatures from the library.
- Toolbox strategy: the quality of creatures in the deck determines the ceiling of Atla's ability.

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Steal effects are devastating** — stealing Atla Palani turns her sacrifice ability against the opponent (their eggs flip your creatures instead).
- Remove Atla Palani before she can generate and sacrifice Egg tokens.

## Game History

### Game 3 (opponent): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. Eldrazi/Zhulodok (Claude)
**Result:** Claude (Eldrazi) wins T11. Stolen Atla Palani + Flayer of Loyalties was game-ending.

### Game 34 (piloted by Claude): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. Cats/Mirri (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T13 via combat damage (concession during lethal attack).
- T3: Marauding Raptor on curve — key combo piece with Atla.
- T7: Atla Palani cast with Raptor's cost reduction ({R}{G}{W}).
- T9: First egg flip → Quartzwood Crasher (6/6 trample). Attacked unblocked for 6, created 6/6 Dino Beast token.
- T11: Mosswort Bridge activated (10 power threshold met) → free Bonehoard Dracosaur. Second egg flip → Gishath, Sun's Avatar (7/6 haste/trample/vigilance). Held Gishath back due to 2 damage from Raptor making it vulnerable to Mirri's first strike.
- T13: Bonehoard Dracosaur upkeep value. Contest of Claws (from exile via Dracosaur) killed Regal Caracal (removing +1/+1 and lifelink from all cats). Discover 4 → Forerunner of the Empire → tutored Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant to top → Atla flipped it → ETB dumped 5 creatures from hand (both Ghaltas, Annoyed Altisaur, Etali, Hulking Raptor). 40 power of eligible attackers including flying. Concession.
- **Key synergy:** Marauding Raptor auto-kills Egg tokens (0/1), triggering Atla immediately. No additional sac outlet needed.
- **MVP:** Atla Palani + Marauding Raptor combo engine.

### Game 45 (piloted by Claude): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T8 (15 sides) via lethal combat damage. Claude 34 life, Human 16.
- T9: Palani's Hatcher cast → 2 Egg tokens. Beginning of combat: sac Egg → 3/3 Dino (haste) + Atla flipped Temple Altisaur (3/4, damage prevention for Dinos). 6 unblocked damage.
- T11: Trumpeting Carnosaur (7/6 trample, haste) + discover 5 → Invasion of Ikoria (battle, 6 defense). Beginning of combat: sac Egg → 3/3 + Atla flipped Pugnacious Hammerskull (6/6, haste). 17 unblocked damage. Siege reduced to 1 counter. Temple Altisaur traded with Ghave block.
- T13: Atla activation → Egg → Raptor kills it → flipped Marauding Raptor. Thrashing Brontodon destroyed Parallel Lives.
- T15: Atla + Raptor engine flipped Rampaging Brontodon (14/14 trample with 7 lands). Herd Heirloom gave Hammerskull trample + card draw. 45 power total, lethal through any blocks.
- **MVP:** Temple Altisaur — damage prevention made the entire Dino army unkillable in combat. Opponent couldn't profitably block anything until Ghave was large enough to kill Temple itself (it doesn't protect itself). Free attacks from T9-T13 chipped opponent from 40 to 16 without losing a creature.
- **Key synergy chain:** Palani's Hatcher (haste + combat sac) → Atla (flip creatures from Egg deaths) → Marauding Raptor (auto-kill future Eggs) = self-sustaining engine.
- **Strategic error (T11):** Only sent 1 non-trampler at the Siege — opponent chumped with a Sap easily. With 30-45 power on board, should send 2-3 creatures INCLUDING a trampler at the Siege. Tramplers make the fork real: opponent can't cheaply chump, and must either over-commit blockers (more face damage) or let Siege die (Zilortha = assign damage as unblocked). Non-tramplers at a battle get chumped for free — only invest in flipping a battle when you have trample to force the choice AND enough total power that diverting some doesn't cost you lethal on the player.

## Matchup Notes
- Atla Palani is the engine — without her, the deck is just fair creatures. Prioritize commander removal.

### vs. Fun Guys/Ghave (Game 45)
- Ghave + Parallel Lives is the main threat. Destroying Parallel Lives is high priority — without it, Ghave's token generation is fair.
- Slimefoot punishes trading with Saprolings (1 damage per death). Avoid killing Saps unnecessarily.
- Brightcap Badger turns Saprolings into mana dorks — accelerates Ghave activations significantly.
- Opponent's best outs: Damnation (board wipe resets the Dino army), Dictate of Erebos (forces sacs on creature death), Glare of Subdual (tap down attackers), Eldrazi Monument (indestructible + flying blocks everything).
- Temple Altisaur is critical — it makes the Dino army nearly unkillable in combat. Opponent correctly targeted it with Ghave block.

### vs. Cats/Mirri (Game 34)
- Mirri's first strike makes her a strong blocker against damaged creatures (Raptor's 2 damage on ETB matters).
- Regal Caracal is the force multiplier — lifelink + lord effect. Removing it early collapses the board.
- Cats deck needs early interaction to stop the Atla + Raptor engine. Without removal, the egg flips snowball fast.
- Prowling Felidar + landfall can grow large, but a single big Cat can't stop multiple tramplers.
