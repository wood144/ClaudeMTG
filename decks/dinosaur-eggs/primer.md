# Dinosaur Eggs / Atla Palani — Deck Primer (Opponent Notes)
<!-- summary reconciled 2026-06-21 -->
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Atla Palani, Nest Tender | Colors: Red/Green/White (Naya)

## Strategy & Key Cards
- **Atla is OPTIONAL.** Multiple wins came with her never activated (Games 111, 114). Primary plan = ramp into fat dinos, cheat them from hand, strip blockers with targeted removal, then alpha. Atla amplifies via Egg-death flips but isn't the foundation. See [[feedback-atla-optional]].
- **Atla Palani** creates Egg tokens that, when they die, reveal from top of library until a creature — it enters the battlefield. Sac engines + Atla = free big creatures. Toolbox: quality of creatures sets the ceiling.
- **Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant = the nuclear finisher.** Her ETB cheats the rest of hand onto the battlefield. Lands via Atla flip, Mosswort hideaway, or hardcast — any of them can turn a board into instant lethal (Games 95, 34, 77, 88, 111).
- **Forerunner of the Empire → discover/top-deck combo.** Forerunner ETB tutors a dino to top; Pantlaza discover (X = toughness) or an Atla flip then drops it free. Precise, guaranteed cheat (Games 95, 85, 34).
- **Greater Good + Marauding Raptor = draw engine.** Raptor gains +2/+0 per dino entry; sac the pumped Raptor mid-explosion to draw double-digit cards (26 in Game 77) — reliably finds Path/STP/Akroma's Will.
- **Marauding Raptor** auto-kills 0/1 Eggs → fires Atla with no sac outlet needed, and is the cheap combo enabler (Games 34, 45, 95).
- **Hatcher + Marauding Raptor + Atla on board = full board explosion** off one Hatcher cast (2 Eggs → pings → flips → Ghalta hits → hand-dump). Engine pivot without a look-back activation (Game 95).
- **Bonehoard Dracosaur = midgame value MVP.** 5/5 flying first strike — free clock vs grounded/flyerless boards + 2 cards/turn (Games 114, 85, 34).
- **Growing Rites of Itlimoc → Itlimoc ramp engine** vs a slow opp; **Rampaging Brontodon** scales with land count into a one-sided trampler finisher (Game 114, 45).
- **Look-back vs wipes (hidden combo):** activate Atla in response to a board wipe but DON'T sac the Egg — wipe destroys Atla + Egg simultaneously, the "Egg dies" trigger fires post-wipe, and the flipped creature enters after resolution and survives (Games 85, 88).
- **Targeted removal pile** (Savage Order, Savage Stomp, Ram Through, Contest of Claws, STP/Path, Generous/Beast/Gift): strip the opp's blockers, then alpha. No counterspells — interaction is all sorcery/creature-based.

## How to Beat This Deck
- **Removing Atla is NOT a reliable plan** — she's optional and the deck wins without her (Games 111, 114). Killing her repeatedly just taxes a card it can ignore.
- **No counterspells / interaction-light.** A resolved kill button wins: Fiery Emancipation, Ruinous Ultimatum, or a big Bello-style enchantment cast on its own turn has no answer here (Games 94, 99). Race the deck to that turn.
- **Fragile RGW mana base.** Fast aggression that cheats threats before ~T8 punishes color screw — especially a missing red source (Cruelclaw, Game 94). When it stumbles on lands it stalls 8+ turns.
- **Turn its own cards against it.** Wand of Wonder and Crabomination mine its singleton removal/engine pieces and replay them on your side (Games 88, 94). Ragavan's T2-T3 exile-cast steals its best 1-mana removal before it's cast.
- **Dice/value lock that top-decks removal every turn** beats it: Mr. House + Wyll + Barbarian Class makes every removal-on-roll (Myrkul, Contraband Livestock) a high-roll, so Atla never sticks and the engine never fires (Games 88, 99).
- **Wall trample with exact-toughness blocks.** A wide robot/token wall absorbs the tramplers for 0-2 face each (Game 88).
- **Lae'zel's Acrobatics / phase-out beats the 8-mana Wakening Sun's Avatar wipe** (Game 88) — and Avatar must be *cast* (not Atla-flipped) to trigger, and it's rarely castable in time anyway.
- **Steal effects are still devastating** — stealing Atla turns her sac ability against you (your Eggs flip her creatures); Game 3 was lost to stolen Atla + Flayer.

**Result:** Human wins T19 by Claude concession. Sides 19. Human 38, Claude 7. Dominance 3. **First win vs Dicebots (now 1-2 in the matchup).**

**Win path — no Atla activation needed, AGAIN:**
- Opp (Dicebots) was mana-screwed (stuck on 2 lands ~7 turns). Punished it by just developing the ramp/dino plan unmolested.
- **Growing Rites of Itlimoc is the engine vs a slow opp.** Atla's Eggs + Bonehoard's Dino tokens get you to 4 creatures fast → flips to Itlimoc, Cradle of the Multitude (taps {G} per creature) for explosive mana. Bankrolled everything after.
- **Bonehoard Dracosaur was MVP** — 5/5 flying first strike clocked Claude's flyerless board every turn AND its upkeep exile made Dino tokens + Treasures (more bodies for Itlimoc, more ramp).
- **The targeted removal pile dismantled Dicebots piece by piece:** Contest of Claws killed their Ojer (4-toughness god), Ram Through (off trampling Brontodon) killed Hedron Detonator + 4 to face, Savage Stomp killed Wyll, and Wrathful Raptors' enrage mopped up Ragavan + Reckless Fireweaver when they chump-blocked into my Dinos. Strip their blockers, then alpha.
- **Rampaging Brontodon was the finisher** — attacks as +1/+1 per land, so with the Itlimoc-fueled ~10-land board it swung as a 17/17 trampler that no single chump could survive. Conceded facing it + Bonehoard.

**Key lessons:**
- **Atla stayed in the command zone the whole game (never activated).** Eggs still mattered — they padded the creature count to flip Growing Rites and served as chump fodder. Reinforces [[feedback-atla-optional]]: the raw ramp-into-fat-dinos + targeted-removal shell beats a stumbling opponent on its own.
- **Wrathful Raptors is a fantastic punish vs go-wide chump decks.** Any time they block one of my Dinos with a creature that has power, the Dino takes damage → Wrathful reflects it to kill another of their bodies. It turned their blocks into 2-for-1s.
- **Vs Dicebots specifically: race their mana screw HARD.** Past two losses (Games 88, 99) were close grinds where their dice engine assembled. This win came from them never getting online — when Dicebots stumbles, just deploy threats and don't durdle. Growing Rites + Bonehoard out-ramps and out-clocks a screwed Mardu deck easily.

With my second-best removal (Path to Exile) not drawn until T14, the deck spent 10 turns answer-less against Channeler, Mr. House, and Wyll. **Lesson:** consider mulligan priority for Sol Ring / cheap interaction in matchups where Ragavan can connect T2-T3 (mana-screwed Atla = unprotected library).
- **Cannot cast Atla + Akroma's Will in same turn at 12 mana** unless I have 9+ lands by ~T8. Slow ramp means AW is a T11+ play, and Atla's tax compounds before AW is castable. **Lesson:** Sol Ring / Arcane Signet / fast 2-mana ramp would have shaved 1-2 turns off the Atla recast curve — critical when opp removes Atla on a recurring basis.
- **Growing Rites whiffed top 4** — no creatures in top 4 = no tutor + 4 cards bottomed. **Lesson:** GR is a 3-mana gamble in this deck. Decklists with ~30 creatures have a ~75-80% hit rate on top 4. The miss this game pushed back engine setup by 1 turn and bottomed Greater Good (would've been castable T11+ for huge value).
- **Concede at 8 life with no creature + AW combination in hand was correct.** All 4 conditions met: life ≤25% start, lethal next turn (16+ vs 8), no save in hand, no flip reachable (Atla in CZ + no Egg). Opp revealed Ruinous Ultimatum afterward — would've wiped my 9 lands and Goat.

### Game 95 (Claude): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. Boros Equipment/Kellan (Human)
**Result:** Claude wins T12 (23 sides) via combat damage concession. Claude 20, Human 41 (concede facing 49 lethal). Comeback. Dominance 2.

**Game flow:**
- Opener kept: Plains, Fortified Village, STP, Pyrohemia, Hatcher, Thunderherd, Growing Rites. No R but Thunderherd Migration acts as R fixing.
- T2-T4: Slow ramp. Thunderherd revealing Hatcher fetched Mountain. Growing Rites cast T4 (3 mana exact) found Marauding Raptor in top 4.
- T7 opp: Kellan equipped with Sword of the Animist swung for 6 face + 6 CDMG.
- T8 (my T4): Marauding Raptor + STP on Kellan (gave opp 3 life). Saved a swing.
- T9 opp: Recast Kellan (+2 tax = 5 mana). Sick.
- T10 (my T5): Atla cast for {R}{G}{W} via Raptor discount.
- T11 opp: Leveled Blacksmith's Talent to L2 ({2}{R}), cast Wrecking Ball Arm ({2}), Talent's beginning-of-combat trigger auto-attached WBA → Kellan 7/7 DS unblockable by ≤2 power. **Attack for 14 face + 14 CDMG** (double strike unblocked). Life 20, CDMG 20 — **one point from CDMG lethal.**
- **T12 (THE TURN):** Forest land drop → 4 mana. Cast Palani's Hatcher → ETB 2 Eggs. Marauding Raptor pings Hatcher + both Eggs for 2 each — Eggs die. Atla flips Thrashing Brontodon, then Ghalta Stampede Tyrant. Ghalta's ETB cheats Pantlaza + Forerunner + Temple Altisaur from hand onto BF. Forerunner ETB tutors Ripjaw Raptor to top of library. Pantlaza's discover X=4 exiles top (Ripjaw, MV 4), cast free. Ripjaw enrage draws Garruk's Uprising. Raptor's +2/+0 per Dino damaged stacks 9 times → 20/3 UEOT.
- Attack: Atla 2 + Raptor 20 + Brontodon 3 + Ghalta 12 trample + Forerunner 1 + Pantlaza 4 + Altisaur 3 + Ripjaw 4 = **49 power unblocked** (Kellan tapped from previous attack, no other creatures). Concession at 41 life.

**MVP winner: Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant.** ETB cheating 3 creatures from hand in a single turn turned the lethal-next-turn position into instant lethal.
**MVP loser (theirs): Wrecking Ball Arm.** Talent L2 free-attach + 7/7 unblockable-by-≤2-power set up T13 lethal CDMG. Forced the all-in Atla flip turn.

**Key lessons:**
- **Look-back Atla activation isn't the only engine pivot.** With Hatcher + Marauding Raptor + Atla on BF, Hatcher's 2-Egg ETB triggers cascade Raptor pings → Atla flips → Ghalta hits → free hand-dump from Ghalta's ETB. The single Hatcher cast on a board with Raptor + Atla is a complete board explosion.
- **Forerunner → discover combo is a guaranteed cheat.** Forerunner's tutor-to-top + Pantlaza's discover X = creature toughness on the same turn lets you precisely select an MV ≤ X dino into play free. Mana value 4 catches Ripjaw, Regisaur Alpha, Wrathful Raptors.
- **Wrecking Ball Arm is the Kellan finisher line to respect.** Talent L2 free-attach + WBA = 7/7 DS + unblockable by ≤2 power. STP Kellan EARLY (T7-T8) — even at +2 tax, second STP delays them another turn. Boros has no counters; STP windows are reliable. **Save STP for after Sword of the Animist equips or after WBA cast — first STP wasted if Kellan recasts cheap.** Counterpoint: at 6 CDMG already taken, every additional Kellan swing is on the lethal clock. Burn STP on the FIRST connect to avoid compounding CDMG.
- **Don't burn STP from a tapped land state.** Made the error of announcing STP-response with no untapped mana available. Always recount tap state before declaring instant-speed responses.
- **Atla flip variance can save desperate boards.** Two flips off 2 Eggs hit Brontodon + Ghalta — Ghalta-tier hits enable hand-dump rebuilds even from a 1-CDMG-from-lethal position.

### Game 94 (Claude): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T12 (12 sides). Claude conceded at 31 life facing 57-power lethal alpha. Dominance 1.

**Game flow:**
- Opener kept: 3 lands (Forest, Plains, Wolf Run — no R), Beast Within, Avatar, Altisaur, Brontodon. No R source.
- T3-T9: **Mana screwed.** Drew Hatcher, Velociraptor, Contest of Claws, Savage Order. None castable — all need R or 2G. Only Plains/Wolf Run came in. Six straight turns of pass-with-land-drop.
- T7: Beast Within → Cruelclaw to CZ (+2 tax). Used only because no creatures in play and no way to block menace next turn. Bought 1 turn.
- T8: Opp recast Cruelclaw (5 mana) + Beast Token attacked → 3 dmg.
- T10: Cruelclaw connected (3 dmg + 3 CDMG). Cheat trigger → Crabomination free-cast (discarding Kokusho). **Crabomination ETB exiled Claude's top library card (Harrow), random GY (Beast Within), random HAND (Palani's Hatcher).** Opp cast Hatcher from exile → 2 Egg tokens on opp's side. Total damage T10: 6 face.
- T11: Finally drew Kodama's Reach. Fetched Mountain (BF tapped) + Forest. First red source on T11. Way too late.
- T12: Opp hardcast **Fiery Emancipation** {3}{R}{R}{R}. Tripled all damage. 57-power lethal alpha on the table with no blockers. Concede.

**MVP loser (mine): Beast Within.** Hit Cruelclaw T7 but recast at +2 tax wasn't punishing — 5 mana was reachable on opp's T8. Net effect: 1 turn delay.

**MVP winner (theirs): Crabomination.** Hand-exile mode pulled Hatcher (my engine) from my hand and let opp free-cast it on their side. Single biggest swing in the game — turned my engine into theirs.

**Key lessons:**
- **Don't keep openers with 3 lands but no red source in 3-color decks.** "3 lands + reasonable curve" looks fine on paper, but if all 3 lands are 2 colors, you're effectively mana-screwed on the 3rd color. Hatcher (3RG), Atla (1RGW), Velociraptor (2R), Marauding Raptor (1R) all stall. Mull aggressively for at least 1 R source in opener, or for Sol Ring / Signet that fixes.
- **Beast Within on Cruelclaw is questionable removal target — opp recasts cheap.** +2 tax → 5 mana recast. Better Beast Within target: Lightning Greaves (when it lands), Butcher of Malakir, or Dread Defiler. Cruelclaw isn't the engine — the CHEATS off connect are. Avatar wipe (or Wrathful Raptors anti-wipe) is the cleaner stop. If Beast Within is the only interaction, save it for the cheated bomb (Crab/Hatcher/Dread Defiler), not the commander himself.
- **Cruelclaw + Crabomination is a 2-card cascade nightmare for this deck.** Crabomination's hand-exile picks our engine piece (Hatcher, Atla, Avatar — whatever's in hand) and casts it on opp's side. Atla deck cannot keep critical pieces in hand for long against Cruelclaw — must dump them via Greater Good / Forerunner top-deck setups, or play them T5-T7 before Cruelclaw connects.
- **Fiery Emancipation is uncastable through this deck's removal.** Beast Within (gone), Brontodon sac, Avatar wipe — none of these are instant-speed answers to a 6-mana enchantment cast on opp's turn. The window to interact is the cast itself. Without counterspells, FE will resolve.
- **Avatar wipe is too slow as the ONLY answer.** 8 mana 5WWW requires 3 white sources + 5 generic. This deck doesn't ramp into 8 mana reliably by T10-T12. Need to win with the Atla engine BEFORE opp finds their kill turn — Avatar is a backup, not the plan.
- **Mulligan rule reinforced: count R sources separately in 3-color hands.** 1+ R in opener or mulligan. Don't trust topdecks to fix color.

### Game 88 (Claude): Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani vs. Dicebots/Mr. House (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T22 (sides 22) via Brass Dragon lethal + Mr. House dice engine attrition. Human life 9, Claude life 5 (concede before T22 lethal). Comeback. Dominance 2.

- **T15 Wakening Sun's Avatar play:** Cast for board wipe. **Human held priority and cast Lae'zel's Acrobatics** in response — phased out their non-token creatures (Mr. House, Fireweaver, Squelcher, Channeler) AND life-locked themselves until their next turn. Avatar wipe still hit tokens (robots + knights + treasure animations). Effectively traded my 8-mana wipe for my own permanents being phased + couldn't attack opp's locked life.

Plus Arcane Signet + Rampant Growth from Altar mana. From near-empty board to 9-creature board in one turn.
- **T21 alpha attack:** Heirloom-pumped Etali (trample + draw), animated Restless Ridgeline (3/4 Dino), Brontodon attack-trigger to 18/18 (11 lands), Restless +2 to Brontodon → 20/18 trample. 9 attackers, 64 total power, 44 trample.

Opp's optimal block: 6 robots on Brontodon (perfect 18 toughness vs 20 power = 2 trample only), 4 on Ghalta (12=12 perfect, 0 trample), 2 on Etali (6=6, 0 trample), 2 on Token (6=6, 0 trample). Hex pinged me on every noncreature spell + scaled Mr. House's robot economy.
- **MVP (mine): Atla Palani + Greater Good + Ghalta ETB chain T19.** Single turn rebuild from broken board to 9-attacker army. Nearly worked.
- **Key lessons:**
  - **Wakening Sun's Avatar is a 1-shot board wipe with no follow-up if removed/sacrificed.** Avoid casting before the killing blow turn — once in graveyard, no reanimation in this deck.
  - **Lae'zel's Acrobatics is the answer to my 8-mana wipe** if Human-side has it. 4-mana phase-out beats 8-mana wipe + protects life. Counter by holding Avatar for a turn opp can't TP/Acrobatics into.
  - **Wand of Wonder is the recurring soft-counter engine.** Each activation finds my own removal/utility and casts it AT me. Ram Through killed Atla. STP killed Zacama. Stroke of Midnight killed Crasher. TP saved opp's engine. Atla deck doesn't have many flyers/reach to threaten Wand directly — only answer is to RACE Wand activations before opp finds 4-5 instants/sorceries from my deck.

  - **The flyer answers are there but were already burned.** Deck has Zetalpa (flying), Bonehoard Dracosaur (flying first strike), Whiptail Moloch / 6/5 cascading reach, plus instant removal (STP, Path, Ram Through, Generous Gift, Stroke of Midnight, Contest of Claws). Greater Good dig is the natural answer (T17 dug 7, found a flyer would've been ideal).
  - **Ghalta + Greater Good still the deck's nuclear option.** When Ghalta hits BF (Atla flip OR cast from hand), her ETB cheats the rest of hand into play. Plus Greater Good's discard 3 can be planned (discard the cards you'd cheat in via Ghalta anyway = extra draws for free).
