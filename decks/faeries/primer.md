# Faeries / Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor — Deck Primer
Commander: Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor | Colors: Blue/Black (Dimir)

## Strategy Overview
UB Faerie tribal tempo/control deck. Leverages flash creatures, counterspells, and evasive flyers to control the board while chipping in damage. Tegwyll provides a lord effect (+1/+1 to other Faeries) and card advantage whenever a Faerie dies (draw a card, lose 1 life). The deck plays heavily on the opponent's turn with flash threats and countermagic, then attacks with evasive flyers.

## Commander
**Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor** — {1}{U}{B} — 2/3 Legendary Faerie Noble
- Flying, deathtouch
- Other Faeries get +1/+1
- Whenever another Faerie you control dies, draw a card and lose 1 life
- Key: deathtouch makes him an excellent blocker/attacker. The death draw trigger means opponents can't profitably wipe your board — mass removal refills your hand.

## Key Synergy Engines

### Flash Package (play on opponent's turn)
The deck wants to play reactively — cast flash creatures on opponent's end step if no counter was needed:
- **Alela, Cunning Conqueror** ({2}{U}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn creates a 1/1 Faerie Rogue with flying. Combat damage goads a creature.
- **Nymris, Oona's Trickster** ({3}{U}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn draws/filters (top 2, keep 1).
- **Blightwing Bandit** ({3}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn exiles top of opponent's library, playable.
- **Voracious Tome-Skimmer** ({U/B}{U/B}{U/B}) — Pay 1 life to draw whenever casting on opponent's turn.
- **Unwelcome Sprite** ({1}{U}) — Surveil 2 whenever casting on opponent's turn.
- **High Fae Trickster** ({3}{U}) — Gives ALL your spells flash.

### Lords & Anthems
- **Tegwyll** — +1/+1 to other Faeries
- **Scion of Oona** ({2}{U}) — Flash. Other Faeries get +1/+1 and shroud. Critical protection piece.
- **Glen Elendra Liege** ({1}{U/B}{U/B}{U/B}) — Other blue creatures +1/+1, other black creatures +1/+1 (UB creatures get +2/+2).
- **Door of Destinies** ({4}) — +1/+1 per charge counter; grows as you cast Faeries.

### Token Generators
- **Bitterblossom** ({1}{B}) — Upkeep: lose 1 life, create 1/1 flying Faerie Rogue. Steady stream of bodies for Tegwyll triggers and board presence.
- **Bitterbloom Bearer** ({B}{B}) — Flash, same effect as Bitterblossom on a 1/1 body.
- **Alela** — Free tokens for casting on opponent's turn.
- **Into the Fae Court** ({3}{U}{U}) — Draw 3 + create a 1/1 Faerie token.
- **Shadow Puppeteers** ({6}{U}) — ETB creates two 1/1 Faerie tokens. Flying attackers become 4/4 red Dragons.

### Draw/Card Advantage
- **Tegwyll's death trigger** — Faeries dying draws cards (at cost of 1 life each).
- **Nymris** — Filters top 2 on opponent's turn spell.
- **Deepfathom Skulker** ({5}{U}) — Draw on each creature dealing combat damage. {3}{C}: unblockable.
- **Into the Fae Court** — Draw 3.
- **Talion's Messenger** ({2}{U}) — Attack with Faeries: draw/discard, +1/+1 counter.
- **Ringsight** ({1}{U}{B}) — Tutor for any UB card (Ring tempts).

## Key Lines of Play

### Ideal Curve
T1: Land (hold up nothing or Snaremaster Sprite)
T2: Mana rock (Sol Ring T1 → rock T2), or Bitterblossom, or hold up counter
T3: Tegwyll. Now all Faeries are buffed and deaths draw cards.
T4+: Flash creatures on opponent's end step, hold up counters. Attack with evasive flyers.

### Board Wipe Recovery
Tegwyll's death trigger means a wipe that kills 3-4 Faeries draws 3-4 cards (at 1 life each). The deck recovers fast from wipes. Tegwyll's Scouring is a one-sided wipe if you flash it with 3 flyers (destroys all creatures, but you get 3 fresh tokens).

### Pressure Plan
Stack lords (Tegwyll + Scion + Glen Elendra Liege) to make 1/1 tokens into 4/4 or 5/5 flyers. Bitterblossom creates a free attacker every turn. Faerie Bladecrafter grows with each combat step.

## How to Beat Fblthp (Opponent)
- **Counter combo pieces:** Enter the Infinite, Leveler, Mirror of Fate, Thassa's Oracle, Lab Maniac, Jace.
- **Counter mana doublers:** Extraplanar Lens, Gauntlet of Power. Without these, 12-mana spells are very hard to cast.
- **Counter enablers:** Teferi Mage of Zhalfir and Tidal Barracuda shut off instant-speed interaction before combo. Counter these on sight.
- **Clock them:** Fblthp has few blockers. Evasive flyers can pressure life total before combo is assembled.
- **Bojuka Bog:** Exile their graveyard to prevent graveyard-based recursion.
- **Hullbreaker Horror:** 7/8 uncounterable — save hard removal (Go for the Throat, Murder, Sower of Temptation steal) rather than trying to counter.
- Dense counter suite (12+ counters) is the primary advantage. Force them to bait counters before deploying combo.

## Game History

### Game 25 (piloted): Faeries/Tegwyll (Claude) vs Fblthp (Human)
**Result: Fblthp wins T13 via Leveler + Thassa's Oracle. Final life: Human 31, Claude 41.**

**Key plays:**
- T1: Opponent free-deployed Leyline of Anticipation (all spells have flash).
- T6: Tegwyll deployed on curve. Began applying pressure with evasive flyers.
- T7: Opponent flashed in Lab Maniac at end of Claude's turn (tapped out from Tegwyll).
- T8: Killed Lab Maniac with Breathe Your Last. Exiled opponent GY with Bojuka Bog (Lab Maniac exiled permanently).
- T10: Deployed Unwelcome Sprite + countered Rain of Revelation (draw 3) with Lofty Denial. Exiled opponent GY again with Bojuka Bog.
- T12: Cast Glen Elendra Liege for buffed board. Opponent bounced Liege with Disperse, then tried to flash in Yue, the Moon Spirit — countered with Arcane Denial. Dealt 5 flying damage.
- T13: Claude tapped out, no free counters. Opponent cast Leveler ({5}) exiling library, then Thassa's Oracle ({U}{U}) for the win.

**Key lessons:**
- **Counter budget is everything vs combo.** Used Lofty Denial on Rain of Revelation (card draw) and Arcane Denial on Yue. Both were defensible calls, but left zero interaction for the actual combo turn. Consider saving at least one counter at all times.
- **Arcane Denial on Yue gave opponent 2 extra draws** — those cards may have included combo pieces. Against Fblthp, card denial > board protection.
- **Leyline of Anticipation changes everything.** Opponent can deploy combo at instant speed on your end step. Must always hold up mana/interaction when possible.
- **Need more card draw.** Only drew naturally + 1 Arcane Denial trigger over 6 turns. The counter suite depletes fast without refueling.
- **Free counters (Fierce Guardianship, Flare of Denial) are critical.** Neither was drawn. These would have answered Leveler even while tapped out.

### Game 27 (opponent): Faeries/Tegwyll (Human) vs The Claw!/Cruelclaw (Claude)
**Result: Faeries wins T20 via lethal flying combat. Faeries 40, Cruelclaw 6. Dominance 3.**

**Key plays:**
- T7: Snaremaster Sprite tapped + stunned Cruelclaw, delaying first attack by a full turn cycle.
- T8: Claude cast Damnation to clear board. Tegwyll death triggers drew 2 cards. Opponent rebuilt faster with flash package.
- T9: Talion choosing 3 directly taxed every Cruelclaw cast (MV/P/T all 3).
- T12: Claude played Court of Ire for monarch — opponent stole it immediately with flyers. Fed opponent 4-5 extra draws.
- T14: Flare of Denial countered Phyrexian Obliterator (saccing Glamermite). Then Glamermite tap + double block killed Cruelclaw and Tegwyll simultaneously.
- T17: Deepfathom Skulker created overwhelming card draw engine — 3 draws per combat.
- T20: Snap cleanly answered Olivia + reanimated Obliterator. Lethal on board.

**Key lessons:**
- Flash creatures (Snaremaster, Glamermite) are the key to locking out Cruelclaw — tap it down before it can attack.
- Talion choosing 3 is the perfect call vs Cruelclaw.
- Deepfathom Skulker + flying board = massive card advantage engine.
- Opponent's Court of Ire/monarch was a gift — flyers steal monarchy trivially.
- Flare of Denial counters ANY spell (not just noncreature). Keep this in mind for future games.

## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Fblthp (0-1):** Fblthp combo is fast and resilient. Lab Maniac, Thassa's Oracle, and Jace are three independent win conditions — removing one isn't enough. Leyline of Anticipation enables instant-speed combo deployment. Priority targets to counter: Leveler/Mirror of Fate (library exile) > mana doublers > card draw. Faeries' dense counter suite is the primary defense but depletes quickly without draw engines online.
- **vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (1-0):** Cruelclaw is helpless without evasion enablers. Flash creatures tap it down repeatedly. Talion choosing 3 taxes every Cruelclaw cast. Dense counter suite handles any hardcast threats. Deepfathom Skulker is the MVP draw engine. Monarch effects from opponent are free card advantage — flyers steal it trivially.
