# Faeries / Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor — Deck Primer
> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor | Colors: Blue/Black (Dimir)

## Strategy Overview
UB Faerie tribal tempo/control deck. Leverages flash creatures, counterspells, and evasive flyers to control the board while chipping in damage. Tegwyll provides a lord effect (+1/+1 to other Faeries) and card advantage whenever a Faerie dies (draw a card, lose 1 life). The deck plays heavily on the opponent's turn with flash threats and countermagic, then attacks with evasive flyers.

## Commander
**Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor** — {1}{U}{B} — 2/3 Legendary Faerie Noble
- Flying, deathtouch
- Other Faeries get +1/+1
- Whenever another Faerie you control dies, draw a card and lose 1 life
- Key: deathtouch makes him an excellent blocker/attacker. The death draw trigger means opponents can't profitably wipe your board — mass removal refills your hand.

## Key Synergy Engines

### Flash Package (play on opponent's turn)
The deck wants to play reactively — cast flash creatures on opponent's end step if no counter was needed:
- **Alela, Cunning Conqueror** ({2}{U}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn creates a 1/1 Faerie Rogue with flying. Combat damage goads a creature.
- **Nymris, Oona's Trickster** ({3}{U}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn draws/filters (top 2, keep 1).
- **Blightwing Bandit** ({3}{B}) — First spell on opponent's turn exiles top of opponent's library, playable.
- **Voracious Tome-Skimmer** ({U/B}{U/B}{U/B}) — Pay 1 life to draw whenever casting on opponent's turn.
- **Unwelcome Sprite** ({1}{U}) — Surveil 2 whenever casting on opponent's turn.
- **High Fae Trickster** ({3}{U}) — Gives ALL your spells flash.

### Lords & Anthems
- **Tegwyll** — +1/+1 to other Faeries
- **Scion of Oona** ({2}{U}) — Flash. Other Faeries get +1/+1 and shroud. Critical protection piece.
- **Glen Elendra Liege** ({1}{U/B}{U/B}{U/B}) — Other blue creatures +1/+1, other black creatures +1/+1 (UB creatures get +2/+2).
- **Door of Destinies** ({4}) — +1/+1 per charge counter; grows as you cast Faeries.

### Token Generators
- **Bitterblossom** ({1}{B}) — Upkeep: lose 1 life, create 1/1 flying Faerie Rogue. Steady stream of bodies for Tegwyll triggers and board presence.
- **Bitterbloom Bearer** ({B}{B}) — Flash, same effect as Bitterblossom on a 1/1 body.
- **Alela** — Free tokens for casting on opponent's turn.
- **Into the Fae Court** ({3}{U}{U}) — Draw 3 + create a 1/1 Faerie token.
- **Shadow Puppeteers** ({6}{U}) — ETB creates two 1/1 Faerie tokens. Flying attackers become 4/4 red Dragons.

### Draw/Card Advantage
- **Tegwyll's death trigger** — Faeries dying draws cards (at cost of 1 life each).
- **Nymris** — Filters top 2 on opponent's turn spell.
- **Deepfathom Skulker** ({5}{U}) — Draw on each creature dealing combat damage. {3}{C}: unblockable.
- **Into the Fae Court** — Draw 3.
- **Talion's Messenger** ({2}{U}) — Attack with Faeries: draw/discard, +1/+1 counter.
- **Ringsight** ({1}{U}{B}) — Tutor for any UB card (Ring tempts).

### Countermagic (dense package)
- **Counterspell** ({U}{U}) — hard counter
- **Mana Drain** ({U}{U}) — hard counter + colorless mana next turn
- **Arcane Denial** ({1}{U}) — hard counter (opp draws 2, you draw 1)
- **Fierce Guardianship** ({2}{U}) — FREE with commander; counters noncreature spells
- **Flare of Denial** ({2}{U}) — FREE by saccing a nontoken blue creature; counters noncreature spells
- **Lofty Denial** ({1}{U}) — Counter unless pay 4 (with flyer)
- **Spell Stutter** ({1}{U}) — Counter unless pay 2+1 per Faerie
- **Spellstutter Sprite** ({1}{U}) — Flash Faerie; ETB counter spell with MV ≤ Faerie count
- **An Offer You Can't Refuse** ({U}) — Counter noncreature, opp gets 2 Treasures
- **Annul** ({U}) — Counter artifact or enchantment
- **Glen Elendra Guardian** ({2}{U}) — Flash 3/4 (enters with -1/-1 counter). Remove counter: counter noncreature spell (opp draws 1).
- **Glen Elendra's Answer** ({2}{U}{U}) — Uncounterable. Counter ALL opponent spells and abilities on stack. Create Faerie tokens equal to countered.
- **Divert Disaster** ({1}{U}) — Counter unless pay 2; if they pay, you get a Lander token.

### Soft Lock / Tempo
- **Procrastinate** ({X}{U}) — Tap target creature. Put 2X stun counters on it (each stun replaces one untap). At X=2 (3 mana), target locked tapped for 4 turns. **Use on commander-dependent decks (Wayta, etc.) to disable activated abilities.** Excellent vs ability-based combo where counters are dead.

### Removal

- **No Mercy** ({2}{B}{B}) — Enchantment: any creature that damages you is destroyed.
- **Aetherize** ({3}{U}) — Return all attacking creatures to hand.
- **Recoil** ({1}{U}{B}) — Bounce permanent; if no hand, discard.

## Key Lines of Play

### Ideal Curve
T1: Land (hold up nothing or Snaremaster Sprite)
T2: Mana rock (Sol Ring T1 → rock T2), or Bitterblossom, or hold up counter
T3: Tegwyll. Now all Faeries are buffed and deaths draw cards.
T4+: Flash creatures on opponent's end step, hold up counters. Attack with evasive flyers.

### Board Wipe Recovery
Tegwyll's death trigger means a wipe that kills 3-4 Faeries draws 3-4 cards (at 1 life each). The deck recovers fast from wipes. Tegwyll's Scouring is a one-sided wipe if you flash it with 3 flyers (destroys all creatures, but you get 3 fresh tokens).

### Pressure Plan
Stack lords (Tegwyll + Scion + Glen Elendra Liege) to make 1/1 tokens into 4/4 or 5/5 flyers. Bitterblossom creates a free attacker every turn. Faerie Bladecrafter grows with each combat step.

## How to Beat Fblthp (Opponent)
- **Counter combo pieces:** Enter the Infinite, Leveler, Mirror of Fate, Thassa's Oracle, Lab Maniac, Jace.
- **Counter mana doublers:** Extraplanar Lens, Gauntlet of Power. Without these, 12-mana spells are very hard to cast.
- **Counter enablers:** Teferi Mage of Zhalfir and Tidal Barracuda shut off instant-speed interaction before combo. Counter these on sight.
- **Clock them:** Fblthp has few blockers. Evasive flyers can pressure life total before combo is assembled.
- **Bojuka Bog:** Exile their graveyard to prevent graveyard-based recursion.
- **Hullbreaker Horror:** 7/8 uncounterable — save hard removal (Go for the Throat, Murder, Sower of Temptation steal) rather than trying to counter.
- Dense counter suite (12+ counters) is the primary advantage. Force them to bait counters before deploying combo.

## Game History

### Game 25 (piloted): Faeries/Tegwyll (Claude) vs Fblthp (Human)
**Result: Fblthp wins T13 via Leveler + Thassa's Oracle. Final life: Human 31, Claude 41.**

**Key plays:**
- T1: Opponent free-deployed Leyline of Anticipation (all spells have flash).
- T6: Tegwyll deployed on curve. Began applying pressure with evasive flyers.
- T7: Opponent flashed in Lab Maniac at end of Claude's turn (tapped out from Tegwyll).
- T8: Killed Lab Maniac with Breathe Your Last. Exiled opponent GY with Bojuka Bog (Lab Maniac exiled permanently).
- T10: Deployed Unwelcome Sprite + countered Rain of Revelation (draw 3) with Lofty Denial. Exiled opponent GY again with Bojuka Bog.
- T12: Cast Glen Elendra Liege for buffed board. Opponent bounced Liege with Disperse, then tried to flash in Yue, the Moon Spirit — countered with Arcane Denial. Dealt 5 flying damage.
- T13: Claude tapped out, no free counters. Opponent cast Leveler ({5}) exiling library, then Thassa's Oracle ({U}{U}) for the win.

**Key lessons:**
- **Counter budget is everything vs combo.** Used Lofty Denial on Rain of Revelation (card draw) and Arcane Denial on Yue. Both were defensible calls, but left zero interaction for the actual combo turn. Consider saving at least one counter at all times.
- **Arcane Denial on Yue gave opponent 2 extra draws** — those cards may have included combo pieces. Against Fblthp, card denial > board protection.
- **Leyline of Anticipation changes everything.** Opponent can deploy combo at instant speed on your end step. Must always hold up mana/interaction when possible.
- **Need more card draw.** Only drew naturally + 1 Arcane Denial trigger over 6 turns. The counter suite depletes fast without refueling.
- **Free counters (Fierce Guardianship, Flare of Denial) are critical.** Neither was drawn. These would have answered Leveler even while tapped out.

**Result: Faeries lose T9 (sides 18) by concession to lethal Gishath+Zacama+Etali swing. Final life: Human 40, Claude 23. Dominance 2.**

**Key plays:**

- T12: Tegwyll sac'd to Flare of Denial countering Zetalpa, Primal Dawn (4/8 flying double strike vigilance trample indestructible — game-ending threat). Tax → +4.

- T15: Tegwyll's Scouring at 6 mana wiped Atla. Got 3 Faerie tokens.
- **T16 (game-losing turn):** Opp activated Atla in response to Scouring (Egg created), then PASSED PRIORITY — did not sac in response. Wipe destroyed Atla + Egg simultaneously. Atla's "Egg dies" trigger fired via look-back rule (Atla was on BF when Egg died), went on stack POST-Scouring resolution. Resolved → flipped Etali, Primal Storm. Etali entered AFTER Scouring resolved → survived the wipe.
- T16 opp continued: Zacama (9/9 reach trample vigilance, ETB untaps lands) + Regisaur Alpha (3/3 trample token + haste to other Dinos). Combat: 18 face. Etali trigger exiled Flitterwing Nuisance (mine) + Cultivate, cast both free. Sac'd Flitterwing to Ashnod's Altar → Forerunner of the Empire → tutored Gishath top of library.

**Key lessons:**
- **Wipe-into-Atla is a trap if opp can activate Atla at instant speed.** The look-back rule lets the post-wipe Egg-death trigger flip a creature that enters AFTER the wipe and survives. Counter Atla BEFORE wiping, or wipe only when opp is tapped out / Atla can't activate.
- **Counter sequencing:** Burned all 4 counters on midgame (Generous Gift, Ram Through, Zetalpa, Raptor). Left nothing for Zacama (T16) and Gishath (T18). The Ram Through counter was probably wrong — should have let Tegwyll die that turn (he died next turn anyway to Contest of Claws) and saved Offer for Atla or another noncreature wincon.

- **No removal drawn for Etali post-wipe.** Faeries lacks instant-speed creature removal beyond Sower (steal) and bounce (Snap, Cyclonic Rift). Once Etali resolved through and Snap was in GY, no answer.
- **Ringsight was a brick most of the game** — Tegwyll kept dying / staying in CZ. Tutor never went online when needed.

- T10: Opp Sliver recast + Shaman en-Kor cast. Claude could not counter — 0 mana up.
- **T11+ (lethal chain):** End of Claude T17 + opp upkeep T18: Wayta-fight Sliver vs Nemesis ({G}, T). en-Kor {1}{W} redirects Sliver-incoming damage to itself, then en-Kor {0} redistributes 1 to Sliver / 1 to Wayta / 1 stays on en-Kor. ## Matchup Notes
- **vs. Meteor Apes/Roxanne (1-0):** Roxanne's Meteorite ETB (2 damage) threatens Faerie tokens but with Tegwyll lord they survive at 2/2. **Eaten by Piranhas is the #1 answer** — strips Roxanne's abilities entirely. Flash in threats on end steps to dodge sorcery-speed interaction. Snaremaster Sprite locks down Roxanne for 2 turns. Gruul has very limited flying/reach blockers — air force is nearly unblockable. Watch for Starstorm/Magmaquake/Rolling Earthquake as board wipes. Silverback Elder destroys enchantments on creature cast trigger — remove it or bounce it before Elder can trigger.
- **vs. Fblthp (0-1):** Fblthp combo is fast and resilient. Lab Maniac, Thassa's Oracle, and Jace are three independent win conditions — removing one isn't enough. Leyline of Anticipation enables instant-speed combo deployment. Priority targets to counter: Leveler/Mirror of Fate (library exile) > mana doublers > card draw. Faeries' dense counter suite is the primary defense but depletes quickly without draw engines online.
- **vs. The Claw!/Cruelclaw (1-0):** Cruelclaw is helpless without evasion enablers. Flash creatures tap it down repeatedly. Talion choosing 3 taxes every Cruelclaw cast. Dense counter suite handles any hardcast threats. Deepfathom Skulker is the MVP draw engine. Monarch effects from opponent are free card advantage — flyers steal it trivially.
- **vs. Mill-Steal/Wrexial (1-0):** Talion the Kindly Lord choosing 2 punishes the entire mill suite (Cacophony, Glimpse, Drown, most counters all MV2). Tegwyll + Liege lord stack turns Talion's Messenger into a one-card win condition (snowball +1/+1 every attack). Discard preferentially non-instant/sorcery cards off Messenger trigger to deny Wrexial steal targets. Bitterblossom is safe to deploy aggressively — even if Drown counters it, the rest of the lord chain wins anyway. Apply pressure early; opp's deck is slow and reactive without combat.
- **vs. Dinosaur Eggs/Atla Palani (0-1):** Atla is the engine — must counter on cast OR remove before she can activate. With Ashnod's Altar, opp doesn't even need Marauding Raptor to enable the Egg flip combo. **DO NOT WIPE while Atla is on board with mana up** — the look-back rule lets opp activate Atla in response, let the wipe destroy both, then the Egg-death trigger flips a creature post-wipe that survives. Etali, Primal Storm is the worst single card to flip into vs Faeries — exiles top of each library on attack and casts free, drains my counter suite via my own deck. Bonehoard Dracosaur (5/5 flying first strike) is high priority if it lands — Faerie tokens lose to first strike, hard to race in air. Save Cyclonic Rift overload as the panic button — Naya can't easily recast 3+ creatures in one turn. Snap is critical bounce — use on Atla (tax + reset) before Bonehoard.
- **vs. Quit Hitting Yourself/Wayta (0-1):** Wayta+en-Kor+reflectors is an **ability-based combo, not spell-based**. Counter package (Drain/Denial/Ice Out/Counterspell etc.) is dead vs Wayta-fight and en-Kor redirects. Faeries needs **removal** (Murder, Cyclonic Rift, Snap, Go for the Throat, Recoil) to disrupt. Priority targets: (1) Pain for All on cast — counter or bounce immediately; it's the cheapest Nemesis amp. (2) Shaman en-Kor — the keystone redirector that funnels damage to one reflector. Without en-Kor, fight chains can't stack. (3) Spiteful Sliver / Screaming Nemesis (post Pain for All) — bounce/kill before chain assembles. (4) Wayta itself — Murder or Cyclonic Rift on cast forces +2 tax recast. (5) Save Cyclonic Rift overload as panic button — bounces all opp nonlands, breaks the assembled lock. **Do not tap out for big threats while opp has cards in hand and partial combo on board.** With limited removal, holding mana for ability-source removal beats holding for counter.
