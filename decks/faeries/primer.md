Commander: Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor | Colors: Blue/Black (Dimir)

## Strategy Overview
UB Faerie tribal tempo/control deck. Leverages flash creatures, counterspells, and evasive flyers to control the board while chipping in damage. Tegwyll provides a lord effect (+1/+1 to other Faeries) and card advantage whenever a Faerie dies (draw a card, lose 1 life). The deck plays heavily on the opponent's turn with flash threats and countermagic, then attacks with evasive flyers.

**Win path (proven):** stack lords — Tegwyll + Glen Elendra Liege — to turn 1/1 tokens and **Talion's Messenger** into 5/6+ evasive flyers; Messenger snowballs into a one-card finisher (G84, G27). The flash package can close games with zero counters drawn (G43, G27).

**Counter discipline (repeated lesson):** the counter suite depletes fast without draw engines online — always keep one counter in reserve vs combo (lost the budget vs Fblthp G25, Atla G85). Counters are DEAD vs ability-based combos (Wayta G86) — pivot to removal there. Tegwyll death-draws make wipes refuel you and chump-walling buys turns, but it only delays — dig for a panic-button wipe vs go-wide/go-tall boards (Doran G107, Atla G85).

## Commander
**Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor** — {1}{U}{B} — 2/3 Legendary Faerie Noble
- Flying, deathtouch
- Other Faeries get +1/+1
- Whenever another Faerie you control dies, draw a card and lose 1 life
- Key: deathtouch makes him an excellent blocker/attacker. The death draw trigger means opponents can't profitably wipe your board — mass removal refills your hand.

## Key Synergy Engines

## Key Lines of Play

### Ideal Curve
T1: Land (hold up nothing or Snaremaster Sprite)
T2: Mana rock (Sol Ring T1 → rock T2), or Bitterblossom, or hold up counter
T3: Tegwyll. Now all Faeries are buffed and deaths draw cards.
T4+: Flash creatures on opponent's end step, hold up counters. Attack with evasive flyers.

### Board Wipe Recovery
Tegwyll's death trigger means a wipe that kills 3-4 Faeries draws 3-4 cards (at 1 life each). The deck recovers fast from wipes. Tegwyll's Scouring is a one-sided wipe if you flash it with 3 flyers (destroys all creatures, but you get 3 fresh tokens).

### Pressure Plan
Stack lords (Tegwyll + Scion + Glen Elendra Liege) to make 1/1 tokens into 4/4 or 5/5 flyers. Bitterblossom creates a free attacker every turn. Faerie Bladecrafter grows with each combat step. **Talion's Messenger is the hidden finisher** — under the lord stack it snowballs (1/3 → 6/8 in 4 turns) and wins on its own.

### Panic Button (go-wide / go-tall boards)
Chump-walling on Tegwyll death-draws only buys time. Hold **Tegwyll's Scouring** (flash with 3 flyers, leaves you 3 tokens) or **Cyclonic Rift OVERLOAD** as the reset vs wide/tall boards that out-grind you (Doran's Betor + double-Doran G107, Atla's dinos G85). Don't burn single-target Rift on tempo if you'll need the overload later.

## How to Beat Faeries (this deck's weaknesses)
*(Drawn from recorded losses — how opponents have beaten this deck.)*
- **Deplete the counters, then resolve the wincon.** The suite is dense but runs dry without draw engines online (Fblthp G25, Atla G85). Bait counters with mid-value spells, then land the real threat on the tapped-out turn.
- **Ability-based combos dodge the whole counter package.** Wayta-fight / en-Kor redirects and activated abilities are not spells (Wayta G86). Faeries has thin instant-speed creature removal, so ability engines assemble unchecked.
- **Go wide or go tall to outrun chump-walling.** Tegwyll death-draws + flashed 1/1s wall ~5 lethal swings but only buy time; Faeries must FIND Tegwyll's Scouring / Cyclonic Rift overload (Doran G107, Atla G85). Pressure the board before it digs.
- **Evasive / unblockable voltron beats the air defense.** Vorrac Battlehorns + Kellan's menace = unblockable; equipment persists to a backup voltron (Boros G105). Commander damage sidesteps Faerie blockers.
- **First strike beats Faerie tokens.** 1/1 and 2/2 fliers lose to first-strikers like Bonehoard Dracosaur (G85) — Faeries can't profitably race them in the air.
- **Tax out Tegwyll.** Repeatedly kill/exile the commander; +2 per recast strains the mana and stalls the lord engine (Atla G85, Boros G105).

### Game 25 (piloted): Faeries/Tegwyll (Claude) vs Fblthp (Human)
**Result: Fblthp wins T13 via Leveler + Thassa's Oracle. Final life: Human 31, Claude 41.**

**Key plays:**
- T1: Opponent free-deployed Leyline of Anticipation (all spells have flash).
- T6: Tegwyll deployed on curve. Began applying pressure with evasive flyers.
- T7: Opponent flashed in Lab Maniac at end of Claude's turn (tapped out from Tegwyll).
- T8: Killed Lab Maniac with Breathe Your Last. Exiled opponent GY with Bojuka Bog (Lab Maniac exiled permanently).
- T10: Deployed Unwelcome Sprite + countered Rain of Revelation (draw 3) with Lofty Denial. Exiled opponent GY again with Bojuka Bog.
- T12: Cast Glen Elendra Liege for buffed board. Opponent bounced Liege with Disperse, then tried to flash in Yue, the Moon Spirit — countered with Arcane Denial. Dealt 5 flying damage.
- T13: Claude tapped out, no free counters. Opponent cast Leveler ({5}) exiling library, then Thassa's Oracle ({U}{U}) for the win.

**Key lessons:**
- **Counter budget is everything vs combo.** Used Lofty Denial on Rain of Revelation (card draw) and Arcane Denial on Yue. Both were defensible calls, but left zero interaction for the actual combo turn. Consider saving at least one counter at all times.
- **Arcane Denial on Yue gave opponent 2 extra draws** — those cards may have included combo pieces. Against Fblthp, card denial > board protection.
- **Leyline of Anticipation changes everything.** Opponent can deploy combo at instant speed on your end step. Must always hold up mana/interaction when possible.
- **Need more card draw.** Only drew naturally + 1 Arcane Denial trigger over 6 turns. The counter suite depletes fast without refueling.
- **Free counters (Fierce Guardianship, Flare of Denial) are critical.** Neither was drawn. These would have answered Leveler even while tapped out.

- **Ringsight was a brick most of the game** — Tegwyll kept dying / staying in CZ. Tutor never went online when needed.

Together his 0/5 walls swing for 10. **Cyclonic Rift the Siege Tower** (not the commander) to revert a lethal alpha to power damage; **save Rift OVERLOAD** as the reset for his go-tall board (bounces both Dorans + Betor). **Betor, Kin to All** (5/7 flyer) is the must-answer engine — Murder/Go for the Throat/Sower on sight, or counter on cast; at 40 board-toughness it drains half your life every end step. **Catapult Captain** (flip of Catapult Fodder) saccs a creature to drain you = ignores blockers = inevitability when you're low — kill it or stay out of range. Watch **Anguished Unmaking** on Tegwyll. Annul handles **Barrensteppe Siege** / Banishing Light. Tegwyll's deathtouch deters one attacker but he goes wide; chump-walling on death-draws buys turns but you MUST find Tegwyll's Scouring (wipes his whole board incl.
