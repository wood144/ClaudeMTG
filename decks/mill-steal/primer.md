> **PATH OF ANCESTRY CHECK:** Before using Path of Ancestry mana to cast a creature, verify the creature shares a creature type with your commander. No shared type = no scry.

Commander: Wrexial, the Risen Deep ({3}{U}{U}{B}) | Colors: Blue/Black (Dimir)

## Strategy & Key Cards
**Win path (validated across wins 96/89/49/66/72/35):** Lock evasion → deploy Wrexial behind protection → weaponize their GY. The actual kill is almost always **Stormtide Leviathan lock + Wrexial/Bloatfly unblockable damage**, NOT raw mill-out. Mill is fuel for Wrexial's trigger and a disruption tool, not usually the wincon.

- **Wrexial, the Risen Deep:** 5/8, islandwalk, swampwalk. On combat damage to player: cast instant/sorcery from defending player's GY for free. Mill fuels this ability. **Deploy EARLY but never naked** (see Weaknesses).
- **Stormtide Leviathan:** THE closer/lock (96/49/66/22). All lands become Islands → Wrexial permanently unblockable; non-flyers can't attack. Game-ending vs any deck without fliers/islandwalk. Race to 8 mana.
- **Flood of Mars / Eluge:** Islandwalk enablers vs non-U decks. Flood → Stormtide is the combo lock (35/66/49). Eluge grows huge under Stormtide. Don't deploy Eluge as a fragile 1/1 (dies to small ETB pings — Game 22).
- **Engine-lock answers (Stasis Field / Witness Protection / Imprisoned in the Moon):** Premium vs commander engines — NO command-zone redirect, no tax increase, permanent lockout. Beats Bello, Ayula, Ghave, Sokka, Roxanne. Often better than killing/countering the commander.
- **Wrexial-stolen StP/Path on the opp's commander:** Tax-snowball loop (+2 per CZ trip) — made Sokka uncastable (89). The "steal their interaction and turn it on them" payoff (also Teferi's Protection G35, King Narfi commander-steal G89).
- **Maddening Cacophony / Glimpse the Unthinkable / Traumatize:** Mill bombs. Glimpse + Twincast T3 = defining early tempo swing vs slow decks (96/22). Traumatize + Wrexial/Breach the Multiverse is the dream (66/72). Narset's Reversal your own kicked Cacophony = two big mills (49).
- **Grasping Tentacles + Twincast:** Artifact-steal blowout — rip their mana rocks into your control (89/66).
- **Hedron Crab / Ruin Crab:** Landfall mill — but anti-synergy vs recursion/equipment/enchantment decks (see Weaknesses).
- **Archmage Emeritus ({2}{U}{U}):** Magecraft — draw on every instant/sorcery cast/copy. Card advantage engine.
- **Captain N'ghathrod / Consuming Aberration / Nemesis of Reason / Mirko Vosk:** Mill payoffs; Aberration scales off opp's GY. Nemesis = finisher mill after Traumatize.
- **Bloatfly Swarm:** Flying clock; doubles counters with Sovereign Okinec Ahau (35). Attack EARLY vs decks with no reach — don't hold it back (37).
- Interaction: Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Cancel, Drown in the Loch ({U}{B}), Go for the Throat, Negate (1-mana via Sapphire Medallion). Foretold counters (Saw It Coming) hold up cheaply.

## Key Weaknesses
- **DEPLOY WREXIAL EARLY — but NEVER naked/tapped-out.** Deploying too late lost games (63, 97); deploying without protection got him bounced ({U} Unsummon, G97/63), sac-edicted (Flare of Malice, G106), or killed in FS by an equipped attacker (G101). Hold up a counter or deploy only when he can immediately attack/block profitably. Sac/edict and first-strike bypass his 8 toughness entirely.
- **Mill is anti-synergy vs recursion / equipment / enchantment decks.** Every mill feeds Mantle of the Ancients (equipment, G8/101), reanimation (Cruelclaw/Olivia, G106), or shrine/Hondens recursion (G54). Evaluate opp's GY payoffs FIRST; don't auto-deploy mill cards — discard them if screwed, KEEP ramp.
- **Wrexial's trigger goes dead** vs decks with few instants/sorceries (Shrines G54, Boros G101), or that delve/cycle their own GY (Roxanne delve G37, Fblthp G97). Mill quality > quantity; mill to disrupt, not to feed a dead trigger.
- **Answer the BOARD, not the commander.** Taxing/countering the commander repeatedly still lost (Doran G112, Cruelclaw G106) because the support cast wins. Hold answers for the actual threats.
- **Mulligan for interaction — a smooth no-counter keep is a losing keep.** Both Fblthp losses (63/97) and Scion (44) featured zero early counters drawn. Fast-mana commanders (T3 Scion) and lock pieces (Teferi/Barracuda) end the game before Wrexial matters.
- **No flying blockers; weak to aggro fliers under mana stumble.** Faerie lord-stack (G84) and other air forces run over the board before mill comes online. Mana flood/screw on a control deck = near auto-loss.
- **Dimir has no enchantment removal.** Aura/enchantment threats (Mantle of the Ancients, Jaws of Defeat, shrines) are unanswerable once resolved — counter on cast or steal a Decimate/Broken Bond-type to kill them.
- **Wrexial's walk needs Islands/Swamps in play** — dead vs non-U/B without Flood/Eluge/land-type changers; menace alone (Nemesis, N'ghathrod) isn't reliable evasion.

- T11: Arcane Denial countered Sokka, Swordmaster (equipment cost reducer + auto-equip engine). Good use of single counter.
- T14: Stasis Field on Gingerbrute (lock 4/turn beater) + Boomerang Haystack (delay defense). Best turn of the game — used Haystack-offline window.
- T15: Opp recast Haystack + Kellan + Haunted Cloak. Cloak haste = Kellan attacks T15 for 4 CDMG.
- T17: Sword moved to Kellan. Kellan 3/3 DS trample = 7 CDMG with Hedron Crab block.

- **Mill is BAD vs Boros Equipment.** Ruin Crab + Hedron Crab + Water Crystal all anti-synergy — each mill puts equipment in opp GY for Mantle of the Ancients. The natural mill from playing the deck ALREADY fuels their wincon. Active mill cards should NOT be deployed.
- **When discarding from a mana-screw hand, cut DEAD CARDS first, not ramp.** In this matchup, Water Crystal (mill+4 fuels Mantle), Hedron Crab (landfall mill anti-synergy), and even Twincast (Boros has minimal inst/sorc to copy) are all dead. Sky Diamond and Jet Medallion are NOT dead — they're the ramp that justifies keeping a 1-land hand. **The keep decision and the discard decision must be coherent.**
- **Stasis Field + Boomerang T14 was the right use of the Haystack-offline window.** But locking Gingerbrute when Kellan is the wincon = wrong target priority. Should have saved Stasis for Kellan (cast T16 in my main phase before Haystack could phase him out — but Haystack was recast T15, so already too late). Better play: bait Haystack with a less important target, OR find a way to remove Haystack permanently.
- **Haystack is the matchup-defining card.** Once it lands, it phases out any creature being targeted. Stasis Field / Boomerang on Kellan get phased around. Need: counter Haystack on cast, OR draw multiple bounces to deplete it, OR pressure opp out of mana for Haystack activation. Bouncing Haystack is 1-turn tempo only — opp recasts {1}{W} cheaply.
- **Wrexial as a wall vs Kellan + equipment doesn't work.** Wrexial 5/8 dies in FS phase to Kellan 8+ FS damage. Wrexial never deals normal damage. Only useful if Wrexial can survive FS (need >8 toughness OR Kellan <8 FS damage).
- **The deck has NO answer to Haunted Cloak + immediate-turn Kellan.** Cloak grants haste, so Kellan attacks T15 (the turn he's cast) before I can react T16. Need counter-on-cast or pre-emptive Stasis on Gingerbrute T14 to absorb the alpha.

### Game 22 (Claude): Mill/Steal (Claude) vs. Meteor Apes/Roxanne (Human)
**Result:** Human wins T18 via combat damage (Heroic Intervention + Savage Twister one-sided wipe into lethal swing). Claude at 11 life.
**Key plays:**
- T7: Eluge flood-countered Stomping Ground to enable Wrexial's islandwalk. Eluge sniped by Meteorite ETB.
- T13: Glimpse the Unthinkable milled Aggravated Assault, Savage Beating — two key combo pieces neutralized.
- T15: Manic Scribe ETB milled Relentless Assault — third extra combat spell removed. Also milled Worldly Tutor and Regrowth (due to incorrect Delirium trigger, kept per user ruling).
- T16: Mirelurk Queen blocked and killed Roxanne, but opponent recast Roxanne immediately in Main 2 and sniped Queen with Meteorite ETB (4 damage marked from combat + 2 from Meteorite = lethal on 5 toughness). Brilliant sequence.
- T16 M2: Arcane Denial countered Anzrag (8/4 with infinite combat potential via Bloodscent + Withstand Death in hand). Best play of the game — saved 3+ turns of survival.
- T18: Heroic Intervention + Savage Twister X=6 wiped Claude's board (N'ghathrod) while opponent's creatures survived indestructible. Lethal swing followed.
**Lessons:**
- **Eluge is the key enabler vs non-U/B decks.** Flood counters make Wrexial unblockable. But deploying Eluge as a 1/1 (only 1 Island) is extremely risky — it needs 2 attack cycles to grow past Meteorite range (3/3). Against Roxanne specifically, do NOT deploy Eluge until you have 3+ Islands in play (survives Meteorite ETB) or can hold up protection. T7 Eluge into T10 Roxanne Meteorite snipe was predictable and preventable.
- **Glimpse the Unthinkable is the MVP mill spell.** Milling 10 hit 3 extra combat pieces — completely dismantled the deck's combo engine.
- **Roxanne's Meteorite engine is oppressive.** Each attack = 2 damage ping + mana ramp. With Roxanne's artifact token doubling, Meteorites produce 2 mana each. Killing Roxanne is critical but she can be recast with all the accumulated mana.
- **Mirelurk Queen + combat damage trick:** Blocking Roxanne to put damage on Queen, then recasting Roxanne to snipe Queen with Meteorite ETB is a known line now. Don't block with Queen if opponent has mana to recast Roxanne.
- **Save counterspells for Anzrag.** Anzrag + forced block effects = infinite combats. Must be countered on the stack.
- **Stormtide Leviathan would have been the ultimate answer** — shuts down all non-flying/non-islandwalk attackers. At 8 mana it was too slow this game. Deck already runs heavy 2-drop ramp — the issue is tempo, not ramp count. Prioritize early blockers and saving removal/counters for key threats while racing to 8 mana.
